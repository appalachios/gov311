The municipal directory lists **7930 Nelson Road, Millington, TN 38053-2044** as the contact address for Millington. Call **(901) 873-5701** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/millington) · [Municipal website](https://www.millingtontn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/millington)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.millingtontn.gov/requesttracker.aspx)
- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.millingtontn.gov/201/Sanitation)
