For an address served by Millington, visit City Hall at **7930 Nelson Road**. The water department lists the identification and ownership or lease records it needs, plus a connection fee and deposit. Review the current amounts and acceptable documents before visiting. [City water service requirements](https://www.millingtontn.gov/209/Water)

For a disconnection due to nonpayment, contact the utility office about the full balance, other charges and its business-day restoration times. The separate after-hours number **901-497-2997** is for a broken water main or line. Billing reconnections go through the utility office. [Millington restoration and after-hours instructions](https://www.millingtontn.gov/209/Water)
