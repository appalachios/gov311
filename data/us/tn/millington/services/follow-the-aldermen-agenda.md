In Millington’s Agenda Center, select **Board of Mayor & Aldermen – Regular Meeting**. Download the packet and identify the ordinance by title and number. Check the date on the notice before attending. The board lists City Hall Chambers for its regular meetings. [Agenda Center](https://www.millingtontn.gov/AgendaCenter) · [Board schedule](https://www.millingtontn.gov/108/Board-of-Mayor-and-Aldermen)

After the meeting, check the motion in the minutes, including whether it advanced one reading, amended the text or completed adoption. If the packet is the only document posted, use the date and item number to request the approved minutes and a copy of the final ordinance from City Hall. [Board records archive](https://www.millingtontn.gov/413/Agenda-Center)
