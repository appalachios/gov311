The municipal directory lists **104 East Main Street, Smithville, TN 37166** as the contact address for Smithville. Call **(615) 597-4745** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/smithville) · [Municipal website](https://www.smithvillecityhall.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/smithville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [DeKalb County records](../../dekalb-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
