The municipal directory lists **12180 St Hwy 111; PO Box 187, Spencer, TN 38585** as the contact address for Spencer. Call **(931) 946-2351** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spencer) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofspencertn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spencer)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Van Buren County records](../../van-buren-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
