The municipal directory lists **109 West Main, P.O. Box 325, Byrdstown, TN 38549** as the contact address for Byrdstown. Call **(931) 864-6215** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/byrdstown) · [Municipal website](https://byrdstownwater.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/byrdstown)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Pickett County records](../../pickett-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
