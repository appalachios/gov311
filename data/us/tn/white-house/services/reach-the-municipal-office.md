The municipal directory lists **105-D College Street, White House, TN 37188** as the contact address for White House. Call **(615) 672-4350** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/white-house) · [Municipal website](https://www.whitehousetn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/white-house)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Robertson, Sumner. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://www.whitehousetn.gov/242/Request-for-Permits)
- **Report an Issue**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report an Issue](https://www.whitehousetn.gov/243/Service-Request)
