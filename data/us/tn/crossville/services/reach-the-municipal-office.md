The municipal directory lists **392 N Main Street, Crossville, TN 38555** as the contact address for Crossville. Call **(931) 484-5113** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/crossville) · [Municipal website](https://crossvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/crossville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cumberland County records](../../cumberland-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk](https://crossvilletn.gov/administration/city-clerk/)
- **Applications & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Applications & Permits](https://crossvilletn.gov/applications-permits/)
- **Open Records**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records](https://crossvilletn.gov/administration/city-clerk/open-records/)
- **Utility Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing](https://crossvilletn.gov/department/utility-billing/)
- **Online Services - Utilities, Court, Property Taxes, Business License**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Online Services - Utilities, Court, Property Taxes, Business License](https://crossvilletn.municipalonlinepayments.com:443/crossvilletn)
