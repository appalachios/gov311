Confirm the parcel is in the Tennessee town. Its permit form separates building, electrical, plumbing and mechanical or gas work. Enter the address, owner and contractor, describe the work and provide the front, side and rear setbacks. For contractor work, the form requests plans, proof of business and contractor licensing, and liability insurance. Call Town Hall at **423-821-1226** if you need help routing the application.
