The municipal directory lists **710 Scenic Highway, PO Box 111, Lookout Mountain, TN 37350** as the contact address for Lookout Mountain. Call **(423) 821-1226** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lookout-mountain) · [Municipal website](https://lookoutmtn.us/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lookout-mountain)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
