The municipal directory lists **1001 Park Street, P.O. Box 8, Westmoreland, TN 37186** as the contact address for Westmoreland. Call **(615) 644-3382** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/westmoreland) · [Municipal website](https://westmorelandtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/westmoreland)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
