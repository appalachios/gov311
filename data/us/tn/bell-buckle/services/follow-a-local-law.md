Open Minutes & Agendas on the Board of Mayor and Alderpersons page. Write down the proposed ordinance number and ask for the full text before the meeting. The board normally meets on the second Tuesday at 6 p.m. in Town Hall. Check the notice for your meeting. [Board and meeting papers](https://www.townofbellbuckle.com/boma)

The adoption procedure printed at the front of the municipal code calls for consideration and adoption on two separate days. After final passage, the town may publish the ordinance or just its caption in a newspaper of general circulation. Publication is required for it to take effect. For the actual restrictions or exceptions, compare the adopted text and any amendments with the published caption.
