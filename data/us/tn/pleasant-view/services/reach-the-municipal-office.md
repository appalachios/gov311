The municipal directory lists **1008 Civic Court, PO Box 127, Pleasant View, TN 37146** as the contact address for Pleasant View. Call **(615) 746-0600** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pleasant-view) · [Municipal website](https://townofpleasantview.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cheatham County records](../../cheatham-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
