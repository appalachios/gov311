The municipal directory lists **600 East Walnut Street, Dickson, TN 37055** as the contact address for Dickson. Call **(615) 441-9508** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dickson) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofdickson.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dickson)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Dickson County records](../../dickson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.cityofdickson.com/city-recorder)
