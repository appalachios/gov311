The municipal directory lists **105 South Main Street, Dyer, TN 38330** as the contact address for Dyer. Call **(731) 692-3767** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dyer) · [Municipal website](https://cityofdyertn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dyer)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
