To follow a proposal in Dyer, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd and 4th Monday at 7:00 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dyer)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-dyer) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/dyer)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Dyer Code § 1-104 requires two readings on different days. The first may occur when the written ordinance is introduced. Unless it sets another date, it takes effect after the final reading, adoption and the mayor’s signature. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/dyer) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/dyer-t1.pdf)
