For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Van Buren County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/van-buren)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(931) 946-2121**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(931) 946-7363**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(931) 946-2451**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(931) 946-2263**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(931) 946-2728**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Van Buren County include Spencer. [Municipal boundary map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)
