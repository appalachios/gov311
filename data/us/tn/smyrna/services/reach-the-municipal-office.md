The municipal directory lists **315 South Lowry Street, Smyrna, TN 37167** as the contact address for Smyrna. Call **(615) 459-2553** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/smyrna) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/smyrna)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rutherford County records](../../rutherford-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Open Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records Request](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/departments/administration/open_records_request.php)
- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/departments/building_safety/permits.php)
- **Town Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [Town Clerk](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/departments/town_clerk/index.php)
- **Sign Up For Utility Service**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Sign Up For Utility Service](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/departments/utilities/index.php)
- **Report An Issue**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report An Issue](https://www.townofsmyrna.org/how_do_i/report_an_issue.php)
