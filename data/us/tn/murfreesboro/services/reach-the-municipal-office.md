The municipal directory lists **111 West Vine Street, Murfreesboro, TN 37130** as the contact address for Murfreesboro. Call **(615) 893-5210** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/murfreesboro) · [Municipal website](https://www.murfreesborotn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/murfreesboro)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rutherford County records](../../rutherford-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
