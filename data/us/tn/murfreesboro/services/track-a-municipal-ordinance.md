To follow a proposal in Murfreesboro, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “Every Thursday; Council workshops may also be held 2nd Wednesday of each month at 11:30 AM.  They are usually held at the Murfreesboro Municipal Airport Business Center. at 6:00 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/murfreesboro) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://www.murfreesborotn.gov/agendacenter)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-murfreesboro) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/murfreesboro)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter article V, § 24(a) requires the council to consider a written ordinance and pass it by majority vote on two different days, at least one week apart. Council approved this charter change in 2016, reducing the required votes from three to two. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/murfreesboro) · [Governing text](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/109/Bill/HB1858.pdf)
