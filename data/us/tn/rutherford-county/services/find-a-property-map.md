Open **View County Map** from Rutherford County GIS and locate the property by address. Under **Map Layers**, open **County Land/Property Information** and turn on **Parcels**. You can also display city limits, zoning and flood hazards. For the property assessment, enter the owner, address or map and parcel number in the assessor’s search. [Open the County Map](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/2277bc024750486c9d27d56a94c09892/) · [County GIS](https://rutherfordcountytn.gov/gis) · [Property assessment search](https://secured.rutherfordcountytn.gov/OFS/WP/Home)

- **Tax bills.** The County Trustee’s tax portal handles county taxes and city taxes for Smyrna and Eagleville. The portal links to separate city tax offices for Murfreesboro and La Vergne. [Search and pay taxes](https://trustee.pay.rutherfordcountytn.gov/) · [Rutherford County Trustee](https://rctrustee.com/)
- **County commissioner.** Turn on **County Commission Districts** in the County Map and find the district covering the property. Match the district number to the county’s directory for the commissioner’s name and contact information. [County Map](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/2277bc024750486c9d27d56a94c09892/) · [Commissioners by district](https://rutherfordcountytn.gov/list_of_commissioner)
- **School attendance.** Search the home address on Rutherford County Schools’ **Bus Routes and School Zones** page. Choose the school-year and grade-level layer that applies. The map also identifies school board zones. For a child served by Murfreesboro City Schools, use **Map My Home** on the city district’s registration page. [Rutherford County Schools zone map](https://www.rcschools.net/o/od/page/bus-routes-and-school-zones) · [Murfreesboro City Schools registration and map](https://www.cityschools.net/student-info/registration)
