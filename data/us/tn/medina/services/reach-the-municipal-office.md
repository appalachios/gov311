The municipal directory lists **201 Hwy 45E North, PO Box 420, Medina, TN 38355** as the contact address for Medina. Call **(731) 783-3913** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/medina) · [Municipal website](https://cityofmedinatn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/medina)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Codes & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Codes & Permits](https://cityofmedinatn.org/departments/building-codes-permits/)
