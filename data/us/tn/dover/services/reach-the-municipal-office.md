The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 447, Dover, TN 37058** as the contact address for Dover. Call **(931) 232-5907** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dover) · [Municipal website](https://www.dovertn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dover)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Stewart County records](../../stewart-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
