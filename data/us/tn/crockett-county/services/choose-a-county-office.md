For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Crockett County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/crockett)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(731) 696-5452**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(731) 696-5455**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(731) 696-5456**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(731) 696-5454**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(731) 696-5465**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Crockett County include Alamo, Bells, Friendship, Gadsden, Maury City. [Municipal boundary map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)
