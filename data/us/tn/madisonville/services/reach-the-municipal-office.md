The municipal directory lists **400 College St. North, Madisonville, TN 37354** as the contact address for Madisonville. Call **(423) 442-9416** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/madisonville) · [Municipal website](https://www.madisonvilletn.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/madisonville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Monroe County records](../../monroe-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
