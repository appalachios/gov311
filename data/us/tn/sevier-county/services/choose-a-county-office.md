For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Sevier County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/sevier)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(865) 453-5502**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(865) 453-2758**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(865) 453-3242**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(865) 453-2767**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(865) 453-6985**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Sevier County include Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Pittman Center, Sevierville. [Municipal boundary map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)
