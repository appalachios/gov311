The municipal directory lists **15 E. Main Street; PO Box 449, Brownsville, TN 38012** as the contact address for Brownsville. Call **(731) 772-1212** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brownsville) · [Municipal website](https://www.brownsvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Gas, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brownsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Haywood County records](../../haywood-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Clerk’s Office**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk’s Office](https://www.brownsvilletn.gov/city-clerk-s-office/)
- **Building Permit**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Permit](https://www.brownsvilletn.gov/building-permit/)
- **PUBLIC RECORDS POLICY**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [PUBLIC RECORDS POLICY](https://www.brownsvilletn.gov/public-records-policy/)
