The municipal directory lists **301 East Cumberland, P.O. Box 338, Cowan, TN 37318** as the contact address for Cowan. Call **(931) 967-7318** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cowan) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofcowan.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cowan)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.cityofcowan.com/building-permits.html)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Franklin County records](../../franklin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
