**Moving in?** Call Copper Basin Utility District at **423-496-5744**. Give the service address, ask which connections are available there and request the district's service application. The district office is at **2597 Highway 68, Turtletown**. [New service requests](https://copperbasinud.com/new-service-request) · [District office and hours](https://copperbasinud.com/location)

**Already have a bill?** Open the district's payment page and select **Pay Your Bill Now**. For online registration, use the account number and name exactly as printed on the bill. The page also lists payment fees, phone and mail options and participating bank branches. [District payment instructions](https://copperbasinud.com/bill-payment/)

The county directory describes the district's water service. Ducktown's ordinance also names it as the provider for **sewer**. Check the address with the district before assuming a property can connect. [Ducktown Municipal Code, § 18-101](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/combined/Ducktown-code.pdf) · [County utilities directory](https://polkcountytn.gov/services/)
