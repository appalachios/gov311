1. Check the state Department of Revenue's registration page for your type of business and expected gross receipts. It explains when a standard or minimal activity license applies and how to register through the Tennessee Taxpayer Access Point (TNTAP) if you owe state-administered business tax. [Tennessee registration and licensing](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/business-tax/registration-and-licensing.html)
2. If the location is inside Ducktown, contact **City Hall at 423-496-3546**. Give the business address, describe the activity and ask for the city's license application. Ducktown Municipal Code §§ 5-301 and 5-302 adopt the municipal tax and assign licensing to the city manager. [Ducktown business tax ordinance](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/combined/Ducktown-code.pdf) · [City Hall](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/)
3. Contact the **Polk County Clerk** for the county license. Tennessee Revenue tells businesses to arrange payment of applicable local license fees and obtain their licenses from the local offices after state registration. [County Clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/) · [State guidance on local licensing](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360057607711-BUS-14-County-Clerks-and-City-Officials-Issue-Business-Licenses)
