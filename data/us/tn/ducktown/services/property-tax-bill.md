For a **City of Ducktown** bill, call **423-496-3546** and ask for the city treasurer. Have the property address or parcel number and the tax year handy. Ask for the balance, payment instructions and where to send a payment for that year. The charter names the treasurer as city tax collector. Ducktown's audited financial statements also identify the county as the assessor and the city as the collector of its levy. [City Hall](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/) · [City charter, § 6-22-110](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/ducktown) · [City audit, note 1 under Property Taxes](https://comptroller.tn.gov/content/dam/cot/la/advanced-search/2023/city/1662-2023-c-ducktown-rpt-cpa834-2-23-24.pdf)

For a **Polk County** bill, use the trustee's **View or Pay Property Taxes** link. If the question is why the assessed value changed, contact the county property assessor. Keep the city and county receipts with their respective bills. [County tax payments](https://polkcountytn.gov/trustee/) · [Property assessor](https://polkcountytn.gov/property-assessor/)
