Call **423-496-3546** with your street address for city pickup. Ducktown's code puts collection inside the city on an announced schedule and gives the Board of Commissioners authority to set the charge by resolution. Ask City Hall for your collection day and the current rate. For a missed pickup, give your address and usual collection day. [City Hall](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/) · [Ducktown Municipal Code, §§ 17-106 and 17-109](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/combined/Ducktown-code.pdf)

If you're taking household trash yourself, Polk County lists the **Grassy Creek convenience center at 2000 Grassy Creek Road, Copperhill**, along with its other sites. Check the hours and accepted materials before loading up. The county page also explains what proof it accepts when a vehicle carries out-of-county plates. [Polk County sanitation and drop-off locations](https://polkcountytn.gov/sanitation-2/)
