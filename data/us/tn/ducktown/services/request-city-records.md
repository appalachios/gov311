Tennessee residents can ask to inspect or copy existing public records, subject to the law's exceptions. Ducktown's recorder keeps the board's minutes and ordinances. For example, if you're tracking a vote, ask for the minutes for the meeting date and the numbered ordinance adopted that night. [Ducktown charter, §§ 6-21-403 and 6-21-404](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/ducktown)

Call **423-496-3546** or email **ducktown@etcmail.com**. Tell the recorder whether you want to inspect the record or receive a copy. Ask for the city's records policy and an estimate before ordering copies. [City clerk's office](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ducktown)

Readily available records should be available promptly. If the city needs more time, it has seven business days to provide the record, explain a denial in writing or give you a written estimate of how long production will take. [Tennessee Comptroller's public records FAQ](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-public-records-act-faqs.html)
