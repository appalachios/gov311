Open the regional planning office's **Ducktown** page and choose **Interactive Full-Screen Map**. Find your parcel and its district. In the zoning ordinance, open the chapter for that district and look up the use you have in mind. A use listed as “permitted on appeal” needs a decision from the Board of Zoning Appeals. If you are dividing a lot, the same page links the city's subdivision regulations. [Ducktown map and planning documents](https://www.sedev.org/ducktown/) · [Zoning ordinance](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/08.01.24-Ducktown-Zoning-Ordinance.pdf)

The regional planning office posts a copy amended through **August 1, 2024**. The University of Tennessee's combined city code goes through June 2013. For example, the 2024 zoning text marks a change to breweries and distilleries in § 7.03. Check subsequent changes with the city building inspector before relying on either edition for a project. [Zoning ordinance, § 7.03](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/08.01.24-Ducktown-Zoning-Ordinance.pdf) · [Combined code edition](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-ducktown)

For a proposed rezoning, select **Agendas** on the planning page. If the folder asks you to sign in, call **423-496-3546** and ask for the planning agenda, the map showing the proposed change and the public hearing notice. Section 13.09 calls for the planning commission to review the change and make a recommendation to the city's Board of Commissioners. Ask the recorder for the board's agenda and final ordinance to find out what happened to that recommendation. [Planning commission agendas](https://www.sedev.org/ducktown/) · [Zoning ordinance, § 13.09](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/08.01.24-Ducktown-Zoning-Ordinance.pdf)
