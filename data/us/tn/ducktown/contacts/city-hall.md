Call **423-496-3546**. For the agenda, a proposed ordinance or the minutes of a vote, ask for the **city recorder**. For work on a building or a change in how you use land, ask for the **building inspector** and give the street address. [City office directory](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/) · [Zoning ordinance, chapter 13](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/08.01.24-Ducktown-Zoning-Ordinance.pdf)

Visit City Hall at **1646 Highway 68 North, Ducktown**. Send letters to **PO Box 506, Ducktown, TN 37326**. The clerk's office email is **ducktown@etcmail.com**.
