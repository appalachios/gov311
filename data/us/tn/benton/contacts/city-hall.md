Benton City Hall is at 6496 US-411. Call 423-338-5733 and choose option 3 for the recorder. Mail goes to P.O. Box 687, Benton, TN 37307. For a city ordinance or minutes, give the recorder the subject, ordinance number or meeting date. [Polk County city offices](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/) · [Benton’s municipal directory](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/benton)

Benton is also the county seat. City Hall handles city services. For vehicle registration, tags or a marriage license, go to the Polk County Clerk. [County Clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/)

For Benton police administration, choose option 1 on the City Hall line. For a non-emergency requiring dispatch in West Polk or Benton, call **423-338-8215**. For an emergency, call **911**. [Polk County city offices](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/) · [Polk County 911 dispatch](https://polkcountytn911.com/)
