Call Benton City Hall at 423-338-5733 with the street address and say whether it already has a meter. Polk County lists the city as the water and sewer provider inside Benton. Ocoee Utility District and other providers serve parts of the surrounding county. [Polk County utility directory](https://polkcountytn.gov/services/)

For an existing home, ask the city how to put the account in your name and what deposit is due. For a new connection, ask the city to approve service before arranging meter work. Sections 18-103 and 18-104 cover service requests and deposits. Confirm the current charge with City Hall. For a private well or septic question outside the water system, the county directory lists the Ground Water & Septic office. [Benton Municipal Code, title 18](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/combined/Benton-code.pdf) · [County utility contacts](https://polkcountytn.gov/services/)

To pay an existing city bill online, select **Utility Bills** on the Benton payment page. It identifies City Hall at 6496 Highway 411 and may ask you to verify that you are a person before account lookup. If you cannot get to your bill, call 423-338-5733. [Benton payments](https://bentontn.governmentwindow.com/start.html)
