1. Open the **Interactive Full-Screen Map** on the Southeast Tennessee Development District’s Benton page. Locate the parcel and record the zoning district. Then open **Zoning Ordinance** and read the chapter for that district. [Benton planning maps and documents](https://www.sedev.org/benton/)
2. Look for amendments affecting the proposed use. For example, the zoning ordinance’s cover records Ordinance 222 on two dwellings on lots of at least two acres. Read the applicable district text and conditions before relying on that description. For a proposed lot split, open the separate **Subdivision Regulations** and check the plat requirements. [Zoning ordinance](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Benton-Zoning-7.11.2024.pdf) · [Subdivision regulations, amended May 8, 2025](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Benton-Sub-Regs-5.8.25.pdf)
3. Open **Agendas** on the planning page. If the folder asks you to sign in, ask the city recorder for the public agenda for the date you need. The published planning commission schedule is the second Thursday at 10 a.m. Eastern. Ask the city recorder which board will act on your request and whether a later ordinance affects your parcel. [Planning commission](https://www.sedev.org/benton/) · [City offices](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/)

For a new one- or two-family home, the state also issues a residential building permit in Benton. [How to get the building permit](apply-for-a-residential-building-permit.md)
