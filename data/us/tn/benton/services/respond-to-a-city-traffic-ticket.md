Look at the court named on your citation. For Benton City Court, contact the city court clerk at 423-338-5733, extension 104, or clerk@cityofbentontn.com. Have the citation and court date in front of you. Ask the clerk how to appear or contest it, or whether you can pay online. [City court clerk](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/benton) · [Benton charter, § 3.05](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Benton_cht.pdf)

For an eligible city ticket, open Benton’s payment service and select **Traffic Tickets**. The service may ask you to verify that you are a person. A new citation can take several days to appear. If a court date is approaching, contact the clerk even if the ticket has yet to appear online. If the citation names a Polk County court, use the county court clerk listed for that case. [Benton payments](https://bentontn.governmentwindow.com/start.html) · [Ticket lookup notice](https://bentontn.governmentwindow.com/notify.html) · [Polk County court clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/legal-courts/clerk-of-court/)
