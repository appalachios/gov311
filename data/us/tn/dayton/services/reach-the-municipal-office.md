The municipal directory lists **399 First Avenue, Dayton, TN 37321** as the contact address for Dayton. Call **(423) 775-1817** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dayton) · [Municipal website](https://www.daytontn.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Sanitation, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dayton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rhea County records](../../rhea-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.daytontn.net/departments/sanitation.php)
- **Zoning/Codes/Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Zoning/Codes/Permits](https://www.daytontn.net/departments/zoning_codes_permits/index.php)
- **Open records request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open records request](https://www.daytontn.net/residents/open_records_request.php)
- **Utility Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing](https://www.daytontn.net/residents/utilities.php)
