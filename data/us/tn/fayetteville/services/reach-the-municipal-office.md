The municipal directory lists **110 Elk Avenue South, Fayetteville, TN 37334** as the contact address for Fayetteville. Call **(931) 433-6154** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/fayetteville) · [Municipal website](https://www.fayettevilletn.com/index.php)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Internet, VoIP Phone, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/fayetteville)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.fayettevilletn.com/departments/planning_and_codes_department/building_and_codes.php)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lincoln County records](../../lincoln-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
