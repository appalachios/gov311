The municipal directory lists **7617 Highway 41 North, Suite 101, Adams, TN 37010** as the contact address for Adams. Call **(615) 696-2593** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/adams) · [Municipal website](https://adamstennessee.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/adams)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
