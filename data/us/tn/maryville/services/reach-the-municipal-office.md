The municipal directory lists **400 West Broadway, Maryville, TN 37801-4710** as the contact address for Maryville. Call **(865) 273-3900** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/maryville) · [Municipal website](https://www.maryvillegov.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/maryville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Blount County records](../../blount-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
