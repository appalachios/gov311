The municipal directory lists **5548 Franklin Pike, Suite 101, Nashville, TN 37220** as the contact address for Oak Hill. Call **(615) 371-8291** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oak-hill)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Recycling**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oak-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Davidson County records](../../nashville/services/find-a-property-map.md)
