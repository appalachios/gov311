The municipal directory lists **PO Box 39, Unicoi, TN 37692-0039** as the contact address for Unicoi. Call **(423) 743-7162** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/unicoi)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Unicoi County records](../../unicoi-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
