The municipal directory lists **9017 Hiwassee Street NW, PO Box 431, Charleston, TN 37310** as the contact address for Charleston. Call **(423) 336-1483** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/charleston)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/charleston)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Bradley County records](../../bradley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
