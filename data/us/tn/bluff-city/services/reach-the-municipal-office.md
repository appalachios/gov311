The municipal directory lists **4391 Bluff City Highway, P.O. Box 70, Bluff City, TN 37618** as the contact address for Bluff City. Call **(423) 538-7144** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bluff-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.bluffcitytn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bluff-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sullivan County records](../../sullivan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
