The municipal directory lists **131 East Church Street, Lewisburg, TN 37091** as the contact address for Lewisburg. Call **(931) 359-1544** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lewisburg) · [Municipal website](https://lewisburgtn.gov/)
