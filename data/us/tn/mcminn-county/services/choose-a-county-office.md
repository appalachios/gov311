For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The McMinn County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/mcminn)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(423) 506-3527**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(423) 745-1232**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(423) 745-2743**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(423) 745-1291**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(423) 745-0843**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in McMinn County include Athens, Calhoun, Englewood, Etowah, Niota, Sweetwater.
