The municipal directory lists **1061 South Main, Milan, TN 38358** as the contact address for Milan. Call **(731) 686-3301** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/milan) · [Municipal website](https://cityofmilantn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/milan)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
