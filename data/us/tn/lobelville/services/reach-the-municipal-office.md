The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 369, Lobelville, TN 37097** as the contact address for Lobelville. Call **(931) 593-2285** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lobelville) · [Municipal website](https://lobelvilletn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lobelville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Perry County records](../../perry-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Utility Services**. Contact City Hall for water, sewer and gas service or check what the sanitation crew collects. [City Utility Services](https://lobelvilletn.org/index.php/city-services/city-utility-services)
