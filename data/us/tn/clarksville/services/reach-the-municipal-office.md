The municipal directory lists **1 Public Square, PO Box 928, Clarksville, TN 37040** as the contact address for Clarksville. Call **(931) 645-7444** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/clarksville) · [Municipal website](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/clarksville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Montgomery County records](../../montgomery-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report A Concern Submit questions or concerns through SeeClickFix**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report A Concern Submit questions or concerns through SeeClickFix](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/139/Report-a-Concern-Via-SeeClickFix)
- **Obstruction Permit Information**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Obstruction Permit Information](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/378/Obstruction-Permit-Information)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/365/Public-Records-Request)
- **City Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/160/City-Clerk)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/173/Property-Tax)
