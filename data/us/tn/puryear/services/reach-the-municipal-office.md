The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 278, Puryear, TN 38251-0278** as the contact address for Puryear. Call **(731) 247-5362** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/puryear)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/puryear)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Henry County records](../../henry-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
