The municipal directory lists **211 North Main, PO Box 59, Erwin, TN 37650** as the contact address for Erwin. Call **(423) 743-6231** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/erwin) · [Municipal website](https://www.erwintn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/erwin)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Unicoi County records](../../unicoi-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
