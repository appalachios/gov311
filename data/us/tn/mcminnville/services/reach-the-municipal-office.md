The municipal directory lists **101 East Main Street, P.O. Box 7088, McMinnville, TN 37110** as the contact address for McMinnville. Call **(931) 473-1200** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mcminnville) · [Municipal website](https://www.mcminnvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mcminnville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Warren County records](../../warren-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
