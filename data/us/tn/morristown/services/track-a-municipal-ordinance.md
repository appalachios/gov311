To follow a proposal in Morristown, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st and 3rd Tuesday at 5:00 PM at Municipal Building.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/morristown)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-morristown) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/morristown)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Morristown Code § 1-204 requires written ordinances and a council reading and vote on two separate days. The recorder keeps the votes. Compare the text before the council on each date with the ordinance that received final approval. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/morristown) · [Governing text](https://library.municode.com/tn/morristown/codes/code_of_ordinances)
