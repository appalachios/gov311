The municipal directory lists **100 West First North Street, P.O. Box 1499, Morristown, TN 37816-1499** as the contact address for Morristown. Call **(423) 581-0100** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/morristown) · [Municipal website](https://www.mymorristown.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamblen County records](../../hamblen-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Inspections**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspections](https://www.mymorristown.com/departments/building_inspections/index.php)
- **Make an Open Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Make an Open Records Request](https://www.mymorristown.com/government/administration/open_records_request.php)
- **Waste Collection/Recycling**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Waste Collection/Recycling](https://www.mymorristown.com/departments/public_works/solidwaste.php)
- **Pay Real Estate Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Pay Real Estate Taxes](https://morristown.munisselfservice.com/citizens/RealEstate/Default.aspx?mode=new)
