The municipal directory lists **199 Town Center Parkway, Spring Hill, TN 37174** as the contact address for Spring Hill. Call **(931) 486-2252** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spring-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.springhilltn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spring-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Maury, Williamson. [Maury County records](../../maury-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Williamson County records](../../williamson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **trash | Recycling Pickup**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [trash | Recycling Pickup](https://www.springhilltn.org/915/Trash-Recycling-Pickup)
- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.springhilltn.org/140/City-Recorder)
- **New Water Service Application & Agreement**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [New Water Service Application & Agreement](https://www.springhilltn.org/487/New-Water-Service-Application-Agreement)
- **Applications & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Applications & Permits](https://www.springhilltn.org/884/Applications-Permits)
- **Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Taxes](https://www.springhilltn.org/296/Property-Taxes)
