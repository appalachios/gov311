The municipal directory lists **PO Box 256, Mt. Juliet, TN 37121** as the contact address for Mt. Juliet. Call **(615) 754-2552** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mt-juliet) · [Municipal website](https://www.mtjuliet-tn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mt-juliet)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Wilson County records](../../wilson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utility Payments**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Payments](https://www.invoicecloud.com/portal/%28S%281pswqmf3ay3tourpxfplaigj%29%29/2/Site2.aspx?G=b49ad663-e475-46a3-acac-e0c4bffb24b2)
- **Building & Codes Department**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes Department](https://www.mtjuliet-tn.gov/150/Building-Codes-Department)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.mtjuliet-tn.gov/160/Property-Tax)
- **Stormwater-Sewer Procedures for New Projects**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Stormwater-Sewer Procedures for New Projects](https://www.mtjuliet-tn.gov/230/Stormwater-Sewer-Procedures-for-New-Proj)
