The municipal directory lists **190 Church Street NE, P.O. Box 1519, Cleveland, TN 37364-1519** as the contact address for Cleveland. Call **(423) 476-8931** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cleveland) · [Municipal website](https://clevelandtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cleveland)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Bradley County records](../../bradley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Records & Data Open Records Requests**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Records & Data Open Records Requests](https://clevelandtn.gov/468/Open-Records)
- **property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [property Taxes](https://clevelandtn.gov/171/Taxes)
- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://clevelandtn.gov/163/Business-Licenses)
