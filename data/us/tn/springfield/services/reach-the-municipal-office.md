The municipal directory lists **405 North Main Street, P.O. Box 788, Springfield, TN 37172** as the contact address for Springfield. Call **(615) 382-2200** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/springfield) · [Municipal website](https://springfieldtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Gas, Sanitation, Stormwater, Wastewater, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/springfield)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://springfieldtn.gov/requesttracker.aspx)
