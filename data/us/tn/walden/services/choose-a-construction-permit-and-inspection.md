Walden has a Signal Mountain mailing address, and the Town of Signal Mountain operates its own permit office. Check the parcel’s actual town. For **Walden**, use Hamilton County’s forms and call **423-209-7860** for construction permits or inspections. Give the project address and identify Walden, district **3W**. Contact Walden Town Hall at **423-886-4362** about local approvals and other town requests.
