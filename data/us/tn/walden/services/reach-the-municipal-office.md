The municipal directory lists **1836 Taft Highway, Walden, TN 37377-3512** as the contact address for Walden. Call **(423) 886-4362** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/walden) · [Municipal website](https://www.waldentn.gov/)
