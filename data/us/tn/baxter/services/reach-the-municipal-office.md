The municipal directory lists **200 Main Street, P.O. Box  335, Baxter, TN 38544** as the contact address for Baxter. Call **(931) 858-4111** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/baxter) · [Municipal website](https://www.baxtertn.gov/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.baxtertn.gov/bldg_permits.html)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Putnam County records](../../putnam-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.baxtertn.gov/city_recorder.html)
