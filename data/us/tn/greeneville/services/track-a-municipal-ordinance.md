To follow a proposal in Greeneville, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st and 3rd Tuesday at 4:00 PM at G. Thomas Love Boardroom.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greeneville)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-greeneville) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/greeneville)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 17 requires presentation on two days and an affirmative vote of a majority of the entire council on the second presentation. The caption or a statement of substance ordinarily suffices, but a council member or town taxpayer can request a full reading before final passage. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/greeneville) · [Governing text](https://core-docs.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/4541/TOG/3915210/TOWNCHARTER.2023.pdf#page=9)
