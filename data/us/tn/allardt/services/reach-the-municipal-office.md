The municipal directory lists **2015 Michigan Avenue, PO Box 159, Allardt, TN 38504** as the contact address for Allardt. Call **(931) 879-7125** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/allardt)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/allardt)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fentress County records](../../fentress-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
