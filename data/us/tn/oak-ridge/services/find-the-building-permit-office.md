For a property inside Oak Ridge, start with city Codes Enforcement for local code issues. Identify the address, describe the work or condition and ask which application or inspection applies. A business can also use the city’s business licenses and permits directory.

For an assessment or deed search, check the parcel county first. Oak Ridge lies in Anderson and Roane counties. Use the county containing the land for its assessment and recorded instruments. The city’s municipal rules apply within city limits on either side of the county line.
