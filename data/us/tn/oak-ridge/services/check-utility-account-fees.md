Use the Utility Business Office fee schedule to estimate the initial cost. It lists account setup separately from the deposits for electricity, water and sewer. Ask which services apply at your address and which charges are due before connection. If service was disconnected for nonpayment, check the reconnection and after-hours lines as well. The table includes earlier rates for comparison. Use the adopted rate column for the applicable period. [Utility Business Office fees and deposits](https://www.oakridgetn.gov/388/Utility-Business-Office-Fees)
