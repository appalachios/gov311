Open the City Council meeting in the Agenda Center. Read the ordinance text attached to the item and check the reading. The published December 15, 2025 proceedings explain that council approved ordinances on first reading and scheduled a public hearing and second reading for January 12, 2026. A scheduled second reading still requires a later decision.

Follow the measure to the later meeting. Check amendments, the final vote and the effective date. Contact the City Clerk for the enacted version and related proceedings if the posted documents leave a gap. The charter sets the rules for council action. [City meeting records](https://www.oakridgetn.gov/AgendaCenter) · [Published council proceedings](https://www.oakridgetn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3676/Proceedings-of-the-December-15-2025-City-Council-Meeting-PDF) · [City Clerk](https://oakridgetn.gov/158/City-Clerk) · [City charter](https://www.oakridgetn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2308/City-Charter---Updated-December-2024---Includes-November-Referendum)

Charter § 12 requires two readings at least a week apart, and an ordinary ordinance takes effect ten days after adoption. An emergency ordinance stating the facts and reasons can take effect upon adoption if public safety and welfare require it and a majority of council approves it on two successive days.
