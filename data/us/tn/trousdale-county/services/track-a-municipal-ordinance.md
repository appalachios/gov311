To follow a proposal in Hartsville, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “4th Monday at 7:00 PM at Courthouse.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hartsville)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-hartsville) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/hartsville)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 2.05 ordinarily requires two readings at separate meetings. Charter amendments, taxation and specified service-district measures need three readings. After the second approval, two-thirds of the full commission may vote to proceed with the third reading at the same meeting. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/hartsville) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/HTC%20Charter%20as%20Rev%20202208.pdf#page=7)
