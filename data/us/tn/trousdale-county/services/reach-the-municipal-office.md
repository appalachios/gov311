The municipal directory lists **328 Broadway, Room 6, Hartsville, TN 37074** as the contact address for Hartsville. Call **(615) 374-2461** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hartsville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hartsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Trousdale County records](../../trousdale-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
