The municipal directory lists **Liberty Municipal Building; Main Street; PO Box 8, Liberty, TN 37095** as the contact address for Liberty. Use the municipal directory to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/liberty)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/liberty)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [DeKalb County records](../../dekalb-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
