The municipal directory lists **801 Anderson St., PO Box 1189, Bristol, TN 37621-1189** as the contact address for Bristol. Call **(423) 989-5500** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bristol)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bristol)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sullivan County records](../../sullivan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utility Services**. For a city utility billing question, reach the Finance Department at 423-989-5500. [Utility Services](https://www.bristoltn.gov/143/Utility-Services)
