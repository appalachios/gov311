The municipal directory lists **139 S Main St, PO Box 10, Bulls Gap, TN 37711** as the contact address for Bulls Gap. Call **(423) 235-5216** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bulls-gap) · [Municipal website](https://www.bullsgaptn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bulls-gap)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
