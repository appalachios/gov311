The municipal directory lists **501 South Front Street, Rives, TN 38253-4373** as the contact address for Rives. Call **(731) 536-5689** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rives)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rives)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Obion County records](../../obion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
