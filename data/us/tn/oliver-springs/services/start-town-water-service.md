For a property served by the town, go to City Hall at **717 Main Street** to start service. Bring proof of ownership or your lease and a copy of your valid ID or driver’s license. The Water Department lists a nonrefundable connection fee and accepts cash, check or cashier’s check for this step. Call **865-435-7722** to confirm the current amount, payment method or how to disconnect service. The page gives a different number for after-hours water calls. [Town water and sewer service instructions](https://oliversprings-tn.gov/960/Water-and-Sewer-Services)
