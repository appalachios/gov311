Oliver Springs spans Anderson, Morgan and Roane counties. For a project inside town limits, follow the **Building permit Application Links** on the town’s page. The town sends applicants to the Roane County SmartGov portal. That is the published municipal filing route even though the town has land in three counties. Confirm the address and permitting office with Building Codes at **865-717-4230** if the portal cannot locate it.

The town lists new construction, fences, certain roof work, plumbing renovations and driveway extensions among projects requiring permits. Give the office your proposed scope before starting.
