The Agenda Center can list two meetings on the same day. On October 15, 2026, for example, it lists a Water Board agenda and a Town Council agenda. Open the one that names the body handling your issue. For a water rule, also check the town’s water codes and regulations.

Search the Archive Center by meeting date and record type for the minutes. Find the motion, any amendment and the recorded action before treating an agenda proposal as adopted. If the archive lacks an attachment, ask Town Hall for the minutes and signed measure by date and number.
