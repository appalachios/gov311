The Board of Mayor and Aldermen has to consider and adopt an ordinance on two separate days. A majority of all board seats is a quorum. When a quorum is present, passage takes a majority of the members present. The board records the yes and no votes. The charter allows other forms of board action on one day, so look at whether the item is an ordinance or a resolution. [Farragut charter identification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/farragut) · [Tennessee Code § 6-2-102, page C-21](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/2026-10/Mayor_Aldermanic_Charter.pdf)

After final passage, the town must publish the ordinance **or its caption** in a newspaper of general circulation in the municipality. Publication is required before the ordinance can take effect. A caption is only the title. For the rule that applies to you, request the final ordinance, exhibits, any amendments and the publication date from the recorder. Check the ordinance for a later effective date. [Tennessee Code § 6-2-101](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/2026-10/Mayor_Aldermanic_Charter.pdf) · [Town recorder](https://www.townoffarragut.org/126/Town-Recorder)

To see a proposed change before the vote, use the [meeting guide](../services/follow-a-board-meeting.md). For an existing rule, look up the section in the [Farragut Municipal Code](https://www.townoffarragut.org/843/Board-of-Mayor-and-Aldermen-Resources), then ask the recorder for any amendment adopted after the published edition.
