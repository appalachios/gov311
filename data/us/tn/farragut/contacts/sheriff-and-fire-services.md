Call **911** for immediate danger, fire or a medical emergency. For a **Knox County Sheriff’s Office non-emergency dispatch** call, use **865-215-2444**. To **make a report by phone**, call **865-215-2243** for Sheriff’s Teleserve. The town’s new-resident page labels Teleserve as non-emergency. The emergency communications district lists each line by its function. [Knox 911 directory](https://knox911.org/) · [Town emergency services](https://www.townoffarragut.org/194/Emergency-Services) · [New resident directory](https://www.townoffarragut.org/596/New-Residents)

For questions about a fire subscription at your address, the town lists **Rural Metro Fire**. Knox 911 lists its business office at **865-321-8613**. [Fire service listing](https://www.townoffarragut.org/194/Emergency-Services) · [Emergency communications directory](https://knox911.org/)
