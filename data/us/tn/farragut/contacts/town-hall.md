Call **865-966-7057** for Town Hall. Ask the **town recorder** for a Board of Mayor and Aldermen agenda, minutes or adopted ordinance. For a permit or zoning question, give the property address to **Planning & Codes at 865-675-2384**. [Town Hall](https://www.townoffarragut.org/250/Town-Hall-Meetings) · [Recorder](https://www.townoffarragut.org/126/Town-Recorder)

Farragut’s General Facts page describes its western edge as the Loudon County line. The state’s July 2026 certified population report lists parts of the town in **both Knox and Loudon counties**. The report assigns the town’s entire certified population to the Knox portion and zero to the Loudon portion. The Census Bureau also lists the incorporated place in both counties. For a deed, assessment or tax bill near that boundary, confirm the parcel’s county before choosing the county office. For a town permit, check the town limits on the map.
