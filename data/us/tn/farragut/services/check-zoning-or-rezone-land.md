Start with the street address and parcel number. On the town’s maps page, open **Adopted Zoning Map** for the current district. The **Adopted Future Land Use Map** describes the plan for future growth. Then use the zoning ordinance in the municipal code to check the proposed use, setbacks and conditions. Call Planning & Zoning at **865-675-2384** if a boundary crosses the parcel or a later ordinance may affect it. [Town maps](https://www.townoffarragut.org/112/Maps---Geographic-Information-Systems-GI) · [Municipal code](https://www.townoffarragut.org/843/Board-of-Mayor-and-Aldermen-Resources)

For a change in zoning, file through the town’s MGO system and check the **Municipal Planning Commission** packet for the staff report, map, proposed text and recommendation. Follow a proposed zoning ordinance to the **Board of Mayor and Aldermen** for the readings and final vote. If you need a variance, special exception or appeal of an interpretation, ask staff about the **Board of Zoning Appeals**.
