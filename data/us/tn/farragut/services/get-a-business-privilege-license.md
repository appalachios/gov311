1. Check the physical business location. Farragut’s privilege license generally applies to businesses operating from a location inside town, including home businesses. A separate location may need its own license. Check the exemptions in the town’s FAQ and Chapter 8, Article 2 of its code. A home-based business also needs the town’s **Customary Home Occupation Permit**. [Town license rules, forms and home occupation permit](https://www.townoffarragut.org/125/Licenses-Applications) · [Town filing FAQ](https://www.townoffarragut.org/DocumentCenter/View/8020/Town-of-Farragut-Business-Privilege-License-FAQ)
2. Check county business tax licensing separately. The town webpage says every business with a physical location needs a Knox County license. For the state-law minimal activity license, Tennessee Code § 67-4-723(b)(2) makes it optional when sales in the jurisdiction are **$3,000 or less per year**. This threshold concerns the county business tax license. Check the town’s own privilege license requirements even when the county license is optional. [State law, § 67-4-723(b), PDF pages 596–597](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/TNCodeBills/2024/TNCodeBill_Volume2_2024.pdf#page=596) · [Tennessee Revenue guidance](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360057607771-BUS-15-Minimal-Activity-Licenses)
3. Choose **New Business Privilege License** for a first application, added location or change of ownership. Use the **renewal** for an existing license. Apply online or take the paper form to Town Hall. If the form asks for a county tax license number your business does not need, contact the recorder at **865-966-7057** for filing instructions. [Town forms](https://www.townoffarragut.org/125/Licenses-Applications) · [New application](https://townoffarragut.org/DocumentCenter/View/7964)
