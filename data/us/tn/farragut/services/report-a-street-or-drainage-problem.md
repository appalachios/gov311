Submit a **citizen request** in MyGovernmentOnline with the nearest address or intersection. Say what needs attention and which side of the road it is on. For assistance with a town-maintained street, greenway or public drainage project, call Farragut Public Works at **865-218-7750**. If the road’s owner is unclear, give the location to the town so staff can identify the right office. [Citizen request portal and instructions](https://www.townoffarragut.org/645/Permitting-Information) · [Public Works services](https://www.townoffarragut.org/190/Public-Works) · [Department phone directory](https://www.townoffarragut.org/250/Town-Hall-Meetings)

For winter service, Public Works posts a snow removal schedule that sets the order for plowing. Use the schedule linked on the department page for the street you are asking about. [Snow removal schedule](https://www.townoffarragut.org/190/Public-Works)
