1. Open the [town agenda portal](https://www.townoffarragut.org/Agendas) and choose the **Board of Mayor and Aldermen** meeting for the date you want. Open the agenda for the order of business and the **meeting packet** for proposed wording and exhibits. Check the meeting notice for the time, location and opportunity to speak. The board usually meets at 6 p.m. on the second and fourth Thursdays. [Board meeting information](https://www.townoffarragut.org/87/Board-of-Mayor-Aldermen)
2. Write down the ordinance number and reading. Follow the same number to the next board meeting. A workshop, public comment or Planning Commission recommendation can occur before the elected board votes.
3. After the meeting, open the **approved minutes** and locate the motion, any amendment and the recorded vote. For the final law and its publication record, contact the [town recorder](https://www.townoffarragut.org/126/Town-Recorder).

**An example from the town budget.** The May 14, 2026 agenda item concerned Ordinance 26-11, the annual budget. After public comment, the board approved an amended first-reading motion adding **$15,000 for the Farragut Business Alliance**. On May 28, the board approved the budget ordinance on second reading by five votes to zero. A reader who stopped at the agenda title would miss both the change and the final vote.
