Give the provider the service address when you open or transfer an account. The town’s directory lists different utilities for each service. [Farragut utility directory](https://www.townoffarragut.org/440/Utilities)

- **Water and sewer.** Contact First Utility District of Knox County at **865-966-9741**. Use its online application or visit the office at 122 Durwood Drive. [First Utility District service application](https://www.fudknox.org/start-stop-service/)
- **Electricity.** Contact Lenoir City Utilities Board (LCUB) at **844-687-5282** and use its new-service instructions. [LCUB signup](https://www.lcub.com/sign-up-for-service/)
- **Natural gas.** Contact Knoxville Utilities Board (KUB) at **865-524-2911** and use its start-service process. For a new connection, identify the project as new construction. [KUB service options](https://www.kub.org/start-stop-service/start-or-stop-services-terms-and-conditions)
