For curbside garbage or recycling, contact a private hauler serving the address. Residents arrange their own collection. The town lists companies under **Garbage & Recycling**. For a missed pickup, use the provider on your bill or check with your property manager if pickup is included with the property. [Farragut collection options](https://www.townoffarragut.org/398/Garbage-Recycling) · [New resident services](https://www.townoffarragut.org/596/New-Residents)

For a drop-off, follow the town’s **Dutchtown Convenience Center** link and check the county’s hours and accepted materials for that location. Use the separate household hazardous waste instructions when applicable. McFee Park’s recycling bins serve park visitors. [Drop-off and hazardous waste options](https://www.townoffarragut.org/398/Garbage-Recycling)
