Ask the town recorder for the existing record by name, date, meeting or ordinance number. Call **865-966-7057**, visit Town Hall or email **recorder@townoffarragut.org**. The town asks for proof of Tennessee residency. Say whether you want to inspect the record or receive a copy. Inspection alone does not require a written request or an inspection fee. For copies, the town’s form lets you ask for a cost estimate. [Town recorder](https://www.townoffarragut.org/126/Town-Recorder) · [Request form](https://www.townoffarragut.org/DocumentCenter/View/8614/Ordinance-25-16-Public-Records-Request-Form)

The town’s webpage says “seven days.” Tennessee Code § 10-7-503(a)(2)(B) requires prompt access when practicable. If prompt access is impracticable, the custodian has **seven business days** to provide the record, give the legal basis for a written denial, or explain in writing how much time production will take. If your email request receives no confirmation, call the recorder. [Enacted language, section 1](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Bills/105/Chapter/PC1179.pdf) · [State Comptroller’s records guidance](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-public-records-act-faqs.html)
