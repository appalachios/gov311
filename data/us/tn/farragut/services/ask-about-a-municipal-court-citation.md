Read the court and issuing agency on the notice. Farragut Municipal Court hears violations of town ordinances and adopted codes, including automated red-light enforcement. For a notice from this court, call the clerk at **865-218-3363** or email **court@townoffarragut.org**. Use the court shown on your ticket if another agency issued it. [Municipal court](https://www.townoffarragut.org/104/Municipal-Court)

For a Farragut camera notice, use the **PhotoNotice** link on the town’s traffic enforcement page and enter the town code **FARGTN** to view the video. For a lost citation, use the contact on the same page. To ask about a hearing conflict, call the clerk before the scheduled hearing and confirm any new date with the court. [Camera program](https://www.townoffarragut.org/128/Traffic-Enforcement-Program) · [Court procedures](https://www.townoffarragut.org/243/Courtroom-Procedures)
