1. Check the parcel against the town map, then describe the work to Planning & Codes at **865-675-2384**. Ask about building, trade and zoning approvals. For commercial construction, shell buildings, commercial renovations and projects requiring Planning Commission approval, the town requires a meeting or documented discussion with staff before plans go in. [Town maps](https://www.townoffarragut.org/112/Maps---Geographic-Information-Systems-GI) · [Filing requirements](https://www.townoffarragut.org/645/Permitting-Information)
2. Follow the town’s link to **MyGovernmentOnline (MGO)**. Select Farragut as the jurisdiction, choose the application for the work, and upload required plans as PDFs. When you create an account, answer the confirmation call to activate it. A kiosk on the second floor of Town Hall is available if you need help. [Apply for a permit](https://www.townoffarragut.org/645/Permitting-Information)
3. Use your project in MGO to check for plan comments, make payments, print the issued permit and request inspections. An application confirmation shows the town received your filing. Check for the issued permit before starting work. [Farragut permit instructions and checklists](https://www.townoffarragut.org/664/Documents-Handouts)
