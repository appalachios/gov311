Farragut reports that it has no municipal property tax. The town spans parts of Knox and Loudon counties, although the state's certified population report assigns all residents to Knox. For a parcel near the western edge, use the county name and parcel number from the assessment or recorded land description. A Farragut or Knoxville mailing address alone cannot settle that question. [Town tax information](https://www.townoffarragut.org/274/General-Facts) · [State county-by-county report](https://tnecd.com/?force_download=129209)

- **Knox County parcel.** Find the parcel in KGIS Maps and check its value with the Knox County Property Assessor. Use the Knox County Trustee’s payment page to look up the current bill, tax year and balance. [KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/map.htm) · [Knox assessor](https://property.knoxcounty.org/resources/faq/) · [Knox trustee](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/services/pay-taxes/)
- **Loudon County parcel.** Select **Loudon** in the Tennessee Property Viewer to find the parcel. The Loudon County Property Assessor handles questions about its value. The Loudon County Trustee links its payment service. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/) · [Loudon assessor](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/property-assessor/) · [Loudon trustee](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/trustee/)
