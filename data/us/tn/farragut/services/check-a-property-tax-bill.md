Farragut reports that it has no municipal property tax. The town spans parts of Knox and Loudon counties, although the state's certified population report assigns all residents to Knox. For a parcel near the western edge, use the county name and parcel number from the assessment or recorded land description. A Farragut or Knoxville mailing address alone cannot settle that question. [Town tax information](https://www.townoffarragut.org/274/General-Facts) · [State county-by-county report](https://tnecd.com/?force_download=129209)

- **Knox County parcel.** Check the value or map with the Knox County Property Assessor. Use the Knox County Trustee’s payment page to look up the current bill, tax year and balance. [Knox assessor](https://property.knoxcounty.org/resources/faq/) · [Knox trustee](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/services/pay-taxes/)
- **Loudon County parcel.** Check the parcel and value with the Loudon County Property Assessor. The Loudon County Trustee links its payment service. [Loudon assessor](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/property-assessor/) · [Loudon trustee](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/trustee/)
