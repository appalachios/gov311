The municipal directory lists **1201 Main Street, Humboldt, TN 38343** as the contact address for Humboldt. Call **(731) 784-2511** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/humboldt)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
