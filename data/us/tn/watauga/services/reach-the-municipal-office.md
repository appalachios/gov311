The municipal directory lists **PO Box 68, Watauga, TN 37694** as the contact address for Watauga. Call **(423) 928-3490** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/watauga)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Carter, Washington. [Carter County records](../../carter-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Washington County records](../../washington-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

The Census place-by-county table lists Watauga in Carter, Washington. The 2026 Census TIGER/Line place and county polygons and the Tennessee Comptroller’s current city and county map also show land in each of these counties. Use the parcel’s county for county services.
