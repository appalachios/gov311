The municipal directory lists **860 Orme Road, South Pittsburg, TN 37380** as the contact address for Orme. Use the municipal directory to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/orme)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/orme)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marion County records](../../marion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
