The municipal directory lists **145 Alexander Street, Greeneville, TN 37745** as the contact address for Tusculum. Call **(423) 638-6211** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tusculum) · [Municipal website](https://tusculumcity.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Greene County records](../../greene-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report A Problem**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report A Problem](https://tusculumcity.org/police/)
- **Permits & Licenses**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits & Licenses](https://tusculumcity.org/permits-licenses/)
- **Trash Pick Up**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Trash Pick Up](https://tusculumcity.org/trash/)
- **Pay Utility Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Pay Utility Bill](https://tusculumcity.org/pay-utility-bill/)
