The municipal directory lists **351 East Main Street, Pleasant Hill, TN 38578** as the contact address for Pleasant Hill. Call **(931) 277-3813** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pleasant-hill)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage - Solid Waste/Brush**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pleasant-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cumberland County records](../../cumberland-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
