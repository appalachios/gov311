Open City Meetings & Minutes and choose the City Council packet. Request the proposal from the city recorder if only the title appears. Charter § 4-2 permits a title-only reading when a copy reached the council and recorder at least three days before the first reading. Adoption takes four affirmative votes and two readings on different days at least a week apart. [Meeting records](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/government/city_council/city_meetings___minutes.php) · [Charter, article IV](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/City%20Charter%20-%202018.pdf)

Compare the first version with the final motion and signed text. The ordinary effective date is 15 days after final reading. An emergency ordinance must state the emergency and its reasons to take effect on passage. The city posts adopted ordinances awaiting codification separately. [Adopted ordinances awaiting codification](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/government/city_council/adopted_ordinances_not_yet_codified.php) · [City recorder and records requests](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/departments/city_recorder.php)

The written ordinance must pass two readings in open session on different days at least one week apart. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/shelbyville) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/shelbyville_cht.pdf#page=13)
