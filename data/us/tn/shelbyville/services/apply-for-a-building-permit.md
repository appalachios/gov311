Start with the city Building & Codes department. Identify the site address and describe the work, including additions, accessory structures or changes of use. Check its applications page for the documents that fit the project, then follow the city’s Online Permits, Applications & Services link to submit. [Building & Codes](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/departments/building___codes_department/index.php) · [Applications and permits](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/departments/building___codes_department/applications_permits_information/index.php)
