The municipal directory lists **204 Queen Street, P.O. Box 116, Oakdale, TN 37829** as the contact address for Oakdale. Call **(423) 369-4400** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oakdale)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Morgan County records](../../morgan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
