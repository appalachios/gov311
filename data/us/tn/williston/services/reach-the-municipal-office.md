The municipal directory lists **Highway 193, P.O. Box 131, Williston, TN 38076-0131** as the contact address for Williston. Call **(901) 465-4173** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/williston)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
