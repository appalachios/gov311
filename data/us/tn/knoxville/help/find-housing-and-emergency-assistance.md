For help finding shelter or support, call **211**. Tell the referral service what you need and where you are. If you can use the web, open **211 Resource Directory** on the city page. Search by ZIP code or county for food, housing or other assistance. Contact the provider you find to confirm available help, hours and how to apply. [Knoxville housing assistance and 211 directory](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/503/Do-You-Need-Help)

If you face eviction inside city limits, call **Legal Aid of East Tennessee at 865-371-8730**, or **865-637-0484** if you have a hearing within 14 days. The county’s eviction diversion application is for renters outside Knoxville and Farragut. [Legal Aid intake](https://laet.org/contact-us/) · [County eligibility and court contacts](../../knox-county/help/get-help-with-an-eviction.md)
