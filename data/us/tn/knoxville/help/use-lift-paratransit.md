**If you live in the service area**, call The LIFT at **865-215-7850** or download the application. Describe how the disability affects your ability to use the regular bus. Complete every section, have the healthcare professional complete and sign the verification, and sign the release and application. KAT offers an alternative format if you need one. [Application and signature instructions](https://katbus.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/LIFT-Application-with-Intro.pdf) · [LIFT handbook](https://katbus.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2022_THE-LIFT-HANDBOOK.pdf)

KAT’s page warns about electronic applications because the provider must sign. Farther down, **Contact Us** gives **katlift@katbus.com** and fax **865-215-7820** for sending a completed application. The handbook also allows submission by email. Send a copy of the completed, signed paperwork, or return it to **Knoxville Station, 301 Church Avenue, Knoxville, TN 37915**. Keep a copy and confirm receipt. Federal rules address interim service if KAT has not decided a completed application within the required time. Once certified, call to reserve rides. [How to submit](https://katbus.com/schedules-maps/) · [Station filing instructions](https://katbus.com/about-kat/faq/) · [Federal eligibility process](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-A/part-37/subpart-F/section-37.125)

**If you are visiting**, call ahead and ask for visitor service. KAT’s visitor paragraph mentions a completed application. Under 49 CFR § 37.127(c) and (e), documentation that you are ADA paratransit eligible at home establishes visitor eligibility. KAT cannot require local certification before providing the visitor service covered by this rule. If you lack home-system documentation, subsection (d) explains what other evidence may be requested. [Federal visitor rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-A/part-37/subpart-F/section-37.127) · [KAT visitor contact](https://katbus.com/schedules-maps/)
