If you own and live in a home inside Knoxville and plan to stay there after the repairs, call Housing and Neighborhood Development at **865-215-2120**. The program considers income and can assess needed work, help arrange contractors and provide low-interest or forgivable loans. [Program and income eligibility](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/516/Owner-Occupied-Home-Rehabilitation)

Download **Application** on the program page. The form itself explains that this first step is a screening. Complete and sign it, then return it using the mailing address or fax on the form. If the screening shows you may qualify, the city schedules an interview for the full application. Wait for the city’s decision before assuming it has approved or funded repairs. [Screening application and return instructions](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1008/Application-PDF)
