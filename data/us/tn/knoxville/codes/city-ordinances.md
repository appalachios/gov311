Knoxville has a home-rule charter. Under § 409, an ordinary ordinance must be read at two regular council meetings at least two weeks apart. Between readings, the written proposal is on file for public inspection at the City Recorder’s office. Ask for it by item number. The charter requires an amendment to show enough of the affected provision to make the change clear. [City charter, article IV](https://library.municode.com/tn/knoxville/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTITHCH_ARTIVLEBR_407SAFFVIYORE) · [Find the city recorder](../contacts/city-recorder.md)

Start with the agenda and its attachments. A first-reading vote moves an ordinary proposal forward. To see what became law, compare the version on second reading with the first, then check the recorded vote and the effective-date clause in the final ordinance. Section 409 supplies a default when the measure omits a date. Section 410 allows certain emergency measures to take effect on passage with a two-thirds vote of the full council.
