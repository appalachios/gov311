**For a pothole on a city street**, call **311** or choose **Pothole** in the city’s request form. Give the nearest address or intersection, where the hole is in the street and its approximate size. The city can refer a road issue to the responsible agency if another agency maintains it. For a failed streetlight, use the separate streetlight outage link on the same page. [Street repair and streetlight requests](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/291/Service-Request-Tips)

**For persistent flooding or a blocked drain**, contact 311 or Stormwater Engineering at **865-215-2148**. Give the location and describe where the water comes from and where it goes. An inspector can check who maintains that part of the drainage system. Some systems are on private property and the owner may be responsible for repair. Call **911** if someone is in immediate danger. [Stormwater investigation requests](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/410/Request-for-Service-Stormwater-Drainage) · [Emergency and routine requests](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/291/Service-Request-Tips)
