Enter the street address in the city’s **Service Day & Disposal** tool. Select **Calendar** for the pickup day or a reminder. For an unfamiliar item, **What Goes Where** tells you how to dispose of it. If the tool cannot find your address or the crew missed a collection, call **311** or **865-215-4311**. The same page links the online form for cart and collection problems. [Service day lookup and missed pickups](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/973/Service-Day-Disposal-Information)

For a sofa, appliance or other large item, check the bulky waste rules before setting it out. A construction project’s debris goes through a different disposal route. Appliances with refrigerant require special handling. The city lists which items crews can collect and when to use the solid waste facility. [Bulky waste and disposal alternatives](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/960/Bulky-Waste)
