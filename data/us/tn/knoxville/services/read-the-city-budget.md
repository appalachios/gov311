For the proposal, open the city’s budget page and check the fiscal year on the document. Use the budget calendar for hearings, public comment and council readings. In the 2026 to 2027 schedule, the city lists the mayor’s release, a first reading, public and legislative hearings, and a second reading as separate steps. [Budget office](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/438/Budget) · [2026 to 2027 budget calendar](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/442/2026-to-2027-Budget-Info)

If you need the amount Knoxville authorized, choose the year in the **Budgets - Adopted** archive, locate the department and fund, and read the final budget tables. To see if a later vote changed that amount, search council agendas and minutes for the fund or appropriation. Use the separate six-month financial report to see how the year is going. [Adopted budget archive](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/Archive.aspx?AMID=44) · [Council votes](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/1339/City-Council-Agenda-Minutes)
