Give **311** or **865-215-4311** the street address and describe what the inspector can see. Neighborhood Codes Enforcement lists dilapidated structures, overgrown or dirty lots, dumped material and abandoned vehicles. You can also use **Register a Complaint Online** on the department page or visit Room 520, 400 Main Street. [Neighborhood Codes Enforcement and complaint form](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/927/Neighborhood-Codes-Enforcement)

Keep the request number. The 311 page links to the city’s search for service requests, enforcement cases and work orders. If you need a copy of a notice or property file, ask for that record by address and date. [City record search](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/284/_311) · [Request city records](request-city-records.md)
