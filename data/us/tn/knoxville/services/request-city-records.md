For a building permit, variance or certificate of occupancy, use the property records form and send it to **bldginspections@knoxvilletn.gov**. Give the address and the type of document you need. For a police incident report, body camera recording, fire report, dispatch record or utility record, choose the custodian in the city’s directory. A recorded deed belongs with the **Knox County Register of Deeds**. [Property records form](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/201) · [Directory of records custodians](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/320/Guide-to-Public-Records-Requests) · [Deed office contact](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/477/Property-Taxes)

For other copies, the city provides a request form and directions for sending it with proof of identity to Communications. State the record, dates and whether you want paper or electronic copies. Ask for a cost estimate. For help finding the custodian, call the city records coordinator at **865-215-3480**. [City policy and forms](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/319/Public-Records-Policy)

**If you want only to inspect a record**, Tennessee Code § 10-7-503(a)(7)(A)(i) generally allows an oral request unless another law requires otherwise. A government can require a written request for copies. The state law also allows the city to verify Tennessee residency. Knoxville’s instructions for emailing a form apply to that filing route. Call the coordinator to arrange inspection.
