Check which provider serves the street address for electricity, water, sewer and gas. Knoxville Utilities Board (KUB) has a different service area for each utility. The city also lists other providers, including Lenoir City Utilities Board and water utility districts. For an existing account, start with the provider named on the bill. [City utility directory](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/1156/Utilities) · [KUB service areas](https://www.kub.org/about/about-kub/kub-service-areas/)

For a KUB address, choose **Start/Stop Service** on its website. Have the address, start date, email and identification ready. KUB explains connection fees and when it may require a deposit. If you need to use alternative identification, follow its customer service center instructions or call **865-524-2911** before visiting. [KUB service application and identification instructions](https://www.kub.org/start-stop-service)
