1. Open the [city council calendar](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/1339/City-Council-Agenda-Minutes), select the meeting date and choose **HTML Agenda**. Open the item and its supporting documents. For older business, use **Text Search** or **Advanced Search** and enter the topic, meeting date or case number. For public comment, sign up through the recorder after the agenda is posted.
2. Check the action for that item in the minutes. The city usually posts draft minutes after the meeting. If you need proof of the wording in force, get the approved minutes and the adopted ordinance, including later amendments. [City agenda system](https://knoxvilletn.primegov.com/public/portal) · [How Knoxville passes an ordinance](../codes/city-ordinances.md)
3. For a rezoning, compare **which body took each action**. On Vance Road, the Planning Commission denied case **11-L-24-RZ** on November 14, 2024. The planning case still carries that heading. On appeal, Knoxville City Council approved RN-2 residential zoning with the Hillside Protection Overlay retained, first on December 10, 2024, then on January 7, 2025. The final action is recorded under **O-7-2025**. Read the *Legislative Action and Disposition* section on page 3. [Planning case](https://knoxplanning.org/cases/11-L-24-RZ) · [Official case summary, page 3](https://agenda.knoxplanning.org/summary/11-L-24-RZ.pdf#page=3)
