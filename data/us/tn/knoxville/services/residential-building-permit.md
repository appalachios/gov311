1. Check the address in KGIS Maps using the city’s directions. For a parcel inside Knoxville, check its zoning and describe the proposed work. The city provides separate guides for new homes, additions, alterations or repairs, and demolition. [Development process and boundary lookup](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/889/Development-Process) · [City permit types and checklists](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/899/Plans-Review-Permits)
2. Choose **Apply for a permit online** on the city permits page. In the portal, choose **Permits**, then **Create an Application**. Attach the plans required for your project. Staff may return them for revisions before approval. Multifamily and commercial projects have a separate category on the same page. [City permits and application](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/899/Plans-Review-Permits)
3. Wait for the permit to be issued before beginning the permitted work. Arrange the required inspections during construction. The city issues a certificate of occupancy for a building that needs one after required inspections pass. [City inspection and occupancy steps](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/889/Development-Process)
