Enter where you are starting, where you need to go, and when you need to arrive in the KAT trip planner or Transit app. Check the direction, stop and timetable for your travel day. If the trip needs a transfer, look up both routes. A transit agent can help at **865-637-3000**. [KAT trip planner](https://katbus.com/how-to-ride/plan-your-trip/) · [Route maps, schedules and service changes](https://katbus.com/schedules-maps/)

If you are looking for the former downtown trolleys, use the **Downtown Connector** on the route page. If a disability prevents you from using fixed-route service, check the eligibility and reservation process for [The LIFT](../help/use-lift-paratransit.md).
