Knoxville tells businesses inside city limits to obtain both city and county licenses. **Check the sales threshold first.** Under Tennessee Code § 67-4-723(b)(2), a business with $3,000 or less in annual sales in the jurisdiction may choose a minimal activity license. That includes a business at exactly $3,000. For an otherwise taxable in-state business above that amount, the Department of Revenue explains when a minimal activity license or a standard license applies. Check exemptions and any separate occupational permits for your activity. [2013 Public Chapter 313, § 20](https://s3.amazonaws.com/publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/108/pub/pc0313.pdf) · [2023 Public Chapter 377, §§ 9 and 10](https://s3.amazonaws.com/publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/113/pub/pc0377.pdf) · [Revenue registration and licensing](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/business-tax/registration-and-licensing.html.html)

If the location needs both licenses, file the city application with Knoxville’s Business Tax Office and the county application with the Knox County Clerk. The city page mentions Room 450 in its introduction. Its contact block and the application both direct applicants to **400 Main Street, Suite 453**. Call the city at **865-215-2083** if you need help with the filing. The city’s checklist gives **865-215-2392** for the county office. [City license page](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/470/Business-License-Tax-Office) · [City application and filing checklist](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/442/Business-Tax-License-Application-PDF)

For a standard license, follow Revenue’s steps for tax registration through TNTAP. [State business tax guide](../../_state/services/register-for-business-tax.md)
