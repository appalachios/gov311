Read the issuer, any court date and the instructions printed on the citation. For a **KnoxPark** parking ticket, the city directs drivers to the KnoxPark portal or the QR code on the ticket for both payment and appeal. For a **City Court** citation, use the court’s payment instructions or visit **1650 Huron Street, Knoxville, TN 37917**. If a different court is named, use that court’s instructions. [Tickets, fines, appeals and payment routes](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/295/Tickets-Fines-Fees)

Some City Court citations require a personal appearance. If the officer marked **must appear**, follow the court date on the ticket. The city lists other cases that require attendance and explains when a payable citation should appear online. For a missing ticket, contact the office named on the ticket before its deadline. [City Court online payment and appearance rules](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/296/Submit-Online-Payment)
