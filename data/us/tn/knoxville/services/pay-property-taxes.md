Use the **City** search on Knoxville’s property tax page for the city bill. Check the parcel and tax year before paying. The city lists the Knox County Trustee’s search separately. For a parcel inside Knoxville, check both accounts. A receipt from one office covers only the payment to that office. If the city portal is unavailable, call the city Revenue Office at **865-215-2084** for payment options. For the county account, call the trustee at **865-215-2305**. [Knoxville tax contacts and portals](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/477/Property-Taxes) · [City tax search](https://propertytax.knoxvilletn.gov/) · [County tax search](https://propertytax.knoxcountytn.gov/)

If the problem is the **appraised value**, call the Knox County Property Assessor at **865-215-2360**. For the underlying ownership document, contact the Register of Deeds at **865-215-2330**. [Assessment and deed contacts](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/477/Property-Taxes)
