Call the City Recorder at **865-215-2075**. For a pending item, give the meeting date and agenda item or planning case number. Ask for the version being considered and any amendments. For a completed vote, ask for the approved minutes and numbered ordinance or resolution. [City Council agendas and minutes](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/1339/City-Council-Agenda-Minutes)

To speak, tell the recorder whether you want to address an agenda item or the Public Forum at the end of a regular meeting. Sign-ups open after the agenda is released. Check the city’s instructions for the cutoff on meeting day. To reach an elected member directly, use the city’s council directory.
