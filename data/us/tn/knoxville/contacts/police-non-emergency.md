For immediate danger, call **911**. If you cannot safely call, the department says you can text 911. For a Knoxville police matter that can wait, call **865-215-4010**. The city lists that number for recurring speeding or other non-emergency traffic complaints on city streets. [Knoxville Police Department](https://knoxvilletnpolice.gov/) · [City traffic safety contacts](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/434/Safety-Resources)

For paperwork after an incident, the city directory links separate requests for crash or incident reports, body camera or in-car video, and other police records. Have the incident number, date and location ready when available. Dispatch records have a separate custodian. [Knoxville records directory](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/320/Guide-to-Public-Records-Requests)
