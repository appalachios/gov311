The municipal directory lists **330 Colwyn Street, P.O. Box 78, Cumberland Gap, TN 37724** as the contact address for Cumberland Gap. Call **(423) 869-3860** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cumberland-gap) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofcumberlandgap.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cumberland-gap)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Claiborne County records](../../claiborne-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
