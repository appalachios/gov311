The municipal directory lists **City Hall, PO Box 546, Beersheba Springs, TN 37305** as the contact address for Beersheba Springs. Call **(931) 692-3107** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/beersheba-springs)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Grundy County records](../../grundy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
