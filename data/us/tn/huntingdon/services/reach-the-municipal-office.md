The municipal directory lists **19810 East Main St., P.O. Box 668, Huntingdon, TN 38344** as the contact address for Huntingdon. Call **(731) 986-2900** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntingdon)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntingdon)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Carroll County records](../../carroll-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Contact Public Works about town water, sewer or street services at 731-986-2911. [Public Works](https://www.huntingdontn.com/depts-and-services/public-works/)
