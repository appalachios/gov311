The municipal directory lists **223 Associates Blvd., Alcoa, TN 37701-1943** as the contact address for Alcoa. Call **(865) 380-4700** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/alcoa) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofalcoa-tn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/alcoa)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Blount County records](../../blount-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Applications, Procedures, and Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Applications, Procedures, and Permits](https://www.cityofalcoa-tn.gov/351/Applications-Procedures-and-Permits)
- **Stormwater Applications and Forms**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Stormwater Applications and Forms](https://www.cityofalcoa-tn.gov/564/Stormwater-Applications-and-Forms)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.cityofalcoa-tn.gov/689/Property-Tax)
