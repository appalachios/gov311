The municipal directory lists **360 Morrison Road, P.O. Box 27, Rossville, TN 38066** as the contact address for Rossville. Call **(901) 853-4681** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rossville) · [Municipal website](https://townofrossville.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rossville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
