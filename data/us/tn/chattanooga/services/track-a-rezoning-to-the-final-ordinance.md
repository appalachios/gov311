Open the council agenda for the week and check whether the item appears under first reading or final reading. Record the planning case number, parcel address and version. Use Council Minutes for the meeting where the vote occurred. An agenda can also preview later business for the same case. [Council agendas and minutes](https://chattanooga.gov/stay-informed/council-agendas-minutes)

For example, the October 20, 2026 revised agenda put case **2026-0106** on first reading. It proposed changing the zoning of 400, 402 and 405 Garden Road from residential to open space and urban agricultural zoning. The case appeared again under final reading on October 27. Page 5 of the minutes records approval as **Ordinance 14412**, subject to conditions. A council member also mentioned traffic cameras during the vote. To determine exactly what the ordinance requires, ask for Ordinance 14412 and its adopted conditions.
