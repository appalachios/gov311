If Chattanooga normally collects your garbage, first check the collection day. The city asks whether the cart was out by 7 a.m., clear of parked cars or other obstructions, and filled with accepted household garbage. Check whether neighbors were also missed. That helps Public Works distinguish a missed cart from a delayed route. [Garbage collection and missed pickups](https://chattanooga.gov/government/public-works/solid-waste-recycling/garbage-collection)

Use the CHA 311 Service Center or call **423-643-6311**. Give the pickup address, day and what happened. A broken cart can be reported through the same service. [CHA 311](https://chattanooga.gov/request-and-report/311-help-info)
