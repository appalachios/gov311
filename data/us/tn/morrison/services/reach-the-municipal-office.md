The municipal directory lists **130 W Maple St, Morrison, TN 37357** as the contact address for Morrison. Call **(931) 635-2363** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/morrison) · [Municipal website](https://www.morrisoncity.org/)
