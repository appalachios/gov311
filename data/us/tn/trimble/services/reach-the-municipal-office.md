The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 215, Trimble, TN 38259** as the contact address for Trimble. Call **(731) 297-3955** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/trimble)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/trimble)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Dyer, Obion. [Dyer County records](../../dyer-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Obion County records](../../obion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
