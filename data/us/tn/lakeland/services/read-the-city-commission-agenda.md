Open Lakeland’s **Agenda Center** and locate the Board of Commissioners by date. Check the meeting type and read the attached proposal. For a topic discussed in a work session, follow the item number or ordinance title to the later business meeting and find the motion and vote. For a recent change, ask City Hall at **901-867-2717** for the final ordinance and its effective date.
