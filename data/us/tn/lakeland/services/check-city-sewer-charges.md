Use Lakeland’s **Utility Rates** page. The residential sewer charge includes a minimum monthly bill and a charge based on volume. For a commercial account, use the meter-size row for the minimum charge and read the volume charge separately. Waste management appears as a separate fee. If the bill differs, ask the city at **901-867-2717** to confirm the billing period, customer class and meter size. [City utility rate tables](https://lakelandtn.gov/501/Utility-Rates)
