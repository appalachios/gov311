The municipal directory lists **10001 Highway 70, Lakeland, TN 38002** as the contact address for Lakeland. Call **(901) 867-2717** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lakeland) · [Municipal website](https://www.lakelandtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lakeland)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.lakelandtn.gov/198/Property-Tax-Information)
- **Apply For Permit Learn More...**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Apply For Permit Learn More...](https://www.lakelandtn.gov/120/Permits)
- **Trash, Recycling & Yard Services**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Trash, Recycling & Yard Services](https://www.lakelandtn.gov/141/Household-Trash-Recycling-and-Yard-Servi)
