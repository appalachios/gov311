The municipal directory lists **190 Three Way Lane, Three Way, TN 38343** as the contact address for Three Way. Call **(731) 784-7782** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/three-way) · [Municipal website](https://cityofthreeway.org/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://cityofthreeway.org/government/building-permits/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Madison County records](../../madison-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
