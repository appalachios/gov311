The municipal directory lists **535 Tennesee Ave South, Parsons, TN 38363** as the contact address for Parsons. Call **(731) 847-6358** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/parsons) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofparsons.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/parsons)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Decatur County records](../../decatur-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
