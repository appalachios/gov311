The municipal directory lists **136  S. Sycamore Street, Elizabethton, TN 37643** as the contact address for Elizabethton. Call **(423) 547-6200** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/elizabethton) · [Municipal website](https://www.elizabethton.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/elizabethton)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://ci-elizabethton-tn.smartgovcommunity.com/Public/Home)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Carter County records](../../carter-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
