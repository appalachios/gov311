To follow a proposal in Elizabethton, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Thursday at 6:00 PM at Municipal Building.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/elizabethton) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://www.elizabethton.org/government/city_council/agendas___minutes.php)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-elizabethton) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/elizabethton)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Under Tennessee Code § 6-32-202, council reads the caption at two meetings at least a week apart, and an ordinary ordinance takes effect ten days after adoption. An emergency ordinance stating the facts and reasons can take effect on adoption if a majority of council approves it on two successive days and public safety and welfare require it. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/elizabethton) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/2026-10/Modified_Manager_Council_Charter.pdf#page=20)
