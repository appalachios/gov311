The municipal directory lists **425 West Court Street; PO Box 1358, Dyersburg, TN 38025-1358** as the contact address for Dyersburg. Call **(731) 286-7604** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dyersburg)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Gas, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dyersburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Dyer County records](../../dyer-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
