The municipal directory lists **7218 Nolensville Road, Nolensville, TN 37135** as the contact address for Nolensville. Call **(615) 776-3633** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/nolensville) · [Municipal website](https://www.nolensvilletn.gov/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.nolensvilletn.gov/buildingcodes)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Williamson County records](../../williamson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Town Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [Town Recorder](https://www.nolensvilletn.gov/recorder)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.nolensvilletn.gov/form-adminrecords)
