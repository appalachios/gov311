The municipal directory lists **105 South Main Street, Goodlettsville, TN 37072** as the contact address for Goodlettsville. Call **(615) 851-2200** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/goodlettsville) · [Municipal website](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/goodlettsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Davidson, Sumner. [Davidson County records](../../nashville/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/526/City-Clerk)
- **Codes & Building Safety/Fire Marshal's Office**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Codes & Building Safety/Fire Marshal's Office](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/81/Codes-Building-SafetyFire-Marshals-Offic)
- **Solid Waste**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Solid Waste](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/186/Solid-Waste)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/647/City-Recorder)
- **Utility Billing Services**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing Services](https://www.goodlettsville.gov/721/Utility-Billing-Services)
