The municipal directory lists **3623 Louisville Road; PO Box 215, Louisville, TN 37777** as the contact address for Louisville. Call **(865) 681-1983** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/louisville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Blount County records](../../blount-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
