To follow a proposal in Watertown, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “3rd Tuesday at 6:00 PM at Community Center.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/watertown)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-watertown) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/watertown)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

The charter requires two readings by majority vote at different meetings. Check the mayor’s signature or the vote overriding a veto. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/watertown) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/watertown_cht.pdf#page=6)
