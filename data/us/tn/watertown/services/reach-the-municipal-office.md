The municipal directory lists **8630 Sparta Pike, Watertown, TN 37184** as the contact address for Watertown. Call **(615) 237-3326** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/watertown) · [Municipal website](https://watertowntn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/watertown)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Wilson County records](../../wilson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
