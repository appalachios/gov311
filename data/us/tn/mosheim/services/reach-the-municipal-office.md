The municipal directory lists **1000 Main Street, Mosheim, TN 37818** as the contact address for Mosheim. Call **(423) 422-4051** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mosheim) · [Municipal website](https://www.mosheim.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mosheim)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Greene County records](../../greene-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
