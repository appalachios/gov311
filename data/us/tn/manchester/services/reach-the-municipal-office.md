The municipal directory lists **200 West Fort Street, Manchester, TN 37355** as the contact address for Manchester. Call **(931) 728-4652** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/manchester) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofmanchestertn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/manchester)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.cityofmanchestertn.gov/departments/planning_and_codes/building_codes.php)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Coffee County records](../../coffee-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
