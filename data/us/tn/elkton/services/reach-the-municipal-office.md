The municipal directory lists **168 Main Street, P.O. Box 157, Elkton, TN 38455-0157** as the contact address for Elkton. Call **(931) 468-2506** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/elkton) · [Municipal website](https://www.elktontn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Wastewater**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/elkton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Giles County records](../../giles-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
