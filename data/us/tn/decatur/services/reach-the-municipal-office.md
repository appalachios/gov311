The municipal directory lists **116 North Main Street, Decatur, TN 37322** as the contact address for Decatur. Call **(423) 334-5716** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/decatur) · [Municipal website](https://www.decaturtn.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/decatur)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Meigs County records](../../meigs-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
