The municipal directory lists **12987 Hwy 79, P.O. Box 760, Bells, TN 38006** as the contact address for Bells. Call **(731) 663-2334** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bells)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bells)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Crockett County records](../../crockett-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
