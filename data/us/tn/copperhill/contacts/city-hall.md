Visit 160 Main Street, Copperhill, or call 423-496-5141. City Hall lists weekday hours of 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Mail goes to P.O. Box 640, Copperhill, TN 37317. For a signed ordinance, give the recorder its number or the date the board acted. [City Hall](https://copperhill.gov/city-hall/) · [City charter, § 2.06(b)](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/1993/05/Copperhill-Charter.pdf) · [Polk County city directory](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/)

For vehicle tags and registration or a marriage license, use the Polk County Clerk. [Polk County Clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/)

For a non-emergency sheriff’s call in Copperhill or the Copper Basin, call East Polk dispatch at **423-496-3301**. For an emergency, call **911**. The City Hall contact form is for city business and service requests. [City contact directory](https://copperhill.gov/contact/) · [Polk County 911 dispatch](https://polkcountytn911.com/)
