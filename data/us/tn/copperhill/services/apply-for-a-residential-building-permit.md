1. Confirm the parcel is inside Copperhill. The state Fire Marshal lists **Copperhill** as participating in the State Residential Building Program. The separate Polk County row applies to unincorporated land. [State jurisdiction list, under Polk County](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/fire/residential-permits/jurisdictions-inspectors.html)
2. Copperhill’s permits page covers city review of construction and site work. Give City Hall the address and plans and ask what municipal approvals the project requires. [City planning and permits](https://copperhill.gov/city-services/permits-and-licensing/)
3. Before starting construction on a new one- or two-family home or townhouse, use **Apply** on Tennessee’s residential building permit page or purchase the permit through a local issuing agent. The state bases the fee on estimated construction cost. Use **Request inspection** on the same page to arrange the state inspections. [Tennessee residential building permit and inspections](https://licenses.app.tn.gov/licenses/view?id=1503)
