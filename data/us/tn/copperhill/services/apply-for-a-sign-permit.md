Use the city’s sign permit form before installing a sign. It asks for the property address and zoning district, the type and dimensions of the sign, setbacks, mounting method and any lighting. The form cites Article 4.13 of the Copperhill zoning ordinance. [City sign permit](https://copperhill.gov/documents-and-permits-listing/sign-permit-application/)

Attach a site plan and a drawing with dimensions. Include structural details where applicable, electrical details for an illuminated sign, and owner authorization if someone else owns the property. Submit the application to the City Recorder at 160 Main Street or P.O. Box 640, Copperhill, TN 37317. Wait for the city to approve it before installation. [Application and document checklist](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/city-of-copperhill-sign-permit-application.pdf)
