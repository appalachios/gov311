Open **Meeting Agendas & Minutes**, select **Board of Mayor and Aldermen** for the month you need, then open **View / Download**. The cover gives the actual meeting date and time. For example, the November 2026 board packet announces Monday, November 23 at 5:30 p.m. Planning commission and beer board agendas have their own entries. [Meeting archive](https://copperhill.gov/city-hall/documents-and-permits/meeting-agendas-and-minutes/) · [November 2026 board packet](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/copperhill-tn-november-2026-meeting-informational-packet.pdf)

To see what passed, follow the ordinance number across packets. For example, the annual budget for the year ending June 30, 2027 was Ordinance **2026-05-0008**.

1. The May 18 agenda listed the first reading and included the proposed budget text. [May packet](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/copperhill-tn-may-2026-meeting-informational-packet-1.pdf)
2. The June 15 agenda listed the same number for second reading. [June packet](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/copperhill-tn-june-2026-meeting-informational-packet.pdf)
3. The July packet contains the June 15 minutes. Under **Old Business**, the recorded roll call shows all voted to accept the budget on second reading. [June 15 minutes in the July packet](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/copperhill-tn-july-2026-meeting-informational-packet.pdf)
