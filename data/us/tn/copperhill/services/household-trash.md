Take a government-issued ID and your lease or proof of purchase to City Hall to start service. Put up to **six tied bags** of household garbage at the curb by **7 a.m. Thursday**. When Thursday is a holiday, collection moves to the next business day. Leave sidewalks, driveways and the road clear. [City garbage pickup](https://copperhill.gov/city-services/garbage-pickup/)

Call 423-496-5141 for a missed pickup. Keep furniture, appliances, construction waste, paint and batteries out of the household bags. The city's pickup page gives the location of the Polk County recycling facility and lists other disposal options. [Collection rules and disposal contacts](https://copperhill.gov/city-services/garbage-pickup/)
