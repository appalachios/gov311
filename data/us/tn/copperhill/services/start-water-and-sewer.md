Confirm that Copperhill serves the address, especially if it has a Copperhill mailing address outside the city. The county directory lists the city water department and the Copper Basin Utility District separately. [County utility directory](https://polkcountytn.gov/services/)

Take a government-issued ID and your lease or proof of purchase to 160 Main Street. You can print the utility agreement before you go. Ask City Hall for the connection fee for your account type. The city’s water page also links to bill payment and gives 423-496-5141 for a broken pipe, sewer backup or low water pressure. [Water and sewer service](https://copperhill.gov/city-services/water-and-sewer/) · [Utility agreement](https://copperhill.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/copperhill-utilities-service-agreement.pdf)

To pay an existing account, select **Pay Water & Sewer Bill** on Copperhill’s Online Services page. It opens the Copperhill Utilities customer portal. You can also pay in person, by card over the phone, or at the drop box in front of City Hall. [City online services](https://copperhill.gov/city-hall/online-services/) · [Billing and payment options](https://copperhill.gov/city-services/water-and-sewer/)
