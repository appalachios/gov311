The municipal directory lists **13200 Minor Hill Highway, Minor Hill, TN 38473** as the contact address for Minor Hill. Call **(931) 565-3113** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/minor-hill)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/minor-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Giles County records](../../giles-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
