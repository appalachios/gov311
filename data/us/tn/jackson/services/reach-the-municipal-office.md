The municipal directory lists **121 E. Main Street, Suite 301 , Jackson, TN 38301** as the contact address for Jackson. Call **(731) 425-8240** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jackson)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jackson)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Madison County records](../../madison-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Contact the city’s 311 Call Center for service matters such as sanitation, stormwater, streets or street lighting. [Public Works](https://www.jacksontn.gov/government/publicworks)
