Open **Stratum**, the county assessor’s map, and select the parcel. You can see the parcel ID, lot dimensions and aerial photos. Open **Map Layers** to see city limits, wards, county districts or polling locations. For the assessed property record, use the assessor’s **Search/View Property Details**. [Open Stratum](https://gis.montgomerytn.gov/gisportal/apps/experiencebuilder/experience/?id=e4ddaf9dc44740e5b1dd5e9d3d3c81e1) · [Assessor’s map guide](https://montgomerytn.gov/assessor/gis) · [Property assessment search](https://property.spatialest.com/tn/montgomery)

- **Tax bills.** The County Trustee’s **Pay/View Taxes Online** lets you search by tax year and property address or owner. Inside Clarksville city limits, there is also a separate city property tax bill. [County tax lookup](https://montgomery-tn.mygovonline.com/mod.php?mod=propertytax&mode=public_lookup) · [County Trustee’s payment options](https://montgomerytn.gov/trustee/payments) · [Clarksville property tax](https://www.clarksvilletn.gov/173/Property-Tax)
- **Elected officials.** Use the Regional Planning Commission’s **Elected Official Lookup** for the county commission district, Clarksville ward where applicable, and state and federal representatives serving the address. [Elected Official Lookup](https://cmcrpc.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/lookup/index.html?appid=7dedd825fed642a2ad0ed23529abe9ff) · [Planning Commission maps](https://www.cmcrpc.com/maps/)
- **School attendance.** Use the Clarksville-Montgomery County School System’s address lookup for a student’s assigned school and bus information. A school board district tells you who represents the address on the board. [School and bus lookup](https://businfo.cmcss.net/) · [School system’s zoning instructions](https://www.cmcss.net/bus-zoning/)
