The municipal directory lists **136 Harrison Avenue, Graysville, TN 37338** as the contact address for Graysville. Call **(423) 775-9242** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/graysville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/graysville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rhea County records](../../rhea-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
