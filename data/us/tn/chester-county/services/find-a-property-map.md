Open the Chester County assessor’s **Citizen Access Portal** and choose **Property Search**. Select the tax year, then search by **Property Address**, **Name** or **Parcel #**. In the results, select the underlined parcel number. Open **Maps** to see the land and nearby parcels. Use **Property Card** for the assessment record or **Sales** for the parcel’s sale history. [Open the parcel portal](https://chester.capturecama.com/CAMA/CAPortal/CZ_MainPage.aspx) · [Chester County Assessor](https://www.chestercountytn.org/assessor_of_property.html)

For the county tax bill, select **Pay Taxes Online** on the trustee’s page. Use the same parcel and tax year when comparing the bill with the assessment. [Chester County Trustee](https://www.chestercountytn.org/trustee.html)

To find your county commissioner, open **District Maps** on the Election Commission site and locate your address. Match the district number to the county’s commissioner directory. For a child’s school, open **School Zone Map** on the school district’s Transportation page. [Election Commission maps](https://votechestercountytn.gov/) · [Commissioners by district](https://www.chestercountytn.org/county_commission.html) · [School zone map and transportation contacts](https://www.chestercountyschools.org/transportation)
