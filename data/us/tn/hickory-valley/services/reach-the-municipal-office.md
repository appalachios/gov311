The municipal directory lists **7115 Hwy 18 S  P.O. Box 39, Hickory Valley, TN 38042** as the contact address for Hickory Valley. Call **(731) 659-2514** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hickory-valley)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hardeman County records](../../hardeman-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
