The Hickory Valley charter (Private Acts of 1951, chapter 261, as amended) sets no general number of ordinance readings. One reading can suffice under Tennessee law when the charter is silent and no adopted local rule or subject-specific law requires more. Check the rule for the proposal before treating its first vote as final. [Compiled charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/hickoryvalley_cht.pdf#page=8) · [Tennessee ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [Tennessee Supreme Court, *Balsinger*, 435 S.W.2d 803, 805](https://law.justia.com/cases/tennessee/supreme-court/1968/435-s-w-2d-803-2.html)

Hickory Valley’s charter assigns the mayor responsibility for the meeting record. The mayor presides and the aldermen vote. Ask the mayor’s office for the meeting record and any board rule on readings. A search for “Hickory Valley” can also turn up the former taxing district in Hamilton County. Its bond provisions apply to that district. This town is in Hardeman County. [Municipal officials and records contact](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hickory-valley) [Different Hickory Valley taxing district, 1931 act](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/private-acts/private-acts-1931-chapter-689)

To follow a proposal in Hickory Valley, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Tuesday at 7:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hickory-valley)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)
