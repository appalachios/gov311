To follow a proposal in Hickory Valley, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Tuesday at 7:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hickory-valley)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hickory-valley) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/hickory-valley)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 8 makes the mayor responsible for keeping a record of the proceedings and transactions at each meeting of the Mayor and Board of Aldermen. Ask for the record showing the motion, any amendments and final action, along with any board rule setting the number of readings. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/hickory-valley) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/hickoryvalley_cht.pdf#page=8)
