The municipal directory lists **13085 N. Main Street, P.O. Box 909, Somerville, TN 38068** as the contact address for Somerville. Call **(901) 465-7305** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/somerville) · [Municipal website](https://www.somervilletn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/somerville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.somervilletn.org/forms.aspx?fid=554&catid=549)
- **Garbage Services**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage Services](https://www.somervilletn.org/pview.aspx?id=20725&catid=550)
- **Understanding My Sewer Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Understanding My Sewer Bill](https://www.somervilletn.org/pview.aspx?id=20721&catid=550)
