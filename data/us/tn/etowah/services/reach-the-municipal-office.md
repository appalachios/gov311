The municipal directory lists **701 Tennessee Avenue; PO Box 1077 , Etowah, TN 37331-1345** as the contact address for Etowah. Call **(423) 263-2202** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/etowah) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofetowahtn.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McMinn County records](../../mcminn-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
