To follow a proposal in Etowah, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “4th Monday at 6:30 PM at Etowah Community Center, 155 Robinson Street.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/etowah) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://www.cityofetowahtn.com/2179/Agendas-Minutes)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-etowah) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/etowah)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Ordinary ordinances receive two readings on separate days in open session, at least seven calendar days apart. The city can adopt a procedure for reading only the caption. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/etowah) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/charters/etowah-charter.pdf#page=11)
