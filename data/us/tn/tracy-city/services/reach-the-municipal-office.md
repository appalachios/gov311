The municipal directory lists **Town Hall 50 Main Street, Tracy City, TN 37387** as the contact address for Tracy City. Call **(931) 592-6213** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tracy-city)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tracy-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Grundy County records](../../grundy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
