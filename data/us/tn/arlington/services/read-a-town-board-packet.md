On the town website choose **View Public Meeting Agendas** and select **Board of Mayor & Aldermen**. Read the attachment for the meeting date and record the item or ordinance number. In **Town Agendas and Minutes**, look for the board’s motion and vote, including changes made at the meeting. [Town meeting records](https://www.townofarlington.org/)

For the rule already in force, use **Search Town Ordinances**. If a recent amendment has yet to appear there, choose **Submit an Open Records Request** and identify the ordinance, meeting date and final signed text you need. Town Hall can help locate the record at **901-867-2620**. [Ordinance search and records request](https://www.townofarlington.org/)
