The municipal directory lists **5854 Airline Road, PO BOX 507, Arlington, TN 38002** as the contact address for Arlington. Call **(901) 867-2620** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/arlington)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/arlington)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Town of Arlington services**. Choose Apply for a Permit, Submit an Open Records Request or See the Trash Collection Schedule from the town’s service menu. [Town of Arlington services](https://www.townofarlington.org/)
