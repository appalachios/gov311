Start with **Apply for a Permit** on the town website. Give Arlington the project address, proposed use and plans. The Memphis and Shelby County residential checklist says Arlington approves the plans and charges the fees, then forwards the county’s fee. The joint construction office issues the permit after town approval. Confirm the permit has been issued before scheduling construction.
