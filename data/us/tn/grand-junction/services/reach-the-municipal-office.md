The municipal directory lists **226 Highway 57 E, Grand Junction, TN 38039** as the contact address for Grand Junction. Call **(731) 764-2871** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/grand-junction) · [Municipal website](https://www.grandjunctiontn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/grand-junction)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.grandjunctiontn.org/pview.aspx?id=810&catid=977)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Fayette, Hardeman. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Hardeman County records](../../hardeman-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.grandjunctiontn.org/forms.aspx?fid=625&catid=980)
