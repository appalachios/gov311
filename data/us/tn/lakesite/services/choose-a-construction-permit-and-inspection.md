Open the **iWorQ** link on Lakesite’s Building & Codes Enforcement page and select the application for your project. The city official reviews applications and coordinates the contracted building inspectors. Call **423-842-2533** with the site address if you need to identify the correct permit or arrange the next inspection. [City building and codes office](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/government/departments/building_and_codes_enforcement.php) · [Lakesite permit portal](https://lakesitetn.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/lakesitetn)
