The municipal directory lists **9201 Rocky Point Road, Lakesite, TN 37379** as the contact address for Lakesite. Call **(423) 842-2533** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lakesite) · [Municipal website](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://lakesitetn.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/lakesitetn)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Garbage Service**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage Service](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/community/garbage_service/index.php)
- **Transient Vendor Permit**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Transient Vendor Permit](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/document__center.php)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/government/public_records_request.php)
- **Service Request**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Service Request](https://www.lakesitetn.gov/community/service_request_complaints.php)
