The municipal directory lists **746 Highway 163, P.O. Box 115, Calhoun, TN 37309** as the contact address for Calhoun. Call **(423) 336-2348** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/calhoun) · [Municipal website](https://www.calhounrivertown.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/calhoun)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McMinn County records](../../mcminn-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
