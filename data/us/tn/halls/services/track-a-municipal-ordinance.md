To follow a proposal in Halls, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Monday at 7:00 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/halls)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/city-code/halls) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/halls)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Halls Code § 1-104 requires a written ordinance and majority approval from members present at each of two meetings on different days. The minutes record the ayes and noes. Changing an adopted ordinance requires a new ordinance. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/halls) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/city-code/halls/title1/chapter1/section1-104)
