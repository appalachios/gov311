The municipal directory lists **700 North Garden Street, Columbia, TN 38401** as the contact address for Columbia. Call **(931) 560-1500** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/columbia) · [Municipal website](https://www.columbiatn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/columbia)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Maury County records](../../maury-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Pothole**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Pothole](https://www.columbiatn.gov/481/Streets-Maintenance)
- **Commercial Garbage Collection**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Commercial Garbage Collection](https://www.columbiatn.gov/826/Commercial-Garbage-Collection)
