The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 46, Linden, TN 37096** as the contact address for Linden. Call **(931) 589-2736** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/linden) · [Municipal website](https://www.townoflindentn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/linden)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Perry County records](../../perry-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

- **Water Department**. Find the city utility office and check who serves the address. [Water Department](https://www.townoflindentn.gov/water.html)
