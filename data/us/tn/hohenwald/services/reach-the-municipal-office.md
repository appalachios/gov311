The municipal directory lists **118 West Linden Avenue, Hohenwald, TN 38462** as the contact address for Hohenwald. Call **(931) 796-2231** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hohenwald)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hohenwald)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lewis County records](../../lewis-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utilities Department**. If water was disconnected for nonpayment, contact the utilities office to arrange payment before restoration. [Utilities Department](https://www.hohenwald.com/utilities.html)
