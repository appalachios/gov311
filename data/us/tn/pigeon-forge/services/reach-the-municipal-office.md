The municipal directory lists **3221 Rena Street, P.O. Box 1350, Pigeon Forge, TN 37868-1350** as the contact address for Pigeon Forge. Call **(865) 453-9061** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pigeon-forge) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofpigeonforgetn.gov)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pigeon-forge)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sevier County records](../../sevier-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Online Payments Utilities & Taxes**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Online Payments Utilities & Taxes](https://www.cityofpigeonforgetn.gov/251/Pay-Online)
- **Forms (Applications / Permits / Etc.)**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Forms (Applications / Permits / Etc.)](https://www.cityofpigeonforgetn.gov/228/Forms-Applications-Permits-Etc)
- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.cityofpigeonforgetn.gov/230/Sanitation)
