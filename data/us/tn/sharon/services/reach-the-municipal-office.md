The municipal directory lists **5320 Hwy 45 S, PO Box 235, Sharon, TN 38255** as the contact address for Sharon. Call **(731) 456-2122** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sharon)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sharon)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
