To follow a proposal in Sharon, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Monday at 6:30 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sharon)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-sharon) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/sharon)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Sharon Code § 1-401 requires a majority of the full council on two separate days at least seven calendar days apart. The caption is read each time, and the minutes name the members voting for and against. The ordinance takes effect on final passage unless it sets a later date. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/sharon) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/sharon-t1.pdf)
