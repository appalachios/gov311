The municipal directory lists **309 South College Street, Trenton, TN 38382** as the contact address for Trenton. Call **(731) 855-2013** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/trenton) · [Municipal website](https://www.trentontn.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/trenton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
