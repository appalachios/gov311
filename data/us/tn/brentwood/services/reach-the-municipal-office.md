The municipal directory lists **5211 Maryland Way, P.O. Box 788, Brentwood, TN 37024-0788** as the contact address for Brentwood. Call **(615) 371-0060** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brentwood)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brentwood)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Williamson County records](../../williamson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
