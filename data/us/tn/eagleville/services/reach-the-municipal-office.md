The municipal directory lists **108 South Main Street; PO Box 68, Eagleville, TN 37060** as the contact address for Eagleville. Call **(615) 274-2922** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/eagleville) · [Municipal website](https://www.eaglevilletn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/eagleville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rutherford County records](../../rutherford-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
