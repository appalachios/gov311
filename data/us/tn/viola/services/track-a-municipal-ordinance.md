The Viola charter (Private Acts of 1901, chapter 320, as amended) sets no general number of ordinance readings. One reading can suffice under Tennessee law when the charter is silent and no adopted local rule or subject-specific law requires more. Check the rule for the proposal before treating its first vote as final. [Compiled charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Viola_cht.pdf#page=5) · [Tennessee ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [Tennessee Supreme Court, *Balsinger*, 435 S.W.2d 803, 805](https://law.justia.com/cases/tennessee/supreme-court/1968/435-s-w-2d-803-2.html)

Under § 11 the mayor votes with the aldermen and has no veto. Ask the town recorder for the rules the board adopted for passing ordinances, any amendments, and the minutes showing the vote on the proposal. The UT directory lists the recorder and meeting place. [Municipal officials and records contact](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/viola)

To follow a proposal in Viola, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “Last Tuesday at 7:00 at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/viola)

For an older measure, the state archives lists Viola’s minutes from October 1931 through November 1989 on microfilm roll A-8945. [Warren County inventory, printed page 18](https://sostngovbuckets.s3.amazonaws.com/tsla/preservation/countymicro/warr.pdf)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)
