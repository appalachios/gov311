Use the Jackson County directory to reach the governing body or the office keeping its minutes. Give the meeting date and subject when asking for the agenda, proposed resolution and attachments. For regular meetings covered by Tennessee Code § 8-44-110, the agenda must be accessible to the public at least 48 hours beforehand. [County directory](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/jackson) · [Tennessee meeting requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

After the meeting, read the motion, amendments and vote in the minutes. Request the adopted text by number if the minutes refer to an attachment. For a claim about a county-specific power or office, look up the subject in the county’s collection of private acts and check the act’s effective-date and local approval provisions. [Jackson County private acts](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/private-acts/private-acts-1941-chapter-377) · [Public meeting and minute requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Use the county’s meeting page to find the posted records for the date you need. [County Commission Click here for county commission and meeting agendas and minutes.](https://jacksoncountytn.gov/government/commissioners/)
