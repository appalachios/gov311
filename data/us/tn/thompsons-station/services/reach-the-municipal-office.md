The municipal directory lists **1110 Fountain View Blvd, PO Box 100, Thompson's Station, TN 37179** as the contact address for Thompson's Station. Call **(615) 794-4333** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/thompsons-station) · [Municipal website](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Wastewater**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/thompsons-station)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/departments/community-development/building-and-codes)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Williamson County records](../../williamson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Taxes](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/property-taxes)
- **Town Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [Town Clerk](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/departments/town-clerk)
- **Wasterwater Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Wasterwater Billing](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/departments/finance/wastewater-billing)
- **Permits (opens in a new tab)**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits (opens in a new tab)](https://thompsons-station.gov/permits)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.thompsons-station.gov/departments/town-clerk/public-records-request)
