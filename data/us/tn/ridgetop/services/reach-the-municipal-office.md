The municipal directory lists **1730 Highway 41, P.O. Box 650, Ridgetop, TN 37152** as the contact address for Ridgetop. Call **(615) 859-0596** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ridgetop) · [Municipal website](https://ridgetoptn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ridgetop)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Davidson, Robertson. [Davidson County records](../../nashville/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
