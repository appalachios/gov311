Big Sandy’s payment page has a separate **Property Taxes** link below the water bill link. Use it for a town bill. For a Benton County bill, start at the county’s **Pay/Search Property Taxes** link. Compare the tax year and parcel on each notice. For a recorded deed, request a copy from the Benton County Register of Deeds.
