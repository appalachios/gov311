Open the town’s **Pay Your Water Bill** page and use its water payment link. Big Sandy says water bills are due on the 10th and lists the 17th as its shutoff date for unpaid accounts. For a past-due or disconnected account, call **731-593-3213** to confirm the balance and restoration arrangements. The town lists a 3:15 p.m. payment cutoff for same-day restoration. [Town water payments and shutoff instructions](https://bigsandytn.com/benton-county/pay-your-water-bill/)
