Start with the meeting posts on the town website. Open the dated attachment and identify the item you want to follow. To learn what passed, call the recorder through Town Hall at **731-593-3213**. Ask for the meeting minutes, the final text including amendments and the date the measure takes effect. UT’s municipal directory provides the charter and directs residents to the town for local code questions. [Town meeting posts](https://bigsandytn.com/benton-county/) · [Town Hall and charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/big-sandy)

The charter gives the town council powers in section 5. Section 9 requires a majority of council members for a quorum. Section 10 assigns the recorder the job of keeping a record of the proceedings. A notice that an ordinance was scheduled for discussion establishes the question before the council. Use the minutes to see the action taken. [Big Sandy charter, sections 5, 9 and 10](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/bigsandy_cht.pdf)
