The municipal directory lists **52 Graham Street, P.O. Box 300, White Bluff, TN 37187** as the contact address for White Bluff. Call **(615) 797-3131** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/white-bluff) · [Municipal website](https://townofwhitebluff.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/white-bluff)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Dickson County records](../../dickson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
