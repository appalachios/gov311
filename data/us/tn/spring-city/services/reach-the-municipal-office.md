The municipal directory lists **229 Front Street, Spring City, TN 37381** as the contact address for Spring City. Call **(423) 365-6441** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spring-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofspringcitytn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/spring-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rhea County records](../../rhea-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
