The municipal directory lists **2470 Cedar Street; PO Box 160, McKenzie, TN 38201** as the contact address for McKenzie. Call **(731) 352-2292** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mckenzie) · [Municipal website](https://mckenzietn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mckenzie)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Carroll, Henry, Weakley. [Carroll County records](../../carroll-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Henry County records](../../henry-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report an Issue**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report an Issue](https://mckenzietn.org/residents/report-an-issue/)
- **Business Licensing & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Business Licensing & Permits](https://mckenzietn.org/businesses/business-resources/business-licensing-permits/)
