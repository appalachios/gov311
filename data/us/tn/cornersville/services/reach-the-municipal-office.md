The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 128, Cornersville, TN 37047** as the contact address for Cornersville. Call **(931) 293-4482** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cornersville) · [Municipal website](https://www.cornersvilletn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cornersville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marshall County records](../../marshall-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
