To follow a proposal in Petersburg, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Tuesday following the 1st Monday of each month at 6:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/petersburg)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-petersburg) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/petersburg)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 11 requires a written ordinance and passage on two separate days. For an emergency, the board may consider it twice at one meeting. Every member must vote yes both times, and the ordinance must include the charter’s emergency statement. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/petersburg) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/PETERSBU_CHT.pdf#page=7)
