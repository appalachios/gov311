The municipal directory lists **120 Eastside Square, Petersburg, TN 37144-5237** as the contact address for Petersburg. Call **(931) 659-9826** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/petersburg) · [Municipal website](https://www.petersburgtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/petersburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Lincoln, Marshall. [Lincoln County records](../../lincoln-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Marshall County records](../../marshall-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
