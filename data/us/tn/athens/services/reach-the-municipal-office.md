The municipal directory lists **815 North Jackson Street, Athens, TN 37303** as the contact address for Athens. Call **(423) 744-2700** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/athens) · [Municipal website](https://cityofathenstn.com/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://cityofathenstn.com/building-codes/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McMinn County records](../../mcminn-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Sanitation Information**. Call Sanitation for a missed pickup or to replace a tote damaged during collection. [Sanitation Information](https://cityofathenstn.com/sanitation-information/)
