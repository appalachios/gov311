To follow a proposal in Athens, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “3rd Tuesday at 6:00 PM at Municipal Building.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/athens) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://cityofathenstn.com/agendas-minutes/)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-athens) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/athens)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

The council votes on two separate days, ordinarily at least 14 days apart. A recorded vote of a majority of the entire council can waive the interval. A resident or taxpayer can request that the ordinance be read in full before the final vote. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/athens) · [Governing text](https://cms9files.revize.com/athenstn/Document_Center/Team%20Athens/charter.pdf#page=17)
