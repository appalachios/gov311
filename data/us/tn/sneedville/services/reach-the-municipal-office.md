The municipal directory lists **292 New Jail Street, Sneedville, TN 37869** as the contact address for Sneedville. Call **(423) 733-2254** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sneedville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sneedville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hancock County records](../../hancock-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
