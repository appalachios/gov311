The municipal directory lists **97 S. Johnson Street, Alamo, TN 38001** as the contact address for Alamo. Call **(731) 696-4515** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/alamo) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofalamo.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/alamo)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Crockett County records](../../crockett-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
