The municipal directory lists **4839 Old Highway 63, Caryville, TN 37714** as the contact address for Caryville. Call **(423) 562-9478** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/caryville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/caryville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Campbell County records](../../campbell-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
