The municipal directory lists **132 West Main Street, Gallatin, TN 37066** as the contact address for Gallatin. Call **(615) 451-5895** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gallatin) · [Municipal website](https://www.gallatintn.gov/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.gallatintn.gov/168/Permits)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Permits](https://www.gallatintn.gov/168/Permits-Inspections)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.gallatintn.gov/474/Property-Tax)
- **Report a Problem**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Problem](https://www.gallatintn.gov/FormCenter/Engineering-4/Fix-It-Form-52)
- **Garbage Recycling Brush Collection**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage Recycling Brush Collection](https://www.gallatintn.gov/397/Garbage-Recycling-Brush-Collection)
