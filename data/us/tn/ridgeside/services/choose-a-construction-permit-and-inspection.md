For a construction project inside Ridgeside, use Hamilton County’s building permit forms and contact County Building Inspections at **423-209-7860**. The county’s municipal list identifies Ridgeside as district **2R**. For other city requests it lists Ridgeside at **423-622-2613**. Have the parcel address and scope of work ready so each office can identify its part. [County permits and municipal responsibilities](https://www.hamiltontn.gov/Department_BuildingInspections.aspx) · [Apply through Hamilton County](https://buildinginspection.hamiltontn.gov/) · [Ridgeside district contacts](https://buildinginspection.hamiltontn.gov/Municipalities.aspx)
