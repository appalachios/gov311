The municipal directory lists **314 Spring Street, P.O. Box 259, Carthage, TN 37030** as the contact address for Carthage. Call **(615) 735-1881** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/carthage) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofcarthagetn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/carthage)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Smith County records](../../smith-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits & Forms**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits & Forms](https://www.townofcarthagetn.gov/permitsandforms)
