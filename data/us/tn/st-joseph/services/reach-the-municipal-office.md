The municipal directory lists **202 S. Main Street , St. Joseph, TN 38481** as the contact address for St. Joseph. Call **(931) 845-4141** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/st-joseph)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/st-joseph)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lawrence County records](../../lawrence-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
