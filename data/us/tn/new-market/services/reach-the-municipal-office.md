The municipal directory lists **1024 Volunteer Street, P.O. Box 300, New Market, TN 37820** as the contact address for New Market. Call **(865) 475-3018** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/new-market)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Jefferson County records](../../jefferson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
