The municipal directory lists **210 South Church Street, Mountain City, TN 37683** as the contact address for Mountain City. Call **(423) 727-8005** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mountain-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.mountaincitytn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mountain-city)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.mountaincitytn.org/services/building_development_services.php)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Johnson County records](../../johnson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
