The municipal directory lists **PO Box 100, Estill Springs, TN 37330** as the contact address for Estill Springs. Call **(931) 649-5188** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/estill-springs) · [Municipal website](https://www.estillspringstn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/estill-springs)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.estillspringstn.gov/building-permits)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Franklin County records](../../franklin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Recycling**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Recycling](https://www.estillspringstn.gov/recycling)
