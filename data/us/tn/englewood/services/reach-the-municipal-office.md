The municipal directory lists **111 So. Niota Road, P.O. Box 150, Englewood, TN 37329** as the contact address for Englewood. Call **(423) 887-7224** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/englewood) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofenglewood.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/englewood)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McMinn County records](../../mcminn-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
