The municipal directory lists **45 East Broad Street, PO Box 998, Cookeville, TN 38501-0998** as the contact address for Cookeville. Call **(931) 520-5241** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cookeville) · [Municipal website](https://www.cookeville-tn.gov/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Putnam County records](../../putnam-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Services**. Find the building permit form, apply for utilities or check holiday trash pickup. [Services](https://www.cookeville-tn.gov/101/Services)
- **Report a Problem**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Problem](https://www.cookeville-tn.gov/RequestTracker.aspx)
- **Utility Payments**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Payments](https://www.cookeville-tn.gov/189/Utility-Payment-Options)
- **City Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [City Property Tax](https://cookeville-tn.gov/159/Property-Tax)
