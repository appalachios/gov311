The municipal directory lists **323 Long Street, New Johnsonville, TN 37134** as the contact address for New Johnsonville. Call **(931) 535-2715** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/new-johnsonville) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofnewjville.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/new-johnsonville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Humphreys County records](../../humphreys-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
