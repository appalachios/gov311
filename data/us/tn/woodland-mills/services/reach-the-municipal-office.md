The municipal directory lists **201 Cannon Moore Drive, P.O. Box 153, Woodland Mills, TN 38271-0153** as the contact address for Woodland Mills. Call **(731) 885-8754** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/woodland-mills) · [Municipal website](https://www.woodlandmillstn.net/)
