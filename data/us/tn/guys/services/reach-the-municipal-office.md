The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 122, Guys, TN 38339** as the contact address for Guys. Call **(731) 239-4700** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/guys)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
