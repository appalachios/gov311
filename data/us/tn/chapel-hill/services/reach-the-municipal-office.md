The municipal directory lists **4650 Nashville Hwy, PO Box 157, Chapel Hill, TN 37034** as the contact address for Chapel Hill. Call **(931) 364-7632** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/chapel-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofchapelhilltn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/chapel-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marshall County records](../../marshall-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
