The municipal directory lists **120 Gary Wade Blvd., Sevierville, TN 37862** as the contact address for Sevierville. Call **(865) 453-5504** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sevierville) · [Municipal website](https://www.seviervilletn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sevierville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sevier County records](../../sevier-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Water Bill Payment**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Water Bill Payment](https://www.seviervilletn.org/162/Water-Bill-Payment)
- **Open Records**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records](https://www.seviervilletn.org/175/Open-Records)
- **Report an Issue**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report an Issue](https://www.seviervilletn.org/237/Report-an-Issue)
- **Building Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Permits](https://www.seviervilletn.org/442/Building-Permits)
- **Property Tax Payment**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax Payment](https://www.seviervilletn.org/185/Property-Tax-Payment)
