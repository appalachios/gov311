The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 150, Huntsville, TN 37756** as the contact address for Huntsville. Call **(423) 663-3471** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntsville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Scott County records](../../scott-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Town services**. Use the town’s pickup instructions and contact the municipal building at 423-663-3471 to schedule a pickup. [Town services](https://townofhuntsville.com/government-2/services/)
