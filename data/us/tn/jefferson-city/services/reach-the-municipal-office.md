The municipal directory lists **112 City Center Drive, P.O. Box 530, Jefferson City, TN 37760** as the contact address for Jefferson City. Call **(865) 475-9071** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jefferson-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.jeffersoncitytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Recycling, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jefferson-city)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.jeffersoncitytn.gov/government/building_department/index.php#Building%20)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Jefferson County records](../../jefferson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.jeffersoncitytn.gov/government/city_recorder.php)
