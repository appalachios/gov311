The municipal directory lists **14075 Highway 57, Moscow, TN 38057** as the contact address for Moscow. Call **(901) 877-3584** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/moscow) · [Municipal website](https://cityofmoscowtn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/moscow)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
