The municipal directory lists **302 East Commercial Avenue, Monterey, TN 38574** as the contact address for Monterey. Call **(931) 839-3770** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/monterey) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofmontereytn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/monterey)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Putnam County records](../../putnam-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
