To follow a proposal in Monterey, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Monday at 7:00 PM at Monterey Municipal Building.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/monterey) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://montereytn.portal.civicclerk.com/)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-monterey) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/monterey)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Section 1-106 requires a written ordinance and three readings. The ordinance takes effect under its own terms, so check the effective date in the version passed on third reading. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/monterey) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/monterey_t-1.pdf#page=3)
