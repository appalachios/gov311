Start with the parcel. Tennessee’s certified municipal report lists Farragut in both Knox and Loudon counties. Use the municipal boundary map and the county assessment record to locate the land. If the parcel crosses the county line, give the legal description to both registers before recording. [Certified municipal report](https://tnecd.com/?force_download=129209) · [State boundary map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)

- **For the recorded deed, plat or deed of trust**, call the Loudon County Register of Deeds at **865-458-2605**. [Register of Deeds](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/register-of-deeds/)
- **For the parcel map or assessed value**, choose **Loudon** in the Tennessee Property Viewer and search by address, owner or parcel ID. For questions about the value, call the Loudon County Property Assessor at **865-458-2059**. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/) · [Property Assessor](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/property-assessor/)
- **For the county tax bill or payment**, call the Loudon County Trustee at **865-458-3103** or use the payment link on the trustee’s page. For town permits on a Farragut parcel, start with the Town of Farragut. [Trustee](https://loudoncounty-tn.gov/trustee/) · [Farragut](../../farragut/jurisdiction.yaml)
