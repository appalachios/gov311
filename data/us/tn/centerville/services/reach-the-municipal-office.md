The municipal directory lists **102 East Swan Street, P.O. Box 238, Centerville, TN 37033** as the contact address for Centerville. Call **(931) 729-4246** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/centerville) · [Municipal website](https://centervilletn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/centerville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hickman County records](../../hickman-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
