The municipal directory lists **106 East Kyle Street, P.O. Box 788, Rogersville, TN 37857** as the contact address for Rogersville. Call **(423) 272-7497** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rogersville) · [Municipal website](https://rogersvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rogersville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
