The municipal directory lists **196 Main Street, Lynchburg, TN 37352** as the contact address for Lynchburg. Call **(931) 759-7076** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lynchburg)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lynchburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Moore County records](../../moore-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
