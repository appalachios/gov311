To follow a proposal in Loudon, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “4th Monday at 6:30 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/loudon) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://www.cityofloudontn.org/government/agendas___minutes/index.php)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-loudon) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/loudon)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html) · [Municipal records requests](https://www.cityofloudontn.org/departments/city_recorder__finance/public_records_request_.php)

For an ordinary ordinance, the charter requires passage on two different days. Look for the final vote and check the measure for its effective date. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/loudon) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Loudon_CHT.pdf#page=12)
