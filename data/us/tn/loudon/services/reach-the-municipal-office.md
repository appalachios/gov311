The municipal directory lists **2480 Highway 72 N; PO Box 189, Loudon, TN 37774** as the contact address for Loudon. Call **(865) 458-2033** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/loudon) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofloudontn.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Loudon County records](../../loudon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
