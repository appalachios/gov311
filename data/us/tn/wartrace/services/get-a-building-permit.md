Open the building permit application. Show the lot boundaries and dimensions, the location and size of existing and proposed structures, and the existing and intended use. The town requires compliance with its zoning and subdivision rules and payment before issuance. Call Town Hall at **931-389-6144** if you need to confirm what to submit. [Town permit application and instructions](https://www.townofwartrace.com/buildingpermits)
