Choose Agendas on the town government page, then look for the Board of Mayor and Aldermen meeting and ordinance number. A committee or planning recommendation can precede a board vote. Section 7 of the charter requires a majority vote on three separate readings and dates. The minutes of the third reading must include the text if the ordinance passes. [Town agendas and governing bodies](https://www.townofwartrace.com/government) · [Adoption procedure at the front of the municipal code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/combined/Wartrace-code.pdf)

Ask Town Hall for the final ordinance and any mayoral veto or later board vote. Section 7 allows a veto override by a majority of the entire board and specifies when an unsigned ordinance can take effect. The online code may receive replacement pages later. [Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/wartrace)

The charter ordinarily requires two readings. A mayoral veto can trigger a third reading, where approval by majority vote makes the ordinance final.
