The municipal directory lists **1246 Louisville Highway, Millersville, TN 37072** as the contact address for Millersville. Call **(615) 859-0880** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/millersville) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofmillersville.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/millersville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Robertson, Sumner. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Problem**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Problem](https://www.cityofmillersville.com/egov/apps/services/index.egov?view=detail%3Bid=7)
- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://www.cityofmillersville.com/egov/apps/document/center.egov?eGov_searchType=32&eGov_searchSubmit=Search)
- **Utility Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing](https://www.cityofmillersville.com/department/division.php?structureid=46)
