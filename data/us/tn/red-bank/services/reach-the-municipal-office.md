The municipal directory lists **3105 Dayton Blvd, Chattanooga, TN 37415** as the contact address for Red Bank. Call **(423) 877-1103** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/red-bank) · [Municipal website](https://www.redbanktn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/red-bank)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://www.redbanktn.gov/291/Apply-For-A-Permit)
- **Solid Waste**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Solid Waste](https://www.redbanktn.gov/257/Solid-Waste)
