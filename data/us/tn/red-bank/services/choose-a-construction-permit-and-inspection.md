Use **Apply For A Permit** on the City of Red Bank website. If you are unsure which application fits, contact the city at **423-877-1103** with the site address and proposed work. Hamilton County’s inspection directory lists a county phone number beside Red Bank but specifically assigns all local permits to the city.
