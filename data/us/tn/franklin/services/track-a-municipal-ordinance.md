To follow a proposal in Franklin, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd and 4th Tuesday at 7:00 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/franklin)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-franklin) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/franklin)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Under charter article V, § 4, most ordinances must pass on two different days. Appropriations, taxes, zoning changes, annexations and de-annexations require passage on three different days and a majority of the entire board on the final vote. Look up the ordinance by number for the written proposal and the text that passed. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/franklin) · [Governing text](https://library.municode.com/tn/franklin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTICHREPRAC_SP1CH_ARTVBOACPR_S4ORAD)
