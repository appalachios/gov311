The municipal directory lists **334 Atoka Munford Avenue, Atoka, TN 38004** as the contact address for Atoka. Call **(901) 837-5300** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/atoka)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/atoka)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Tipton County records](../../tipton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Contact the town public works office about roads, sewers and stormwater infrastructure. [Public Works](https://www.atokatn.gov/page/public-works)
