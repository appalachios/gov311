The municipal directory lists **33 1st Street, PO Box 1699, Lexington, TN 38351** as the contact address for Lexington. Call **(731) 968-6657** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lexington) · [Municipal website](https://www.lexingtontn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lexington)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Henderson County records](../../henderson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Permit Application**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Permit Application](https://www.lexingtontn.gov/bldg_inspector.html)
