Find the dated meeting of the full Board of Commissioners. Use the committee record to see what was recommended, then look up the motion and vote at the full commission. For a zoning request, check the separate Planning Commission or Board of Zoning Appeals meeting and identify which body has the decision. [Commission meetings](https://www.bedfordcountytn.gov/government/boards_and_members/county_board_meetings/board_of_commissioners_meetings.php) · [Planning and zoning meetings](https://www.bedfordcountytn.gov/departments/planning___zoning/planning_commission_meetings.php)

To speak about an agenda item, sign up before the meeting is called to order. Identify the item and address the chair when recognized. The county limits comments to three minutes. [Public comment instructions](https://www.bedfordcountytn.gov/government/meetings/speak_at_a_meeting.php)

On June 30, 2026, the county reported separate votes on the new budget. The fund appropriations passed 17–0. The detailed line-item budgets passed 15–2. To check how your commissioner voted, ask for the roll call on the specific resolution. [County account of the budget votes](https://www.bedfordcountytn.gov/news_detail_T10_R61.php)
