For an unincorporated site, start with Bedford County Building Codes. Have the parcel details and project scope ready. The county issues permits to the owner or a licensed contractor. Keep your permit number for inspection requests and arrange the required inspections before work moves to the next phase. [County permits and inspections](https://www.bedfordcountytn.gov/departments/building_codes___permits/index.php)

For land inside a municipality, check its own entry in the state residential jurisdiction table. Normandy participates in the state program. Shelbyville, Bell Buckle and Wartrace have local enforcement routes. An exemption from the state program still leaves local requirements to check. [Jurisdiction list](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/fire/residential-permits/jurisdictions-inspectors.html) · [State application for Normandy](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/fire/residential-permits/fire-apply-for-a-residential-permit.html) · [Shelbyville permits](https://www.shelbyvilletn.org/departments/building___codes_department/index.php) · [Wartrace permits](https://www.townofwartrace.com/buildingpermits)
