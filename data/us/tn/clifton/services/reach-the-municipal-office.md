The municipal directory lists **142 Main Street, P.O. Box 192, Clifton, TN 38425** as the contact address for Clifton. Call **(931) 676-3370** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/clifton) · [Municipal website](https://cityofclifton.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/clifton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Wayne County records](../../wayne-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
