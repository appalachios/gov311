The municipal directory lists **300 E. Main Boulevard, P.O. Box 366, Church Hill, TN 37642** as the contact address for Church Hill. Call **(423) 357-6161** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/church-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.churchhilltn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/church-hill)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits](https://www.churchhilltn.gov/business_develop/permits/index.html)
- **Pay Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Pay Property Taxes](https://www.churchhilltn.gov/property_tax/index.html)
