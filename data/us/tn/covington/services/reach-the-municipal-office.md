The municipal directory lists **200 West Washington Ave., P.O. Box 768, Covington, TN 38019-0768** as the contact address for Covington. Call **(901) 476-9613** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/covington) · [Municipal website](https://www.covingtontn.gov/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Tipton County records](../../tipton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Taxes](https://www.covingtontn.gov/property-taxes)
- **Report Issues**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report Issues](https://www.covingtontn.gov/non-pii-forms/report-issues)
- **Utility Billing Division**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing Division](https://www.covingtontn.gov/utility-billing-division)
