The municipal directory lists **301 McHenry Circle, Livingston, TN 38570** as the contact address for Livingston. Call **(931) 823-1269** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/livingston) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityoflivingston.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/livingston)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Overton County records](../../overton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
