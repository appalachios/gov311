The municipal directory lists **Town Hall, 6530 Horton Highway, Baileyton, TN 37745** as the contact address for Baileyton. Call **(423) 234-6911** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/baileyton)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/baileyton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Greene County records](../../greene-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
