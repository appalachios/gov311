To follow a proposal in Slayden, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “Last Tuesday at 7:00 PM at Community Center.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/slayden)

Private Acts of 1913, chapter 346, as amended by Private Acts of 1990, chapter 149, gives Slayden three councilmembers and a mayor who presides. The charter sets no general reading count. [Compiled charter, §§ 3 and 7 and amendment table](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/SLAYDEN_CHT.pdf#page=2)

One reading can suffice when the charter is silent and no other law or adopted local rule requires more. UT lists no public municipal code for Slayden. Call the town recorder at **(615) 636-3496** for any adopted rule on ordinance readings, the proposed text and the minutes showing the council’s vote. [Tennessee Supreme Court decision](https://law.justia.com/cases/tennessee/supreme-court/1968/435-s-w-2d-803-2.html) · [UT guide to ordinance readings](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [Recorder’s contact information](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/slayden)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)
