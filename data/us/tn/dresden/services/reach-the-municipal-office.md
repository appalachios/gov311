The municipal directory lists **117 W Main St, Dresden, TN 38225** as the contact address for Dresden. Call **(731) 364-2270** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dresden) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofdresden.net/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
