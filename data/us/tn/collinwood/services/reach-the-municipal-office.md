The municipal directory lists **101 Third Avenue No, P.O. Box 98, Collinwood, TN 38450** as the contact address for Collinwood. Call **(931) 724-9107** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collinwood) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofcollinwood.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collinwood)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Wayne County records](../../wayne-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utilities Department**. Find the city utility office and check who serves the address. [Utilities Department](https://www.cityofcollinwood.org/index.php/government/utilities-department)
- **City Hall**. Use the office contact information to ask which municipal department handles the request. [City Hall](https://www.cityofcollinwood.org/index.php/government/city-hall)
