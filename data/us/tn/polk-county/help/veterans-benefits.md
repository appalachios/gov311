Call **423-338-4557** and describe the benefit, letter or referral you need help with. The Polk County Veterans Service Office is in Room 207 of the courthouse at 6239 Highway 411 in Benton. Ask which documents are needed before bringing personal records. The county office also publishes contact details for VA clinics and regional services. [County Veterans Service Office](https://polkcountytn.gov/veterans-services/)
