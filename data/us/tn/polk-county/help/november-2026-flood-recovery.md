For immediate danger, call **911**. For local recovery services and current hours, use the county’s [Copperhill flood recovery page](https://polkcountytn.gov/flood-recovery/).

- **To report damage**, use the form on the county page or call Polk Emergency Management at **423-338-1090**. The county uses these reports to document losses. You still need to apply separately for financial assistance.
- **For eligible health and safety costs at a primary home**, use TEMA’s *Individuals and Households* application. Small businesses have separate applications for the initial stabilization grant and additional expenses. Open the table for the November 5 flood and check the window for your application. [Governor’s Response and Recovery Fund](https://www.tn.gov/tema/ready-communities/grants/governor-s-response-and-recovery-fund.html)
- **For a federal disaster loan**, check the Small Business Administration instructions even if you are a homeowner or renter. The SBA and TEMA run different programs. The county posts the SBA fact sheet and local assistance hours. [Polk County’s SBA instructions](https://polkcountytn.gov/flood-recovery/) · [SBA disaster loan portal](https://lending.sba.gov/)

These applications are tied to this flood. For another event or a later inquiry, check the state’s current disaster page before using a dated application or visiting a temporary assistance center. [TEMA’s Polk flooding page](https://www.tn.gov/tema/assistance/active-disasters-and-emergencies/2026-disasters/polk-county-flooding.html)
