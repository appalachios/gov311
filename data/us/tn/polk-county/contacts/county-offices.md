Call the County Executive’s Office at **423-338-4527** if you need help finding a county department. The courthouse is at 6239 Highway 411 in Benton. For vehicle tags and licenses, go directly to the [County Clerk](county-clerk.md).

Benton, Copperhill and Ducktown are the county’s incorporated municipalities. If your question is about construction, give the office the parcel address and ask whether it is inside city limits. Someone with a Benton mailing address might need either the city permit desk or the county assessor. The county also keeps land records and collects county property taxes for parcels inside the cities.
