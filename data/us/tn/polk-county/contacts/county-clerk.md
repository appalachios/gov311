For vehicle titles and tags, marriage licenses or a county business license, call **423-338-4526** in Benton or **423-496-3342** at the Copper Basin branch, 840 Cherokee Trail, Copperhill. The branches keep different hours. Check the day and service before you travel. For tag renewal, follow *Tennessee County Clerk Online Services* from the [county clerk’s page](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/).

Bringing a car from another state? Use the state’s checklist for documents, including what to do when the lender holds the title. [New resident title and registration](../../_state/services/register-vehicle-after-moving.md)

The County Clerk keeps the County Commission’s proceedings. For a lawsuit, court date or probate file, use the [court clerks](court-clerks.md).
