Call the office you plan to visit and say which service you need. In **Benton**, call **423-338-4533** at 2279 Parksville Road. For **Copperhill**, call **423-496-3275** at 840 Cherokee Trail. The county health page lists hours, WIC contacts and other family assistance referrals. [Polk County Health Department](https://polkcountytn.gov/health-department/)

For food benefits, the programs take different applications. Use the [WIC appointment guide](../../_state/services/make-a-wic-appointment.md) for WIC and the [state SNAP guide](../../_state/services/apply-for-snap.md) for a SNAP application.
