For an emergency, call **911**. To report a crime or an ongoing problem to the Polk County Sheriff when there is no immediate danger, the department lists **423-338-8215**. For its offices, call **423-338-4540** in Benton or **423-496-3301** in the Copper Basin. The Benton office is at 164 Industrial Access Circle. [Polk County Sheriff](https://polkcountytn.gov/sheriff/)
