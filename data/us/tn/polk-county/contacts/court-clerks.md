Read the court name on the citation, summons or notice. Two Polk County offices on Industrial Access Circle in Benton have different street numbers.

- **Circuit, Criminal, General Sessions, Juvenile, Child Support, county traffic cases and jury service.** Call the Clerk of Court at **423-338-4524**, **164** Industrial Access Circle. Have the case number or citation handy. If the ticket names a city court, follow the instructions on the ticket. [Clerk of Court](https://polkcountytn.gov/legal-courts/clerk-of-court/)
- **Chancery and probate**, including estate records and court proceedings for delinquent property taxes. Call the Clerk and Master at **423-338-4522**, **161** Industrial Access Circle, Room 110. [Chancery Court](https://polkcountytn.gov/legal-courts/chancery-court/)

For a marriage license, car title or county commission record, use the [County Clerk](county-clerk.md) at the courthouse on Highway 411.
