Start with the parcel and the planning boundary. The county regulations define the Polk County Planning Region as county land outside incorporated municipalities **and their planning regions**, if any. If the property is near a city, ask the planner which commission will review it before commissioning a plat. [Subdivision regulations, definitions and Article 4](https://www.sedev.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Polk-County-Official-Subdivision-Regulations-December-2024.pdf)

The Southeast Tennessee Development District posts the Polk planning contact, meeting agendas, interactive map, flood resolution and subdivision regulations together. Describe the proposed split or use. A campground or mobile home park has its own regulations. If flooding is a concern, locate the site on the linked FEMA map and ask which local approval applies. [Polk planning desk](https://www.sedev.org/polk/)

For a subdivision subject to commission approval, follow Articles 10 through 14 for the preliminary and final plat. Take the approved plat to the Register of Deeds for recording. A recorded deed for the parent parcel does not establish approval for a new subdivision. [Register of Deeds](https://polkcountytn.gov/register-of-deeds-2/)
