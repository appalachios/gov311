A private act is a state law for a particular place. Start with the [Polk County compilation](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/private-acts/Polk) for an existing law. Its update date matters. Take the chapter number and year and check for later amendments or repeal.

For a pending change, search the [General Assembly’s bill records](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillSearch/BillSearchAdvanced) for Polk County in the relevant session. Read both companion histories, the amendments actually adopted and the enrolled text. A broad caption can stay the same while the proposal underneath changes. [How to read a Tennessee caption bill](../../_state/services/read-a-caption-bill.md)

Read the last sections of the enacted private chapter. They say when it takes effect and which local approval is required. Depending on the act, that can mean a two-thirds vote of the county legislative body or a majority of voters voting in a local referendum. For a commission vote, get the minutes and approval record from the County Clerk. For a referendum, get the certified result from the Polk County Election Commission. A vote requesting the state bill can precede the legislature’s work. Check for the later vote accepting the enacted text.
