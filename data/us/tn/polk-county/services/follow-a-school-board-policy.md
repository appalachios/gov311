Use the [district’s Board of Education page](https://www.polk-schools.com/board-information). Open the board meeting information folder for the school year, then find the meeting schedule, agenda and minutes by date. For a proposal, ask for the marked-up policy or attachment if the agenda only gives a policy number. The district office is at **423-299-0471**.

After the meeting, check the motion and vote in the minutes. Then open the school board’s policy manual and search the policy number for its current wording. Section 1 includes separate policies for agendas (1.403), minutes (1.406) and policy development and adoption (1.600). If the minutes show a recent change and the published copy is older, ask the district for the adopted text and its effective date.
