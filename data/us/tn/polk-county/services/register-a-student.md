Call the school office to start registration. If you need help finding the right school, call the district at **423-299-0471**. Review the district list for proof of age, evidence of immunization and documentation of a physical exam for students entering school for the first time. [Student registration and admissions policies](https://www.polk-schools.com/registration-information)

If a listed document is missing, contact the school now. The district says it will work with families to obtain those records and will proceed with enrollment when the documents are not immediately available. The registration page lists contacts for elementary, secondary and pre-K questions. [Polk County Schools](https://www.polk-schools.com/registration-information)
