1. Start with the business location, the activity and taxable gross receipts. Tennessee Revenue explains who needs a minimal activity license, who needs a standard license and which businesses are exempt. A business operating in a city that imposes business tax may need both a county and a city license. [Business license overview](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360057134532-BUS-13-Business-Licenses-Overview)
2. For the county filing, call the Polk County Clerk at **423-338-4526** and describe the location. If it is in Benton, Copperhill or Ducktown, check with that city as well. [County Clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/) · [City offices](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/)
3. If a standard license applies, use Revenue’s instructions for registering the business or adding a location in the Tennessee Taxpayer Access Point. If you already formed an LLC with the Secretary of State, you still need to check the tax and local licensing steps. [Register for Tennessee business tax](../../_state/services/register-for-business-tax.md)
