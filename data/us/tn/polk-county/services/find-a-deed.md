Call the Register of Deeds at **423-338-4537** or visit Room 211, 6239 Highway 411 in Benton. If you have the book and page or instrument number, give that to the office. If you only know the street address, start with the assessor’s parcel lookup to identify the parcel and current assessed owner. For the recorded document, use the register’s index. [Register of Deeds](https://polkcountytn.gov/register-of-deeds-2/) · [Assessor’s property search](https://polkcountytn.gov/property-assessor/)

For a remote copy, use *Search Online Property Records* on the register’s page. The linked service charges by image or subscription. Ask the office directly if you need a certified copy. If you are recording a new document, open *Requirements to Record Documents*. A subdivision plat may first need [planning commission approval](../codes/subdivision-and-floodplain.md).
