**To check the parcel or challenge the value**, start with the county assessor at **423-338-4505**, Room 209 in the Benton courthouse. Use the assessor’s *Property Search*, choose Polk County and match the parcel to your notice. If you want to appeal, check the county Board of Equalization schedule as soon as the notice arrives. [Assessor and local appeal information](https://polkcountytn.gov/property-assessor/) · [State appeal guide](../../_state/services/appeal-property-assessment.md)

**To pay county property tax or get a receipt**, use *View or Pay Property Taxes* on the trustee’s page or call **423-338-4545**. The trustee lists Benton and Copper Basin office hours. If you have a separate city bill, use the office shown on that bill. [Polk County Trustee](https://polkcountytn.gov/trustee/)

**If the account went to court for delinquent taxes**, the Clerk and Master handles court collections. Call **423-338-4522** with the parcel and tax year. [Chancery Court](https://polkcountytn.gov/legal-courts/chancery-court/)
