Start with the [Polk County Election Commission](https://www.votepolktn.com/). Under *Voters*, choose *Registration/Address Change* if you moved, or *Address Lookup* to identify the districts at your residence. Under *Election Information*, check the precincts, dates and times for the election you plan to vote in. Early voting can take place somewhere other than your Election Day precinct.

For a city race or referendum, open [Notices and Sample Ballots](https://www.votepolktn.com/notices-and-sample-ballots) and match the jurisdiction and election date. After the vote, use *Election Results* and check whether the result you are reading is certified. If the address lookup leaves you unsure which ballot applies, call **423-338-4531**. The office is at 6050 Highway 411 in Benton. [Election office contact](https://www.votepolktn.com/contact-us)
