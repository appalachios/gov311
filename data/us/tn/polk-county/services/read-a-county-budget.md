Give Accounts and Budgets the fiscal year and fund you want. Polk’s year runs from July through the following June. Ask for the adopted budget and amendments if you need the amount the county authorized. The director compiles the budget for submission to the county legislative body. The school system and Road Department keep separate accounts. [Accounts and Budgets, 423-338-4527](https://polkcountytn.gov/accounts-budgets/)

Check the [commission packets and minutes](https://polkcountytn.gov/agendas-minutes-2/) for the specific motion. For example, the August 2026 minutes record that commissioners received a final school budget with an amended column missing from their first copy. They approved the version presented at the meeting. [August minutes, item 13](https://polkcountytn.gov/wp-content/uploads/commission-meetings/minutes/minutes-2026-08.pdf#page=3)

To see how the county accounted for the money after the year closed, follow *View County Audit Reports* from Accounts and Budgets and select Polk County and the fiscal year.
