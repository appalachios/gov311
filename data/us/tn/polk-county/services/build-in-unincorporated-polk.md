1. Give the assessor the parcel address and describe the project. For construction outside municipal limits, apply **in person** in Room 209 of the Benton courthouse. The assessor also issues county mobile home and RV permits. Inside Benton, Copperhill or Ducktown, contact the city about its permits. [County building permit instructions](https://polkcountytn.gov/property-assessor/) · [City offices](https://polkcountytn.gov/city-offices/)
2. The State Fire Marshal lists Polk County as **OPT OUT** for the state residential building permit program. That designation concerns state residential code enforcement for one- and two-family dwellings. An owner can request a state inspection under Tennessee Code § 68-120-101(b)(1)(D). Polk County still requires its own permit for construction outside city limits. [State law on owner-requested inspections](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=HB0539&GA=110) · [State jurisdiction table](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/fire/residential-permits/jurisdictions-inspectors.html) · [State guidance for opt-out areas](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/fire/residential-permits/opt-out-jurisdictions.html)
3. If the project needs a septic system, use the Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation’s [septic service application](https://tdec.tn.gov/septic-service-request/). For a new electric meter, the assessor directs applicants to Polk County 911 addressing at **423-338-1090** and links the separate electrical permit service. For a lot split or possible floodplain work, check the [planning commission’s map and regulations](https://www.sedev.org/polk/).
