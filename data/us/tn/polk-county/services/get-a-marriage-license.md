Call the County Clerk in Benton (**423-338-4526**) or at the Copper Basin branch in Copperhill (**423-496-3342**). Check the branch hours, identification, fee and the certificate to bring if you completed qualifying premarital counseling. Most applicants appear in person. If incarceration or a disability prevents an applicant from appearing, Tennessee Code § 36-3-104(a) provides a notarized-statement route. Subsection (b) sets separate requirements for a qualifying military deployment abroad. Call before arranging the paperwork. [Polk County Clerk](https://polkcountytn.gov/county-clerk/) · [State application law, § 36-3-104](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/TNCodeBills/2025/TNCodeBill_Volume1_2025.pdf#page=540)

**If an applicant is 17**, the county page says both people must be 18. Tennessee Code §§ 36-3-105 and 36-3-106 allow a 17-year-old to apply if the other applicant is less than four years older and the applicable consent requirements are met. An age gap of exactly four years rules out issuance when the younger applicant is 17. No one under 17 can receive a license. Ask the clerk which proof of age, custody, consent or prior emancipation applies. The statute permits a person giving consent to join in person or by sworn, notarized affidavit, and it addresses emancipation separately. [2018 Public Chapter 1049, sections 3–4](https://s3.amazonaws.com/publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/110/pub/pc1049.pdf) · [UT county clerk guidance and affidavit](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/eli/minimum-age-applicants)

Older instructions can mention a three-day wait for a minor. Tennessee repealed that provision effective July 1, 2019. [2019 Public Chapter 93, sections 3–4](https://s3.amazonaws.com/publications.tnsosfiles.com/acts/111/pub/pc0093.pdf)

**How a reader could miss the age rule.** As filed, House Bill 2134 changed “days” to “calendar days” in a different statute. House Amendment 1 replaced that provision with child-support language. House Amendment 2 added the marriage provisions. Compare the one-page original with sections 3 and 4 of the signed act.
