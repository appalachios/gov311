Choose the location before loading up. The county lists six convenience centers: Benton, Farner, Grassy Creek, Linsdale, Reliance and South Polk. Each has its own schedule. For the Copperhill area, the county lists Grassy Creek at **2000 Grassy Creek Road**. [Addresses, hours and directions](https://polkcountytn.gov/sanitation-2/)

The centers accept household trash generated in Polk County. The county uses Polk vehicle registration or authorization from the County Executive to establish where the trash came from. If you have another county’s plates or a specialty tag, check the permit, specialty-tag and renewal applications on the sanitation page. Call **423-338-4527** before hauling an unusual item. For curbside pickup within a city, check that city’s sanitation service. [Convenience center applications](https://polkcountytn.gov/sanitation-2/)
