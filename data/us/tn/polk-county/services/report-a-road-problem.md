On a county-maintained road, call the Road Department at **423-338-4177** for West Polk or **423-496-2456** for East Polk. Give the road name, nearest intersection or address, and what a crew will find. The county handles pavement, ditches, culverts and road signs on its road system. [Polk County Highway Department](https://polkcountytn.gov/highway-department/)

For a state highway, submit a [Tennessee Department of Transportation maintenance request](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/maintenance-request.html). For a city-maintained street, start at city hall. If you cannot tell who maintains the road, give the road office the location and ask it to identify the owner. For immediate danger, call **911**.
