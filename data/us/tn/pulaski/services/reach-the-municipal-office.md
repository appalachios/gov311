The municipal directory lists **203 South First Street, P.O. Box 633, Pulaski, TN 38478-0633** as the contact address for Pulaski. Call **(931) 363-2516** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pulaski) · [Municipal website](https://www.pulaski-tn.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Giles County records](../../giles-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Garbage**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage](https://www.pulaski-tn.com/garbage)
