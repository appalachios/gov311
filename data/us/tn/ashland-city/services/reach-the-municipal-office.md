The municipal directory lists **405 N. Main Street, Ashland City, TN 37015** as the contact address for Ashland City. Call **(615) 792-4211** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ashland-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.ashlandcitytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ashland-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cheatham County records](../../cheatham-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Codes, Planning and Zoning**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Codes, Planning and Zoning](https://www.ashlandcitytn.gov/building)
- **Pay/View Utility Bill & Property Taxes**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Pay/View Utility Bill & Property Taxes](https://ashlandtn.municipalonlinepayments.com:443/ashlandtn)
