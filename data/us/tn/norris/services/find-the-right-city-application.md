For construction, find **Permit Forms** and choose the building or permanent sign application. For a variance, home occupation or change of zoning, use the separate **BZA** section and describe the existing parcel and proposed use. A building application alone does not change a parcel’s zoning.

To start or stop municipal utility service use **Utility Forms**. To inspect a city record, use the city records request under **Public Records/FOIA Request** and specify the document and dates. [Norris forms by service](https://www.cityofnorris.com/forms.html)
