The municipal directory lists **PO Box 7888, Saltillo, TN 38370** as the contact address for Saltillo. Call **(731) 325-7002** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/saltillo)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/saltillo)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hardin County records](../../hardin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
