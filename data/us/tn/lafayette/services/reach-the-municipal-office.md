The municipal directory lists **200 E.Locust Street, Lafayette, TN 37083** as the contact address for Lafayette. Call **(615) 666-2194** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lafayette) · [Municipal website](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lafayette)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Macon County records](../../macon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/departments/property_tax/index.php)
- **Trash Collection**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Trash Collection](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/departments/trash_collection.php)
- **Building Inspections**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspections](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/services/_building_inspections.php)
- **Make a Utility Payment Over The Phone or Online**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Make a Utility Payment Over The Phone or Online](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/i_want_to/pay/make_a_utility_payment_over_the_phone_or_online.php)
- **Establish New Utility Service**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Establish New Utility Service](https://www.lafayette-tn.org/i_want_to/find_info/establish_new_utility_service.php)
