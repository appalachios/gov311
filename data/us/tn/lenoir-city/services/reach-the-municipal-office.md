The municipal directory lists **530 Highway 321 N., Lenoir City, TN 37771** as the contact address for Lenoir City. Call **(865) 986-2715** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lenoir-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.lenoircitytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lenoir-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Loudon County records](../../loudon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Inspection & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspection & Permits](https://www.lenoircitytn.gov/building-inspection-permits/)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.lenoircitytn.gov/property-tax/)
