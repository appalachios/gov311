The municipal directory lists **7 South High Street, Winchester, TN 37398** as the contact address for Winchester. Call **(931) 967-2532** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/winchester)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/winchester)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Franklin County records](../../franklin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
