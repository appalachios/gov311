The municipal directory lists **7100 City Center Way, P.O. Box 69, Fairview, TN 37062** as the contact address for Fairview. Call **(615) 799-2484** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/fairview) · [Municipal website](https://www.fairview-tn.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Williamson County records](../../williamson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report Concern](https://www.fairview-tn.org/city/community-comments/)
- **Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Taxes](https://www.fairview-tn.org/depts-services/property-tax-office/)
