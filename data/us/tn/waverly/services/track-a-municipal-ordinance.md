To follow a proposal in Waverly, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Monday at 7:00 PM at City Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/waverly)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-waverly) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/waverly)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 31 requires a majority of the aldermen on two readings at separate meetings. The minutes record each member’s vote on each reading. The mayor can veto part of an ordinance, so check the written objections and whether two-thirds of the full board voted to override. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/waverly) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/waverly_cht.pdf#page=22)
