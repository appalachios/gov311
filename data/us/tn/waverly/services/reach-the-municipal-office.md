The municipal directory lists **101 East Main Street, P.O. Box 70, Waverly, TN 37185** as the contact address for Waverly. Call **(931) 296-2101** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/waverly) · [Municipal website](https://www.waverlytn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/waverly)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Humphreys County records](../../humphreys-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
