The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 100, Dowelltown, TN 37059** as the contact address for Dowelltown. Call **(615) 536-5997** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dowelltown)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dowelltown)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [DeKalb County records](../../dekalb-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
