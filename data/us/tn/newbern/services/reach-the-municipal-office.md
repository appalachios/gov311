The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 460, Newbern, TN 38059** as the contact address for Newbern. Call **(731) 627-3221** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/newbern) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofnewbern.org/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.cityofnewbern.org/government/building-codes)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Dyer County records](../../dyer-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
