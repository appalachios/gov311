To follow a proposal in Newbern, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “3rd Tuesday at 7:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/newbern)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-newbern) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/newbern)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

The board must have a written ordinance before consideration. A majority of members present must approve it at each of two meetings on different days, and the minutes record the votes. A later change to the adopted text requires a new ordinance. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/newbern) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/newbern-t1.pdf#page=2)
