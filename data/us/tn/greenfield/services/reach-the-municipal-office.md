The municipal directory lists **302 North Front Street, Greenfield, TN 38230** as the contact address for Greenfield. Call **(731) 235-2330** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greenfield)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greenfield)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Find collection routes and the schedule for bulk and brush pickup, or pay municipal water, sewer and garbage bills at City Hall. [Public Works](https://www.greenfieldtn.org/public-works.html)
