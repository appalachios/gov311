The municipal directory lists **6 Liberty Square, PO Box 30, Sparta, TN 38583** as the contact address for Sparta. Call **(931) 836-3248** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sparta) · [Municipal website](https://www.spartatn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sparta)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [White County records](../../white-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

- **Utilities**. Find the city utility office and check who serves the address. [Utilities](https://www.spartatn.gov/utilities)
