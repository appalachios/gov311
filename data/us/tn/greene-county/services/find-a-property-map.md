Open the **Tennessee Property Viewer** and choose **Greene**. Search by address, owner name or parcel ID. Select a result to see the parcel outline and open **Property Assessment Data** for the assessor’s record. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/) · [County GIS](https://www.greenecountytngov.com/geographic-information-systems-gis/)

**Tax bills.** For the amount due or a payment record, give the parcel ID and tax year to the Greene County Trustee at **(423) 798-1705**. [County office directory](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/greene) · [County tax lookup](https://citisenportal.com/Search/Greene%20County%20Trustee)

**Who represents this address?** Enter your residential address in the Comptroller’s voter district lookup. Use the local district number and the county directory to find your elected officials. [Voter district lookup](https://tn-cot.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/lookup/index.html?appid=2bd3c9b349634d2ba7153410c0930d27) · [Greene County officials](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/greene)

**Schools.** The state lists several districts here, including Greene County Schools, Greeneville City Schools. Use the school directory for contact details. Give the district the home address, grade and school year to confirm the assigned school. [Tennessee School Directory](https://tnschooldirectory.tnedu.gov/)
