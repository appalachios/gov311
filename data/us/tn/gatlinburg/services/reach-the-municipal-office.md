The municipal directory lists **1230 East Parkway, Gatlinburg, TN 37738** as the contact address for Gatlinburg. Call **(865) 963-9526** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gatlinburg)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gatlinburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sevier County records](../../sevier-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Finance and customer service**. Use the Customer Service Center at 912 East Parkway for city utility, license or permit payments. [Finance and customer service](https://www.gatlinburgtn.gov/page/finance)
