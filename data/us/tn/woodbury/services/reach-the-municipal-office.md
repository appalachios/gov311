The municipal directory lists **101 W. Water Street, Woodbury, TN 37190** as the contact address for Woodbury. Call **(615) 563-4221** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/woodbury)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/woodbury)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cannon County records](../../cannon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Woodbury Water & Sewer**. Use the water and sewer provider’s website for online payments or its automated phone option. [Woodbury Water & Sewer](https://woodburywatertn.com/)
