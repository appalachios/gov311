The municipal directory lists **675 Main Street, Kimball, TN 37347** as the contact address for Kimball. Call **(423) 837-7040** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kimball) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofkimball.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kimball)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marion County records](../../marion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
