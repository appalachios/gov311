The municipal directory lists **140 Main Street, Savannah, TN 38372** as the contact address for Savannah. Call **(731) 925-3300** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/savannah) · [Municipal website](https://cityofsavannah.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/savannah)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hardin County records](../../hardin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Records Request Form**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request Form](https://cityofsavannah.org/public-records-request-form/)
- **Sanitation Services**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation Services](https://cityofsavannah.org/utility-department/sanitation-services/)
