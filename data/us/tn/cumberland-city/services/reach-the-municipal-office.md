The municipal directory lists **121 Main Street, Cumberland City, TN 37050** as the contact address for Cumberland City. Call **(931) 827-2000** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cumberland-city)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cumberland-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Stewart County records](../../stewart-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
