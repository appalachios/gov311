The municipal directory lists **Town Hall; PO Box 1350, Braden, TN 38010** as the contact address for Braden. Call **(901) 594-5100** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/braden)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
