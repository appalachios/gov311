The municipal directory lists **601 East Main Street, P.O. Box 2150, Johnson City, TN 37605-2150** as the contact address for Johnson City. Call **(423) 434-6000** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/johnson-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.johnsoncitytn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/johnson-city)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.johnsoncitytn.org/departments/building_services/building_division/contractor_requirements_and_information.php#outer-1731)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Carter, Sullivan, Washington. [Carter County records](../../carter-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Sullivan County records](../../sullivan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Washington County records](../../washington-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
