Contact Soddy-Daisy Building & Permits with your permit and project address. The city lists separate stages for building, plumbing, electrical, mechanical and gas piping. For example, arrange the in-slab plumbing inspection before gravel or concrete goes in. For new residential construction, the city ties temporary permanent power to approval of all rough-ins. Check the published sequence for your trade before moving to the next stage.
