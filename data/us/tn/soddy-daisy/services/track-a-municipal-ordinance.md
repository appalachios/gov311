To follow a proposal in Soddy-Daisy, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st and 3rd Thursday at 7:00 PM at Municipal Building.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/soddy-daisy) · [Municipal meeting agendas](https://soddy-daisy.org/government/board_of_commissioners/agendas___minutes.php)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-soddy-daisy) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/soddy-daisy)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Tennessee Code § 6-20-215 requires two open sessions at least a week apart, including for ordinances on an authorized consent calendar. If the city allows reading by caption, get the full text from the recorder. The ordinary waiting period is 15 days from first passage, but an eligible emergency ordinance stating the emergency facts can take effect on final passage by unanimous vote of those present.
