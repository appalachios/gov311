The municipal directory lists **9835 Dayton Pike, Soddy-Daisy, TN 37379** as the contact address for Soddy-Daisy. Call **(423) 332-5323** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/soddy-daisy) · [Municipal website](https://soddy-daisy.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report A Concern (Tip Line)**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report A Concern (Tip Line)](https://soddy-daisy.org/how_do_i/report_a_concerns.php)
- **Building & Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Permits](https://soddy-daisy.org/departments/building.php)
- **Sanitation & Pickup Services**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation & Pickup Services](https://soddy-daisy.org/departments/pickup.php)
- **Submit a Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Submit a Public Records Request](https://soddy-daisy.org/government/records.php)
