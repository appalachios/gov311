The municipal directory lists **1111 Ridgeway Avenue, Signal Mountain, TN 37377** as the contact address for Signal Mountain. Call **(423) 886-2177** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/signal-mountain) · [Municipal website](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/signal-mountain)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building & Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/departments/building___codes/index.php)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/departments/finance/open_records_requests.php)
- **Garbage Collection**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage Collection](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/departments/public_works/garbage_and_trash.php)
- **Review Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Review Property Taxes](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/how_do_i/review_property_taxes.php)
- **Report A Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report A Concern](https://www.signalmountaintn.gov/citizen_request_center/index.php)
