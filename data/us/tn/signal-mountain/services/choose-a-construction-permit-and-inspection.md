For land inside the Town of Signal Mountain, open **Online Permit Applications** on the town’s Building & Codes site. Follow the portal link to apply and track the request. For help with an account, call **423-886-2177, extension 204**. A Signal Mountain mailing address in the separate Town of Walden goes through Hamilton County for construction permits.
