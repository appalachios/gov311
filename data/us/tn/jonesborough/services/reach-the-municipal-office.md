The municipal directory lists **123 Boone Street, Jonesborough, TN 37659-1390** as the contact address for Jonesborough. Call **(423) 753-1030** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jonesborough) · [Municipal website](https://www.jonesboroughtn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jonesborough)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Washington County records](../../washington-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Stormwater General Information / Report a Problem**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Stormwater General Information / Report a Problem](https://www.jonesboroughtn.org/town-services/ms4-stormwater-management/stormwater-general-information-report-a-problem/)
- **Town Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [Town Recorder](https://www.jonesboroughtn.org/town-services/town-recorder/)
