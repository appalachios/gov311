The municipal directory lists **25844 Main Street, Ardmore, TN 38449** as the contact address for Ardmore. Call **(931) 427-2124** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ardmore) · [Municipal website](https://cityofardmoretn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ardmore)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Giles, Lincoln. [Giles County records](../../giles-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Lincoln County records](../../lincoln-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

The Census place-by-county table lists Ardmore in Giles, Lincoln. The 2026 Census TIGER/Line place and county polygons and the Tennessee Comptroller’s current city and county map also show land in each of these counties. Use the parcel’s county for county services. [Boundary source](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes2020/national_place_by_county2020.txt)
