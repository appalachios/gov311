The municipal directory lists **900 Waterford Place, Kingston, TN 37763** as the contact address for Kingston. Call **(865) 376-6584** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingston) · [Municipal website](https://kingstontn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingston)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Roane County records](../../roane-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk](https://kingstontn.gov/executive-assistant-and-city-clerk/)
