The municipal directory lists **3020 Highway 64, Crump, TN 38327** as the contact address for Crump. Call **(731) 632-4224** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/crump)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/crump)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hardin County records](../../hardin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
