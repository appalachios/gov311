The municipal directory lists **PO Box 177, Enville, TN 38332** as the contact address for Enville. Call **(731) 688-5511** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/enville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Chester, McNairy. [Chester County records](../../chester-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
