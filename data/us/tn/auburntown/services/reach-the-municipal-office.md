The municipal directory lists **City Hall, PO Box 38, Auburntown, TN 37016** as the contact address for Auburntown. Call **(615) 830-2672** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/auburntown)
