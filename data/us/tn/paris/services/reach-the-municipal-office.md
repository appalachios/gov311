The municipal directory lists **100 N. Caldwell, Paris, TN 38242** as the contact address for Paris. Call **(731) 641-1402** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/paris) · [Municipal website](https://paristn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/paris)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://paristn.gov/building-and-codes/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Henry County records](../../henry-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Call to report a pothole or check what qualifies for bulk pickup. [Public Works](https://paristn.gov/public-works/)
