The municipal directory lists **110 South Washington Street, Ripley, TN 38063** as the contact address for Ripley. Call **(731) 635-4000** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ripley) · [Municipal website](https://cityofripleytn.com/)
