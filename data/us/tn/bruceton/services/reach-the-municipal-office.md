The municipal directory lists **209 Cheatham Street, Bruceton, TN 38317** as the contact address for Bruceton. Call **(731) 586-2401** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bruceton)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bruceton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Carroll County records](../../carroll-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
