The municipal directory lists **333 Old Brownsville Road, Gallaway, TN 38036** as the contact address for Gallaway. Call **(901) 867-3333** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gallaway) · [Municipal website](https://gallawaytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gallaway)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
