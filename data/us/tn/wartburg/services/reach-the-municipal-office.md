The municipal directory lists **407 Eliza Street, Wartburg, TN 37887** as the contact address for Wartburg. Call **(423) 346-2323** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/wartburg)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/wartburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Morgan County records](../../morgan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
