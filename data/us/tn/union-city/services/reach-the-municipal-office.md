The municipal directory lists **408 South Depot Street, Union City, TN 38261** as the contact address for Union City. Call **(731) 885-1341** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/union-city) · [Municipal website](https://www.unioncitytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/union-city)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Obion County records](../../obion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utility Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing](https://www.unioncitytn.gov/utility-billing/)
- **Garbage Collection**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Garbage Collection](https://www.unioncitytn.gov/garbage-refuse-collection/)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.unioncitytn.gov/property-tax/)
- **View Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [View Permits](https://www.unioncitytn.gov/permit-utility-inspection-fees/)
