The municipal directory lists **202 East Main Street, PO Box 487, Rutherford, TN 38369** as the contact address for Rutherford. Call **(731) 665-7166** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rutherford)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/rutherford)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Gibson County records](../../gibson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
