The municipal directory lists **109 University Street, Martin, TN 38237** as the contact address for Martin. Call **(731) 587-3126** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/martin) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofmartin.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/martin)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.cityofmartin.net/government/building-codes/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Pay My Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Pay My Property Tax](https://tennesseetrustee.org/?entity=cityofmartin&state=TN)
- **Manage My Utility Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Manage My Utility Bill](https://www.cityofmartin.net/pay-my-water-bill/)
- **Make a Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Make a Public Records Request](https://www.cityofmartin.net/make-a-public-records-request/)
