The municipal directory lists **10085 2nd Street, PO Box 129, Gates, TN 38037** as the contact address for Gates. Call **(731) 836-7501** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gates)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gates)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lauderdale County records](../../lauderdale-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
