To follow a proposal in Gates, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Monday at 6:30 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gates)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-gates) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/gates)

Gates Municipal Code § 1-104 requires the board to consider and adopt an ordinance on two separate days. Each board member must receive the written proposal before the meeting where it is introduced. Three members make a quorum. A majority of those present must vote in favor, and the mayor breaks a tie. [Municipal Code § 1-104](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/gates-t1.pdf#page=2) · [Charter § 7](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/GATES_CHT.pdf#page=5)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)
