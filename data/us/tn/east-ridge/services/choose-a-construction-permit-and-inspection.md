For a project inside East Ridge, begin with the city’s Building Codes office at **1517 Tombras Avenue**, **423-867-7711**. Give the project address and describe any structural or trade work. Ask which permits and inspections apply before construction begins. The Hamilton County directory lists the East Ridge municipal office as district **2E**.
