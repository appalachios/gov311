The municipal directory lists **1517 Tombras Avenue, East Ridge, TN 37412** as the contact address for East Ridge. Call **(423) 867-7711** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/east-ridge) · [Municipal website](https://www.eastridgetn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/east-ridge)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building & Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes](https://www.eastridgetn.gov/building_codes.html)
- **City Clerk**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Clerk](https://www.eastridgetn.gov/city_clerk.html)
- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.eastridgetn.gov/sanitation.html)
- **Open Records requests**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records requests](https://eastridgetn.govqa.us/WEBAPP/_rs/%28S%28yby4gx2mstfhwslswibha2uu%29%29/supporthome.aspx)
