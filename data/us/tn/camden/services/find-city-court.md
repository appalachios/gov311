If the ticket names **Camden City Court**, contact the city court clerk at **731-584-4622**, 119 West Main Street. Have the citation number and hearing date ready when asking about a payment or appearance. If the papers name Benton County General Sessions, use that court’s clerk for scheduling. [Camden City Court directory](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/city_directory.html) · [Benton County General Sessions](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/general_sessions.html)
