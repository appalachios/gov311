On the City of Camden website, open **Pay Online** and choose **Water Bill** or **Property Taxes**. Have the account or parcel number and billing period handy. Call City Hall at **731-584-4656** if you need help matching the account. For a separate Benton County tax notice, use the county’s **Pay/Search Property Taxes** link. [Camden payment options](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/) · [County tax lookup](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/)
