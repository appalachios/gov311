Call Camden Planning or the building inspector at **731-584-4656**. Give the street address, parcel number and a description of the work. Ask whether it lies within the city and what needs approval before construction. The planning page links the zoning ordinance, subdivision regulations and building permit fees. [Planning and fee schedule](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/planning.html) · [Building and codes directory](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/city_directory.html)

If a hearing is needed, ask for the case number and which board acts next. Compare the Planning Commission’s recommendation with the Board of Mayor and Aldermen’s decision when the matter requires a city ordinance. [Planning Commission records](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/planning.html) · [Aldermen’s meeting records](https://www.cityofcamdentn.com/agenda_minutes_boma.html)
