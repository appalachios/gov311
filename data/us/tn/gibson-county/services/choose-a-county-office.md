For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Gibson County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/gibson)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(731) 855-7642**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(731) 855-7627**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(731) 414-7792**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(731) 855-7669**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Gibson County include Bradford, Dyer, Gibson, Humboldt, Kenton, Medina, Milan, Rutherford, Trenton, Yorkville.
