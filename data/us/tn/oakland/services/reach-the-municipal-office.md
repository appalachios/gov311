The municipal directory lists **170 Doss Circle; PO Box 56, Oakland, TN 38060** as the contact address for Oakland. Call **(901) 465-8523** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oakland)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oakland)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Town of Oakland services**. Use Online Bill Payment for a town bill or the Town Manager Action Center to report a concern. [Town of Oakland services](https://www.oaklandtn.gov/)
