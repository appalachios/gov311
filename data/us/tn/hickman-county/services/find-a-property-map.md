In the Hickman County assessor’s **Citizen Access Portal**, choose **Property Search**. Pick the tax year and search by **Property Address**, **Name** or **Parcel #**. Select the underlined parcel number and open **Maps**. The record also has **Land**, **Buildings**, **Sales** and **Property Card** tabs. For the county bill or a payment, use **Pay Property Taxes** under **Trustee** on the county’s Administrative Offices page and choose Hickman County. [Assessor’s parcel portal](https://hickman.capturecama.com/CAMA/CAPortal/CZ_MainPage.aspx) · [County assessor and trustee](https://hickmancountytn.gov/departments/administrative-offices)

For land-use zoning, open the county’s **Zoning Map**, search for the property and select it to see the zoning district. If you need to know whether a project is allowed, take the address and parcel number to Planning and Zoning. For school attendance, use the attendance zone map published by Hickman County Schools and confirm the assignment with the district. [County zoning map](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/673d3766edd94904970e33f61262a1be) · [Planning and Zoning](https://hickmancountytn.gov/departments/administrative-offices#planning-zoning) · [School attendance zone map](https://www.hickmank12.org/aboutourdistrict)

To find your county commissioners, enter your address in the Comptroller’s **Tennessee Voter District Lookup** and match the county commission district number to Hickman County’s directory. [State voter district lookup](https://comptroller.tn.gov/maps.html) · [Commissioners by district](https://hickmancountytn.gov/government/commissioners)
