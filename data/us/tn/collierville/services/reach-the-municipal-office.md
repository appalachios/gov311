The municipal directory lists **500 Poplar View Parkway, Collierville, TN 38017-3440** as the contact address for Collierville. Call **(901) 457-2200** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collierville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collierville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utilities – Establish Service, Billing and Payments**. Use the town’s service setup instructions for water, sewer and sanitation, or open eSuite to view an existing bill. [Utilities – Establish Service, Billing and Payments](https://www.colliervilletn.gov/residents/utilities-430)
