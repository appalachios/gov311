To follow a proposal in Collierville, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd and 4th Monday at 6:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collierville)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-collierville) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/collierville)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

The board must pass the ordinance on three different days. It may amend the wording even at the third reading. The public hearing must take place by that reading, and the town clerk keeps the signed, numbered ordinance. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/collierville) · [Governing text](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/collierville/latest/collierv_tn/0-0-0-35457)
