Choose the meeting in Collierville’s public meetings portal and read the agenda attachment. For a town ordinance, follow the Board of Mayor and Aldermen. If you cannot speak in person, the town offers **eComment** for live-streamed meetings. Create an account, select the specific agenda item and submit your comment before the meeting starts. [Town public meetings](https://www.colliervilletn.gov/publicmeetings) · [Instructions for commenting](https://www.colliervilletn.gov/government/public-comment)

Use the meeting record afterward to check the motion, any amended language and the vote. A public comment records the speaker’s position. Ask the town for the adopted ordinance and effective date if the posted packet contains only the proposal. [Board of Mayor and Aldermen](https://www.colliervilletn.gov/government/mayor-and-board-of-aldermen) · [Meeting records](https://www.colliervilletn.gov/publicmeetings)
