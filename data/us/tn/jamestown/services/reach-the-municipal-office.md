The municipal directory lists **314 East Central Avenue, PO Box 670, Jamestown, TN 38556** as the contact address for Jamestown. Call **(931) 879-8815** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jamestown)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fentress County records](../../fentress-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
