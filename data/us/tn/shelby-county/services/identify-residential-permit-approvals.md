Give the Memphis and Shelby County Office of Construction Enforcement the street address, lot and project description at **901-222-8300**. Its residential checklist identifies city-specific approvals. For Millington, the city supplies an approval letter. Lakeland provides written approval and collects city fees. Arlington approves the plans and handles fees before the county issues the permit. Contact the named city before submitting county paperwork. [Residential permit checklist, page 1](https://shelbycountytn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/40657/Residential-Building-Permit-Procedures)

For a new home in Germantown, the checklist calls for a separate footing and foundation permit. Submit the city engineer’s approved stormwater form and site plans. Germantown must approve the foundation survey before the building permit can issue. Check the relevant city’s steps with the construction office for the actual site. [Germantown requirements in the county checklist](https://shelbycountytn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/40657/Residential-Building-Permit-Procedures)
