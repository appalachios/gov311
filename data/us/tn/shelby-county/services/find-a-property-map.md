In the **Shelby County Register of Deeds** map, search by address, owner or parcel number, then select the parcel. You can see surrounding properties, read the appraisal, look up recorded sales and open linked deeds. For a sale, look for its instrument number, the identifier used to retrieve the recorded document. [Open the Register of Deeds map](https://gis.register.shelby.tn.us/) · [County register’s search tools](https://register.shelby.tn.us/services)

For the tax bill or payment history, choose **County Tax Info** on the parcel or open the county trustee’s **Tax Look-Up**. If the parcel has a **City Tax Info** link, use it for the separate municipal record. Match the parcel number and tax year before using either bill. [Parcel map](https://gis.register.shelby.tn.us/) · [Shelby County Trustee](https://www.shelbycountytrustee.com/103/Tax-Look-Up)

For representation, use the Election Commission’s address lookup to find your county commission district, then open the county directory for the person who represents it. [Polling location and district lookup](https://www.electionsshelbytn.gov/polling-location-and-complete-district-information/) · [Meet your commissioners](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/1209/Meet-Your-Shelby-County-Commissioners)

Shelby County has seven public school districts. For a home in Memphis-Shelby County Schools, open **School Zone Locator** on the district’s site and enter the home address. For a municipal district, use the county’s school district directory to reach that district’s enrollment office. [Memphis-Shelby County Schools](https://www.scsk12.org/) · [Shelby County school districts](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/3713/School-Districts)
