For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Shelby County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/shelby)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(901) 222-3068**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(901) 222-8101**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(901) 222-7001**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(901) 222-0200**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(901) 222-1200**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Shelby County include Arlington, Bartlett, Collierville, Germantown, Lakeland, Memphis, Millington.
