Get the ordinance number and the complete proposal. The commission makes copies available at 160 North Main Street, Suite 450, Memphis. Follow the item across three separate days when the full commission meets in open session. A favorable committee recommendation still goes to the full commission. Compare the text considered at the last reading with the version you started with. [County ordinance procedure and code](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/72/Code-of-Ordinances) · [County meeting records](https://agenda.shelbycountytn.gov/PublicAccess/)

After passage, check what the county mayor did and read the ordinance’s effective-date clause. The county explains that a signed ordinance takes effect 15 days after passage unless the ordinance specifies a later date. A veto sends the item back to the commission. Ask the Minutes Department for the final ordinance, the roll call and any override action if the archive only shows the proposal. [Adoption and veto procedures](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/72/Code-of-Ordinances) · [Minutes Department](https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/3311/Role-of-the-Minutes-Department)
