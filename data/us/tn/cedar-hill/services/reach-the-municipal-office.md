The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 113, Cedar Hill, TN 37032** as the contact address for Cedar Hill. Call **(615) 696-4802** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/cedar-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofcedarhill.org/)
