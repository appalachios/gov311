The municipal directory lists **151 Mill Street; PO Box 158 , Lynnville, TN 38472** as the contact address for Lynnville. Call **(931) 527-3158** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lynnville) · [Municipal website](https://historiclynnville.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lynnville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Giles County records](../../giles-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
