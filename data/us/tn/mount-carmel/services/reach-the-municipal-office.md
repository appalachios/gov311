The municipal directory lists **100 East Main Street, Mount Carmel, TN 37645-1421** as the contact address for Mount Carmel. Call **(423) 357-7311** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mount-carmel) · [Municipal website](https://mountcarmeltn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mount-carmel)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://mountcarmeltn.gov/government/public_records_request/index.php)
- **Building Inspection**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspection](https://mountcarmeltn.gov/departments/building_inspection.php)
