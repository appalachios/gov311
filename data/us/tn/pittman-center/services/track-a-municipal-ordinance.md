To follow a proposal in Pittman Center, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “3rd Thursday at 4:30 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pittman-center)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/city-code/pittman_center) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/pittman-center)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Under Tennessee Code §§ 6-2-101 and 6-2-102, the board must consider an ordinance on two separate days and record the votes. After final passage, the ordinance can take effect only after the text or its caption appears in a newspaper of general circulation in the municipality. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/pittman-center) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/2026-10/Mayor_Aldermanic_Charter.pdf#page=21)
