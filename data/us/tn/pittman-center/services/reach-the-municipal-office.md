The municipal directory lists **2839 Webb Creek Road, Sevierville, TN 37876** as the contact address for Pittman Center. Call **(865) 436-5499** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/pittman-center) · [Municipal website](https://pittmancentertn.gov/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sevier County records](../../sevier-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Pay Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Pay Property Tax](https://pittmancentertn.gov/pay-property-tax/)
- **Apply for Building Permits**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Apply for Building Permits](https://pittmancentertn.gov/apply-for-building-permits/)
