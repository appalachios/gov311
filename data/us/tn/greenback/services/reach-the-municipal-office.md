The municipal directory lists **6889 Morganton Road, P.O. Box 140, Greenback, TN 37742** as the contact address for Greenback. Call **(865) 856-2224** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greenback) · [Municipal website](https://www.greenbackgov.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Loudon County records](../../loudon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.greenbackgov.com/city-recorder)
- **Building Permits & Inspections**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Permits & Inspections](https://www.greenbackgov.com/building-permits-and-inspections)
