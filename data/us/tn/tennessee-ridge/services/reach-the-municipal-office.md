The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 207, Tennessee Ridge, TN 37178** as the contact address for Tennessee Ridge. Call **(931) 721-3385** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tennessee-ridge)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tennessee-ridge)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Houston, Stewart. [Houston County records](../../houston-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Stewart County records](../../stewart-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
