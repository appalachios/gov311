The municipal directory lists **6400 Stage Road, Bartlett, TN 38134-3739** as the contact address for Bartlett. Call **(901) 385-6400** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bartlett) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofbartlett.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bartlett)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Real Estate Property Tax Lookup**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Real Estate Property Tax Lookup](https://cityofbartletttn.munisselfservice.com/css/citizens/default.aspx)
- **Utility Billing / Water Department**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing / Water Department](https://www.cityofbartlett.org/101/Utility-BillingWater-Administration)
