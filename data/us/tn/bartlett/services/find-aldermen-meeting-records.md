Open **Read Board Agendas & Minutes** and choose **Most Recent & Archived** under Board of Mayor & Aldermen. The separate link for other boards leads to commission and advisory materials. Match the meeting date and ordinance number across the agenda and minutes. Note the motion, vote and any amendment before treating the text as adopted. [Bartlett meeting archive](https://www.cityofbartlett.org/42/Read-Board-Agendas-Minutes)

The city also links **Read Municipal Code**. If a recent measure is missing from the code, contact City Hall at **901-385-6400** with the ordinance number and date. Ask for the adopted language and effective date. [Municipal code and City Hall contact](https://www.cityofbartlett.org/42/Read-Board-Agendas-Minutes)
