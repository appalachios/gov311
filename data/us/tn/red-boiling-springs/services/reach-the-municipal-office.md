The municipal directory lists **361 Lafayette Road, Red Boiling Springs, TN 37150** as the contact address for Red Boiling Springs. Call **(615) 699-2011** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/red-boiling-springs) · [Municipal website](https://redboilingspringstn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/red-boiling-springs)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Macon County records](../../macon-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
