The municipal directory lists **85 Highway 114 South, PO Box 248, Scotts Hill, TN 38374** as the contact address for Scotts Hill. Call **(731) 549-3175** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/scotts-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofscottshill.com/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Decatur, Henderson. [Decatur County records](../../decatur-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Henderson County records](../../henderson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
