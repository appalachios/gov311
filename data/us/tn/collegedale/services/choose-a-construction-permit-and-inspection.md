Start at Building & Codes and choose **Applications Forms & Permits**. Tell the city whether the project involves a building, electrical work, or a sign so you get the appropriate application and any separate review. Use the city-linked online building permit portal for the submission. Include the site address, even when its mailing city differs from the municipality. [City applications and permits](https://www.collegedaletn.gov/departments/building___codes_department/applications_forms___permits.php) · [Collegedale permit portal](https://collegedaletn.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/collegedaletn)
