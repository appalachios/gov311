The municipal directory lists **4910 Swinyar Drive, P.O. Box 1880, Collegedale, TN 37315-1880** as the contact address for Collegedale. Call **(423) 396-3135** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collegedale) · [Municipal website](https://www.collegedaletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/collegedale)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://collegedaletn.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/collegedaletn)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hamilton County records](../../hamilton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building & Codes Department**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes Department](https://www.collegedaletn.gov/departments/building___codes_department/index.php)
- **Recycling**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Recycling](https://www.collegedaletn.gov/services/recycling/index.php)
- **Public Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Request](https://www.collegedaletn.gov/citizen_help_center/request/index.php)
