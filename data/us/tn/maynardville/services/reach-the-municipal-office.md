The municipal directory lists **125 Johnson Road, Maynardville, TN 37807** as the contact address for Maynardville. Call **(865) 992-3821** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/maynardville) · [Municipal website](https://www.maynardvilletn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/maynardville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Union County records](../../union-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
