The municipal directory lists **415 N Military Street, Loretto, TN 38469** as the contact address for Loretto. Call **(931) 853-6797** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/loretto) · [Municipal website](https://cityoflorettotn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/loretto)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lawrence County records](../../lawrence-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
