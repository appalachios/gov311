In the **BMA Archive**, choose the Board of Mayor and Aldermen meeting by date. Use **all past** for older meetings. For 2010 through 2014, open the meeting title itself for documents. The archive also directs readers to video for meetings after December 2009. [Germantown meeting archive](https://www.germantown-tn.gov/government/city-boards-commissions/bma-archive)

Read the agenda for the proposal, then use the minutes for the board’s action. Follow the ordinance number to subsequent meetings for later readings or a revised motion. Request the adopted text if a vote refers to an amendment described only in the discussion.
