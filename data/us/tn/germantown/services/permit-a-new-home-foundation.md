For a new home inside Germantown city limits, start by obtaining the city engineers’ approval of the Homebuilder Storm Water Guidelines form and preparing the site plans for the footing and foundation permit. After that permit is issued, submit the separate building permit application and a complete plan set. Obtain Germantown’s approval of the foundation survey before the joint office issues the building permit. Call Construction Enforcement at **901-222-8300** with the site address and permit number. [Memphis and Shelby County residential permit procedures](https://shelbycountytn.gov/DocumentCenter/View/40657/Residential-Building-Permit-Procedures)
