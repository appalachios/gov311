The municipal directory lists **101 College Street, PO Box 720, Gleason, TN 38229** as the contact address for Gleason. Call **(731) 648-5547** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gleason) · [Municipal website](https://cityofgleasontn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gleason)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Weakley County records](../../weakley-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
