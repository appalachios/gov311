The municipal directory lists **5153 Burgess Gower Rd, Coopertown, TN 37172** as the contact address for Coopertown. Call **(615) 382-4470** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/coopertown) · [Municipal website](https://www.coopertowntn.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Records Policy**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records Policy](https://www.coopertowntn.org/public-records-policy)
- **Building & Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes](https://www.coopertowntn.org/permits)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.coopertowntn.org/property-tax)
- **Report an Issue to the Street Dept**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report an Issue to the Street Dept](https://www.coopertowntn.org/street-department)
