The municipal directory lists **19922 Alberta Street, P.O. Box 4237, Oneida, TN 37841** as the contact address for Oneida. Call **(423) 569-4295** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oneida) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofoneida.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Wastewater, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/oneida)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.townofoneida.com/government/building-codes/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Scott County records](../../scott-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Records**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records](https://www.townofoneida.com/government/public-records/)
- **Pay Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Pay Property Tax](https://www.townofoneida.com/living/taxes/)
