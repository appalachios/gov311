The municipal directory lists **100 South Russell Street, Portland, TN 37148** as the contact address for Portland. Call **(615) 325-6776** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/portland) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage - Solid Waste/Brush, Gas, Sewer, Stormwater, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/portland)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/157/Building-Codes)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Sumner, Robertson. [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

The Census place-by-county table lists Portland in Sumner, Robertson. The 2026 Census TIGER/Line place and county polygons and the Tennessee Comptroller’s current city and county map also show land in each of these counties. Use the parcel’s county for county services. [Boundary source](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes2020/national_place_by_county2020.txt)

## More municipal services

- **Open Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records Request](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/286/Open-Records-Request)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/177/Property-Tax)
- **Utility Bill Calculator**. Use the city calculator to estimate the permit fee for your project. [Utility Bill Calculator](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/384/Utility-Bill-Calculator)
- **Connect or Disconnect Utility Services**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Connect or Disconnect Utility Services](https://www.cityofportlandtn.gov/567/Existing-Utility-Service-Request)
