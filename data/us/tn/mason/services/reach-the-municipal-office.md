The municipal directory lists **12157 Main Street, Mason, TN 38049** as the contact address for Mason. Call **(901) 294-3525** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mason) · [Municipal website](https://townofmasontn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mason)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Tipton County records](../../tipton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
