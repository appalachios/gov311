The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 38, Ramer, TN 38367** as the contact address for Ramer. Call **(731) 645-7564** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ramer)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ramer)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
