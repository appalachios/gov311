Start with the site address and parcel. Inside Clinton, use Codes Enforcement / Planning & Zoning for building and zoning applications, local code amendments, and planning or appeals schedules. Check the submission deadline for the meeting you need. The city lists a separate state inspector for electrical inspections. For Anderson County zoning or codes outside Clinton, the city lists **865-457-6244**. Verify the municipality before using the county route.
