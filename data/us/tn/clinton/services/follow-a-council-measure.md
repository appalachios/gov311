Find the next council agenda from Clinton’s city website. Record the item number and any ordinance number. For a zoning item, the planning or appeals board has its own packet. Follow the matter to City Council when council action is required.

Find the matching meeting in **City Council Minutes** and then the measure in **City Ordinances**. Check amendments and the final text before relying on a summary. For example, Clinton describes Ordinance 686 as adopting the 2021 International Codes **with amendments**. The edition printed on a code book alone does not identify the local changes. Direct a request for the final signed record to the city recorder. [City Council](https://www.clintontn.net/page/city-council/) · [Council minutes](https://www.clintontn.net/documents/departments/clinton-city-council/clinton-city-county-minutes/518747) · [Ordinance files](https://www.clintontn.net/documents/departments/clinton-city-council/clinton-city-ordinances/518941) · [Local code amendments and records contact](https://www.clintontn.net/page/codes-enforcement-zoning/)

The charter requires majority approval at two meetings at least a week apart. When an emergency requires action for public safety and welfare, those meetings can be as close as 24 hours apart. The council reads the title first and, unless it votes to waive the full reading, the whole ordinance at the second meeting.
