The municipal directory lists **396 Spring Street, Kingston Springs, TN 37082** as the contact address for Kingston Springs. Call **(615) 952-2110** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingston-springs) · [Municipal website](https://kingstonsprings.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingston-springs)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://kingstonsprings.net/building-and-codes)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Cheatham County records](../../cheatham-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
