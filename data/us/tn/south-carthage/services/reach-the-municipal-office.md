The municipal directory lists **106 South Main, South Carthage, TN 37030** as the contact address for South Carthage. Call **(615) 735-2727** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-carthage) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofsouthcarthage.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-carthage)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Smith County records](../../smith-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
