The municipal directory lists **32 Courthouse Square, Jasper, TN 37347** as the contact address for Jasper. Call **(423) 942-3180** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jasper)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/jasper)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marion County records](../../marion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City contact directory**. Reach Water Billing at 423-942-3180 or the waterworks manager at 423-942-2324. [City contact directory](https://www.jasper-tn.com/contact_us.html)
