Open the dated **Regular Meeting** agenda and note the resolution number. An **Agenda Session** is a discussion of items expected at a later voting meeting. The county directs public comments to the regular meeting, when you may speak about an item before its vote and again at the end. Check the posted schedule for changes before going. [Current agendas and comment instructions](https://hamiltontn.gov/CurrentAgenda.aspx)

After the meeting, open the Commission Meeting minutes for the date of the vote. Search for the resolution number and read the motion, amendments and disposition. The archive also includes agenda-preparation records. Use the meeting label to distinguish the discussion from the decision. For older minutes, follow the resolutions archive link. [Commission minutes and audio](https://www.hamiltontn.gov/CountyCommission_Minutes.aspx)
