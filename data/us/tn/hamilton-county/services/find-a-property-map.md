Open **GISMO**, Hamilton County’s parcel map, and search the street address. Select the parcel and keep its number. For ownership or appraised value, search the assessor’s records by owner, address or parcel number. The county GIS office also identifies who maintains the zoning and flood map layers. [Open GISMO](https://gis.hamiltontn.gov/Html5Viewer/index.html?viewer=gismo) · [County map information](https://gis.hamiltontn.gov/gishome.html) · [Assessor’s property search](https://assessor.hamiltontn.gov/)

- **Tax bill.** Use the Hamilton County Trustee’s property tax inquiry for the county bill. If the property is inside Chattanooga, the city has a separate search for city property tax and stormwater bills. [County tax inquiry](https://tpti.hamiltontn.gov/AppFolder/Trustee_PropertySearch.aspx?Dept=Trustee) · [Chattanooga tax search](https://chattanooga.gov/stay-informed/tax-information/property-tax)
- **Elected officials.** In GISMO, open the layers and turn on **Political Boundaries**. Select the commission district or voting precinct layer for your address. Match the commission district number to the name and contact information in the Election Commission’s directory. [GISMO](https://gis.hamiltontn.gov/Html5Viewer/index.html?viewer=gismo) · [Current elected officials](https://elect.hamiltontn.gov/CurrentElected.aspx)
- **Schools.** On the Hamilton County Schools zoning page, open **School Zone Finder** and enter the home address for the current school year. The same page has attendance maps by school level and explains which magnet schools have attendance zones. [Hamilton County Schools zoning tools](https://hcde.org/operations/zoning/)
