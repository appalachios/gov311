Open the county Planning Department’s Current Zoning Map, turn on **RPA Zoning** in Layers, and search the street address. Check whether the site is inside Chattanooga or in unincorporated Hamilton County. A future land-use map describes a plan for an area. The current zoning map identifies the parcel’s existing classification. [County maps and case search](https://www.hamiltontn.gov/Department_Planning.aspx)

For an unincorporated county case, find its number under the county’s case information and use the county public comment instructions. For a Chattanooga case use the Regional Planning Agency’s agenda and comment form. Read the notice for that case’s submission cutoff. A regional planning recommendation can precede another hearing before the County Commission or Chattanooga City Council.
