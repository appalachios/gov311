The municipal directory lists **20 Main Street; PO Box 621, La Grange, TN 38046** as the contact address for La Grange. Call **(901) 878-1246** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/la-grange)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/la-grange)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utilities**. Contact the town recorder about garbage collection or use the listed electric provider for an outage. [Utilities](https://lagrangetn.com/government/utilities/)
