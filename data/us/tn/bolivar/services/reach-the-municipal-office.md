The municipal directory lists **City Hall, 211 North Washington Street, Bolivar, TN 38008** as the contact address for Bolivar. Call **(731) 658-2020** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bolivar) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofbolivar.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bolivar)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hardeman County records](../../hardeman-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
