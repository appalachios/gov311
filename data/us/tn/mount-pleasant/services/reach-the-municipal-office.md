The municipal directory lists **100 Public Square, Mount Pleasant, TN 38474** as the contact address for Mount Pleasant. Call **(931) 379-7717** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mount-pleasant) · [Municipal website](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Maury County records](../../maury-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/sanitation)
- **Open Records Request**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Open Records Request](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/open-records-request)
- **Pay Utility Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Pay Utility Bill](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/utility-payments)
- **Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Taxes](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/property-tax)
- **Report Pothole**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report Pothole](https://www.mtpleasant-tn.gov/potholes)
