To follow a proposal in Dandridge, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Tuesday at 6:00 PM at Dandridge Public Works Facility/Police Dept..” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dandridge)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/city-code/dandridge) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/dandridge)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

The mayor signs the ordinances and resolutions the board approves and signs the minutes of its proceedings. Ask for the signed ordinance and the minutes of the vote to see what took effect. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/dandridge) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/dandridge_cht.pdf#page=4)
