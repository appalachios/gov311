The municipal directory lists **131 East Main Street, P.O. Box 249, Dandridge, TN 37725** as the contact address for Dandridge. Call **(865) 397-7420** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dandridge) · [Municipal website](https://dandridgetn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dandridge)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Jefferson County records](../../jefferson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
