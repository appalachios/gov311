The Benton County Clerk issues vehicle titles and registrations, marriage licenses and business licenses. Choose the transaction on the clerk’s page and confirm which documents and payments the office needs before traveling. For commission records, give the clerk the meeting date and number of the resolution you need. [County clerk services and contact](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/county_court_clerk.html)

If your paperwork is about a lawsuit or court appearance, check the name of the court. Circuit Court records go to the Circuit Court Clerk, and Chancery Court records go to the Clerk and Master. [Circuit Court Clerk](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/circuit_court_clerk.html) · [Clerk and Master](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/clerk_master.html)
