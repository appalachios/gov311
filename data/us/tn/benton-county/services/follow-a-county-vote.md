Open the meeting archive and choose the date. The Agenda column contains the proposals. The Minutes column records what the commissioners did. To speak at the Citizens Forum, sign the sheet on the courtroom podium before the meeting begins. Confirm the date on the schedule. [Agendas and minutes](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/commission_agendas_minutes.html) · [Meeting and speaking instructions](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/county_commission.html)

For example, item 8 on the November 16, 2026 agenda proposed adding $570 to the schools budget for a youth risk behavior survey. Follow resolution **20261116-01** to the minutes to see the motion, amendments and vote. If the minutes have yet to appear, ask the county clerk for the adopted text and voting record using that number. [November 16 packet](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/pdfs/board_agendas_minutes/agendas/2026/11162026.pdf) · [County clerk](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/county_court_clerk.html)
