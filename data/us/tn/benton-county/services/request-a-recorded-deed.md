For a recorded deed, mortgage or plat, contact the Benton County Register of Deeds at **731-584-6661**. Give the parties’ names, the recording date or the book and page if you have them. If you only have a street address, identify the parcel in the state property viewer first. Ask the register about copy charges and whether the document can be sent electronically. [Register of Deeds](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/register_of_deeds.html) · [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/)
