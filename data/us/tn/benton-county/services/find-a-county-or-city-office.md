Camden and Big Sandy are the two incorporated municipalities in Benton County. The county website also lists Eva and Holladay as communities. For unincorporated land, start with the county for zoning. A Camden or Big Sandy mailing address alone cannot settle which government has jurisdiction. Give the office the street address or parcel number. [County government](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/) · [County profile listing incorporated municipalities](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/benton) · [County zoning authority](https://www.bentoncountytn.gov/county_commission.html)

For city services inside Camden, call **731-584-4656**. For town services in Big Sandy, call **731-593-3213**. For a county service call **731-584-6011** or use the county department directory. Benton County is separate from the City of Benton in Polk County.
