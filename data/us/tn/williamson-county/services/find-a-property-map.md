Open **Williamson County Maps** and choose **Click here to View the Online Maps**. Select the property on the **Parcels** layer for its address, land and improvement assessments, and deed book and page references. You can also turn on flood zones or voting districts. The county keeps the link to its current viewer on the Maps page. [County maps](https://williamsoncounty-tn.gov/371/Maps) · [Open the interactive map](https://williamsontn.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=70ae32b7255e48cdad65792ffb2bbf2a)

- **Assessment or bill.** The assessor’s **Property Search** has the assessment record. The trustee’s **Search & Pay Property Taxes** has the county bill. The trustee also collects city tax for Brentwood, Franklin, Nolensville and Thompson’s Station. Fairview and Spring Hill collect their own city bills. [Property Search](https://inigo.williamsoncounty-tn.gov/property_search/) · [Trustee](https://www.williamsoncounty-tn.gov/Trustee) · [Which office collects city tax](https://williamsoncounty-tn.gov/65/Property-Tax-Rates)
- **Elected officials.** Use the **Official Voting District Map** on the county Maps page to find the district. The commission directory lists both county commissioners who represent each district and how to reach them. [District map](https://williamsoncounty-tn.gov/371/Maps) · [Commissioners by district](https://www.williamsoncounty-tn.gov/Directory/Home/DepartmentListing?DID=3)
- **Schools.** The county map includes a **Franklin Special School District** boundary layer. For a home inside that boundary, use the Franklin Special District school map for kindergarten through eighth grade. For Williamson County Schools, including high school, use **Zoned School** on the district’s bus routes and school zones page. Enter the home address to get the school and bus stop. [Franklin Special District map](https://www.fssd.org/about-us/district-map) · [Williamson County Schools zone lookup](https://www.wcs.edu/about-us/school-zones)
