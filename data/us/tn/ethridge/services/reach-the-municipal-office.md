The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 173, Ethridge, TN 38456** as the contact address for Ethridge. Call **(931) 829-2150** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ethridge) · [Municipal website](https://cityofethridge.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/ethridge)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lawrence County records](../../lawrence-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
