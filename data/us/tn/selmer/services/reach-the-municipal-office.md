The municipal directory lists **144 North Second Street, Selmer, TN 38375** as the contact address for Selmer. Call **(731) 645-3241** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/selmer) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofselmer.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/selmer)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
