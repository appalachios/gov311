The municipal directory lists **200 North Castle Heights Avenue, Lebanon, TN 37087** as the contact address for Lebanon. Call **(615) 443-2839** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lebanon) · [Municipal website](https://www.lebanontn.org/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Wilson County records](../../wilson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Utility Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Utility Billing](https://www.lebanontn.org/324/Customer-Service-Utility-Billing)
- **Building Inspection**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspection](https://www.lebanontn.org/149/Building-Inspection)
- **Stormwater Applications, Bonds & Fees**. Check the requirements for starting service at your address. [Stormwater Applications, Bonds & Fees](https://www.lebanontn.org/831/Stormwater-Applications-Bonds-Fees)
- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.lebanontn.org/717/Sanitation)
