To follow a proposal in Lebanon, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st and 3rd Tuesday at 6:00 PM at Town Meeting Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lebanon)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-lebanon) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/lebanon)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter article IV, § 3 requires two readings on separate days in open council sessions. A resolution can pass on one reading. For an adopted ordinance, the city can publish or post its caption, so ask Finance and Revenue for the numbered text in the ordinance book. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/lebanon) · [Governing text](https://www.lebanontn.org/DocumentCenter/View/1014/Lebanon-City-Charter-Current-through-2023#page=29)
