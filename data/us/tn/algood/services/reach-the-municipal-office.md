The municipal directory lists **215 W. Main Street, Algood, TN 38506** as the contact address for Algood. Call **(931) 537-9545** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/algood) · [Municipal website](https://algood-tn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage - Solid Waste/Brush, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/algood)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Putnam County records](../../putnam-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Public Works**. Call 931-537-9545 to find the scheduled pickup day for your neighborhood. [Public Works](https://algood-tn.com/index.php/departments/public-works/)
