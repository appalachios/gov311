The municipal directory lists **25 Public Square, Lawrenceburg, TN 38464** as the contact address for Lawrenceburg. Call **(931) 762-4459** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lawrenceburg) · [Municipal website](https://www.lawrenceburgtn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/lawrenceburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Lawrence County records](../../lawrence-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.lawrenceburgtn.gov/167/Sanitation)
