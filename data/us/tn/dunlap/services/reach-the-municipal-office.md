The municipal directory lists **15595 Rankin Avenue, P.O. Box 546, Dunlap, TN 37327** as the contact address for Dunlap. Call **(423) 949-2115** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dunlap)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/dunlap)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sequatchie County records](../../sequatchie-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Resident services**. Call 423-949-4119 for municipal utility service or to report a water leak. [Resident services](https://dunlaptn.gov/residents/)
