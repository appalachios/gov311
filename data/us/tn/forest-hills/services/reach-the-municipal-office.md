The municipal directory lists **6300 Hillsboro Pike, Nashville, TN 37215** as the contact address for Forest Hills. Call **(615) 372-8677** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/forest-hills)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Davidson County records](../../nashville/services/find-a-property-map.md)
