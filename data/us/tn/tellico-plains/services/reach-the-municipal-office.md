The municipal directory lists **405 Veterans Memorial Dr., Tellico Plains, TN 37385** as the contact address for Tellico Plains. Call **(423) 253-2030** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tellico-plains) · [Municipal website](https://www.tellicoplainstn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tellico-plains)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Monroe County records](../../monroe-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Water & Sewer**. Choose the form for an existing meter or a new tap. Contact City Hall for paperless billing. [Water & Sewer](https://www.tellicotn.org/water-sewer)
- **Water**. Find the city utility office and check who serves the address. [Water](https://www.tellicoplainstn.com/water)
