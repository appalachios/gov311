To follow a proposal in Gainesboro, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “1st Thursday at 7:00 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/gainesboro)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/code/municipal-code-gainesboro) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/gainesboro)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Municipal Code § 1-104 requires a majority of the Board of Mayor and Aldermen to vote yes on both first and second reading. An ordinance takes effect upon passage. A one-time administrative resolution can take effect when stated for the record and approved by a majority. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/gainesboro) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/system/files/codes/Gainesboro_T-1.pdf#page=2)
