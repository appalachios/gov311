The municipal directory lists **3300 Hwy 196 S, Piperton, TN 38017** as the contact address for Piperton. Call **(901) 853-4830** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/piperton) · [Municipal website](https://www.pipertontn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/piperton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Fayette County records](../../fayette-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.pipertontn.com/requesttracker.aspx)
- **Permits & Inspections**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits & Inspections](https://www.pipertontn.com/320/Building-Permits)
- **Property Tax**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [Property Tax](https://www.pipertontn.com/316/Property-Tax)
