The municipal directory lists **201 West Grundy Street, PO Box 807, Tullahoma, TN 37388** as the contact address for Tullahoma. Call **(931) 455-2648** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tullahoma) · [Municipal website](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/tullahoma)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Coffee, Franklin. [Coffee County records](../../coffee-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Franklin County records](../../franklin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **City Recorder's Office**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder's Office](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/departments/city_recorders_office/city_recorders_office.php)
- **Commercial Garbage Collection Fees**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Commercial Garbage Collection Fees](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/departments/city_recorders_office/commercial_garbage_collection_fees.php)
- **City Property Taxes**. Use the city’s property tax page to look up billing or payment information. [City Property Taxes](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/departments/city_recorders_office/city_property_taxes.php)
- **Report a Pothole**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Pothole](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/departments/public_works/report_a_pothole.php)
- **Permit Information**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permit Information](https://www.tullahomatn.gov/departments/safety___codes_compliance/permits.php)
