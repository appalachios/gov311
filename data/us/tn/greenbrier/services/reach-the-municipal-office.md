The municipal directory lists **2414 Hwy 41 S, Greenbrier, TN 37073** as the contact address for Greenbrier. Call **(615) 643-4531** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greenbrier) · [Municipal website](https://www.greenbriertn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/greenbrier)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Robertson County records](../../robertson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building & Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes](https://www.greenbriertn.org/158/Building-Codes)
- **Sanitation**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Sanitation](https://www.greenbriertn.org/168/Sanitation)
- **Ways to Reduce Your Sewer Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Ways to Reduce Your Sewer Bill](https://www.greenbriertn.org/239/Ways-to-Reduce-Your-Sewer-Bill)
