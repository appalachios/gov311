The municipal directory lists **35 School Road, P.O. Box 219, Palmer, TN 37365** as the contact address for Palmer. Call **(931) 779-3247** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/palmer)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/palmer)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Grundy County records](../../grundy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
