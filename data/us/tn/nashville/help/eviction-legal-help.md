## What they help with

The Legal Aid Society of Middle Tennessee and the Cumberlands is a nonprofit law firm that gives free civil legal help to people who qualify. Its Nashville office serves Davidson and Williamson counties. It runs an eviction help program for Davidson County renters.

## How to ask for help

1. Call 833-837-HOME and leave a message.
2. Give your name, date of birth and phone number. Include your court date if you have one.
3. For other legal problems, call the Nashville office at 615-244-6610 or 800-238-1443. The Legal Aid Society asks that you call before you come in.

## Deadlines

Eviction deadlines are short, and missing one can cost you your home. Do not wait to call.

In Tennessee, an eviction lawsuit is called a detainer action, and the court paper that starts it is a detainer warrant. In Davidson County, the General Sessions Court hears these cases.

Gov311 does not restate the deadlines, because a wrong or outdated number here could hurt you. These official pages state them.

## The court clerk

For questions about your case file or court date, contact the General Sessions Civil Clerk at (615) 862-5195 or gscivilclerk@jisnashville.gov. The office is at 408 Second Avenue North, Suite 2110, Nashville, TN 37201, and is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
