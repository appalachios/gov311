Open **Nashville Parcel Viewer**, select the magnifying glass and search by street address, parcel ID or owner. Select the matching parcel and **View Details** for ownership, zoning, permit and assessment history. Write down the parcel ID if you plan to check its tax bill. [Nashville Parcel Viewer](https://maps.nashville.gov/ParcelViewer/) · [Metro’s guide to reading the map](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/codes/construction-and-permits/land-use-and-zoning-information/understanding-zoning-code)

- **Tax bill or payment.** Use **Pay and Check Property Taxes** from the Metropolitan Trustee. The parcel map shows the assessment used to calculate a bill. The Trustee handles the amount owed and payments. [Metropolitan Trustee](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/trustee) · [Metro tax lookup](https://metronashville.taxandrevenue.opengov.com/)
- **Elected officials.** Enter the address in Metro’s **Council District Lookup** to find its council and school board districts, as well as its state and congressional districts. For the council member’s name and contact information, match the district to the Metro Council roster. [District lookup](https://nashville.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/lookup/index.html?appid=d4f2569a11ae474da897e31e09e056a6) · [Council members](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/council/districts)
- **School attendance.** Enter the student’s home address in Metro Nashville Public Schools’ **Zone Finder** to see the schools serving that address. [MNPS Zone Finder](https://zonefinder.mnps.org/) · [Find My School](https://www.mnps.org/students-families/register-for-school/find-my-school)
