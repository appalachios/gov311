The municipal directory lists **1 Public Sq, Nashville, TN 37201** as the contact address for Nashville. Call **(615) 862-6000** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/nashville) · [Municipal website](https://www.nashville.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/nashville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Davidson County records](../../nashville/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits and Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits and Codes](https://www.nashville.gov/services?keyword=&departments=5401)
- **Find Your Trash and Recycling Schedule**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Find Your Trash and Recycling Schedule](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/waste-services/trash-and-recycling-services/recycling-app)
