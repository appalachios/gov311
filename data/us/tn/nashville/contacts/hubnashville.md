## What this office handles

hubNashville takes service requests, questions, and feedback for Metro Nashville departments, then routes each one to the right department. Use it when you are not sure who to call.

hubNashville is for non-emergency services. In an emergency, call 911.

## Best way to reach them

- **Online.** Submit a request at [hub.nashville.gov](https://hub.nashville.gov).
- **By phone.** Call 311 from inside Davidson County. From anywhere, call 615-862-5000.
- **By app.** Metro lists the hubNashville 311 mobile app on its [mobile apps page](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/government/mobile-apps).

You can submit a request anonymously. If you create an account, you can track your requests.

The phone line opens at 8 a.m. on weekdays. It closes at 5 p.m., except on the first and third Wednesdays of each month, when it closes at 4 p.m. You can submit a request online or in the app at any hour.
