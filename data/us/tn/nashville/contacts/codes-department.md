## What this office handles

The department issues building permits, reviews zoning, inspects construction, and enforces property standards in Nashville and Davidson County.

## Best way to reach them

Call the line that matches your question.

| Question | Phone |
| --- | --- |
| Main line | 615-862-6500 |
| Zoning and permit questions (Zoning Division and Codes Help Desk) | 615-862-6510 |
| Building inspections | 615-862-6550 |
| Property standards | 615-862-6590 |

The office is on the 1st floor of the Metro Office Building, 800 President Ronald Reagan Way.
