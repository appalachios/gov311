The municipal directory lists **59 Main Street, P.O. Box 18, Milledgeville, TN 38359** as the contact address for Milledgeville. Call **(731) 687-3011** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/milledgeville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/milledgeville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Chester, Hardin, McNairy.
