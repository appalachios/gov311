The municipal directory lists **415 Broad Street, Kingsport, TN 37660** as the contact address for Kingsport. Call **(423) 229-9400** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingsport) · [Municipal website](https://www.kingsporttn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/kingsport)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Hawkins, Sullivan. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [Sullivan County records](../../sullivan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
