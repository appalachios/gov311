The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 200, Altamont, TN 37301** as the contact address for Altamont. Call **(931) 692-3971** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/altamont)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Grundy County records](../../grundy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
