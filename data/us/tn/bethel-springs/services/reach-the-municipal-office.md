The municipal directory lists **4066 Main Street, P.O. Box 214, Bethel Springs, TN 38315** as the contact address for Bethel Springs. Call **(731) 934-7266** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bethel-springs) · [Municipal website](https://townofbethelsprings.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/bethel-springs)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Water Information**. Find the city utility office and check who serves the address. [Water Information](https://townofbethelsprings.com/residents/water)
- **City Hall**. Use the office contact information to ask which municipal department handles the request. [City Hall](https://townofbethelsprings.com/government/city-hall)
