The municipal directory lists **City Hall, 521 Harrison Ferry Road, Baneberry, TN 37890** as the contact address for Baneberry. Call **(865) 674-0124** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/baneberry) · [Municipal website](https://cityofbaneberry.com/)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://cityofbaneberry.com/building-permits/)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Jefferson County records](../../jefferson-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
