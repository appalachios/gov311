Mitchellville’s charter gives the mayor and aldermen authority to pass ordinances. It sets no general reading count. Tennessee’s default allows an ordinance to pass on one reading unless an applicable law or an adopted local rule requires more. The Tennessee Supreme Court applied that principle to a municipal charter that did not specify readings. [Mitchellville charter § 8](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Mitchelville_cht.pdf#page=8) · [Tennessee ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [State ex rel. Balsinger v. Town of Madisonville](https://law.justia.com/cases/tennessee/supreme-court/1968/435-s-w-2d-803-2.html)

The town lists its regular meeting for the second Thursday at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. Charter § 4 also lets the mayor call a special session. Call (615) 325-6020 and ask the recorder for the board’s current rules of procedure, the proposed ordinance and the agenda. After the vote, ask for the signed text and the regular or special meeting minutes.
