The municipal directory lists **P.O. Box 13, Mitchellville, TN 37119** as the contact address for Mitchellville. Call **(615) 325-6020** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mitchellville)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mitchellville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Sumner County records](../../sumner-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
