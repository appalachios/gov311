The municipal directory lists **100 Banks Street, P.O. Drawer H, Huntland, TN 37345** as the contact address for Huntland. Call **(931) 469-0283** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntland)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/huntland)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Franklin County records](../../franklin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
