Choose the category that matches the problem in Memphis 311, such as **Trash, Recycling & Litter** or **Sewer Problem**. Enter the service location and describe what a crew would need to find. An account is optional. Save the request number and use **Track It** to check the existing report. Use the Memphis portal for City of Memphis services. [Memphis 311 requests](https://311.memphistn.gov/public/) · [Track a request](https://311.memphistn.gov/public/Home/TrackIt)
