The municipal directory lists **125 N. Main Street, Memphis, TN 38103** as the contact address for Memphis. Call **(901) 576-6500** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/memphis) · [Municipal website](https://memphistn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Garbage, Gas, Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/memphis)

For construction at this address, check the municipal permit office’s project instructions. [Municipal building permits](https://aca-prod.accela.com/SHELBYCO/Default.aspx)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Shelby County records](../../shelby-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Solid Waste**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Solid Waste](https://memphistn.gov/solid-waste/)
- **311 Report Issues​**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [311 Report Issues​](https://311.memphistn.gov/public)
