On the City Council agenda page, choose the date and open the regular meeting documents. Committee materials are listed separately. Record the ordinance number and reading, then open the signed minutes for the action taken. Follow an item marked held to a later meeting. When the council amends an item, request the version adopted under that number for the language that took effect. [City Council agendas, documents and minutes](https://memphistn.gov/city-council-meeting-agenda/)

The May 12, 2026 minutes illustrate two distinct outcomes. Item 10, Ordinance 5959 on the Small Business Enterprise Opportunity Program, had been held at several meetings. On May 12 the council approved an amended version on third and final reading. At the same meeting, budget Ordinance 5971 received first-reading approval on the consent agenda. A record of approval alone would give you an incomplete answer about the budget. [Signed minutes, items 10 and 6](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Signed-5-12-26-MINUTES.pdf)
