The municipal directory lists **231 East Main Street, P.O. Box 301, Adamsville, TN 38310** as the contact address for Adamsville. Call **(731) 632-1401** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/adamsville) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofadamsville.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/adamsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Hardin, McNairy. [Hardin County records](../../hardin-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [McNairy County records](../../mcnairy-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Permits, Licenses, Taxes & Zoning**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Permits, Licenses, Taxes & Zoning](https://www.cityofadamsville.com/city_services/licenses_permits_fees/index.html)
- **Trash Collection & Recycling**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Trash Collection & Recycling](https://www.cityofadamsville.com/city_services/trash_collection/index.html)
