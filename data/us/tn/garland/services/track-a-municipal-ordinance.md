The Garland charter (Private Acts of 1913, chapter 35, as amended) sets no general number of ordinance readings. One reading can suffice under Tennessee law when the charter is silent and no adopted local rule or subject-specific law requires more. Check the rule for the proposal before treating its first vote as final. [Compiled charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Garland_cht.pdf#page=5) · [Tennessee ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [Tennessee Supreme Court, *Balsinger*, 435 S.W.2d 803, 805](https://law.justia.com/cases/tennessee/supreme-court/1968/435-s-w-2d-803-2.html)

Section 14 sets a quorum of four. Section 15 directs the recorder to keep both the minutes and an ordinance book. Ask for the adopted procedural rule in the ordinance book, any later amendment, and the minutes of the vote. For older measures, the Tennessee State Library and Archives lists Garland’s minutes, ordinances and resolutions for December 1980 through April 2001 on roll A-17,352. [Municipal officials and records contact](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/garland) [State archives, Tipton County inventory, printed page 29](https://sostngovbuckets.s3.amazonaws.com/tsla/preservation/countymicro/tipt.pdf)

To follow a proposal in Garland, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Tuesday at 7:00 PM at Community Center.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/garland)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)
