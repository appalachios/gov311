**For a license**, use the optional online pre-application, then visit a county clerk location with proof of age and the information on the county checklist. The clerk posts separate notarized forms for an incarcerated applicant or one whose disability prevents appearing. Tennessee Code § 36-3-104 also describes a separate route for qualifying military service abroad. Call the clerk about those documents or a 17-year-old applicant before traveling. [Knox County application and forms](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/marriagelicense.php) · [State application law](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/TNCodeBills/2025/TNCodeBill_Volume1_2025.pdf#page=540)

**For a courthouse wedding**, the clerk’s ceremony service is by appointment for Knox County residents at the Old Courthouse. Call **865-215-2385**. Receiving the license at a branch does not book the ceremony. [Clerk’s ceremony instructions](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/marriagelicense.php)

**For a certified copy**, use *Copy of a Marriage License* on the same page or visit a branch. Knox County can supply the record if it issued the license, even if the ceremony took place elsewhere in Tennessee. Have both applicants’ names and the date of marriage ready. [County marriage records](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/marriagelicense.php)
