If you want to know what Knox County funded, open the adopted book for the fiscal year. The year runs from July through June. For example, the FY 2027 book covers July 2026 through June 2027. Use the table of contents to find a department, fund or capital project. [Adopted budgets by year](https://www.knoxcounty.org/finance/adopted_budget_reports.php) · [FY 2027 adopted budget](https://www.knoxcounty.org/finance/pdfs/2026_2027_budget/FY27_Adopted_Budget_Book.pdf)

If you want to change what gets funded, start earlier. Departments submit requests to Finance. Finance and the county mayor put the proposal together. The Board of Education approves its school budget request and sends it through the mayor to the County Commission. Look for the proposed book, the dates of public hearings and the budget resolution in the Commission agenda. [Budget development](https://www.knoxcounty.org/finance/budget_dev.php) · [Commission agendas](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691)

**The first reading is when commissioners can introduce a new budget amendment.** On August 31, 2026, the Commission adopted ordinance O-26-7-103, changing the rules for subsequent budgets. Under County Code § 2-548 the budget resolution needs two readings at least seven days apart, with the final reading by June 30. At the second reading commissioners can remove an amendment adopted at the first, or reconsider one proposed there that failed to pass. Reconsideration takes two-thirds of members present and voting. If a road or library appropriation matters to you, find out whether anyone offered it at the first reading.
