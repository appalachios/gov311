For a **county-maintained road**, call Knox County Highway Maintenance at **865-215-5800**. Give the road name, nearest intersection or address, and describe the pavement, ditch, drainage or bridge problem. The division routes work by maintenance district. [County Highway Maintenance](https://www.knoxcounty.org/epw/highwaymain.php)

For a state-maintained route, use TDOT’s maintenance request. For a municipal street, start with Knoxville or Farragut public works. Where a road crosses a boundary, give the location of the defect so the office can identify the responsible crew. [TDOT maintenance requests](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/maintenance-request.html) · [City limits map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)
