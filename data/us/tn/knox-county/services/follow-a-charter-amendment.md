The charter sets the rules for county government. A commissioner-sponsored amendment has to reach county voters before it can change those rules. Start with the ordinance proposing the change. Compare the full text it would add or delete with the charter section in force today. In the code library, open Part I, Article IX, § 9.05. It also describes the citizen-petition route. [Knox County Charter](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances) · [Commission agenda packets](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691)

Under the Commission's rules, the Law Director reviews a proposal before first reading. If it passes that reading, there must be at least two public hearings before the second reading. The charter requires two-thirds of the Commission's membership to propose an amendment by ordinance. Its passage sends the proposal to the County Election Commission. For the outcome, find the ballot question and the certified election results. Adoption requires a majority of the votes cast on the question. [Commission rules, Charter Amendments, page 24](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/wp-content/uploads/CommissionRules_2026_07_27.pdf#page=24) · [Knox County Election Commission](https://www.knoxcounty.org/election/)
