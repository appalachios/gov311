**To pay the county bill**, open *Look Up & Pay Taxes* from the Knox County website, or reach the trustee’s downtown office at **865-215-2305**, 400 Main Street, Suite 427. Match the parcel, tax year and collecting office before paying. A Knoxville city tax bill is a separate account. Farragut says it does not levy a municipal property tax. [County payment portal](https://propertytax.knoxcountytn.gov/) · [Trustee locations](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/about/locations) · [Knoxville tax office](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/477/Property-Taxes) · [Farragut tax information](https://www.townoffarragut.org/274/General-Facts)

**To question the assessment**, look up the assessor’s parcel record. If the property details are wrong, use the field review form to ask for an inspection. If you intend to appeal the value, check the county Board of Equalization’s schedule and filing instructions in time to make your appeal. A field review request is a different step. [Assessor’s valuation FAQ](https://property.knoxcounty.org/resources/faq/) · [Request a field review](https://property.knoxcounty.org/i-want-to/request-field-review/) · [State appeal guide](../../_state/services/appeal-property-assessment.md)
