Check the site address and city limits before starting the application. Use Knox County Codes Administration for a project in unincorporated Knox County. If the parcel is inside Knoxville or Farragut, start with that municipality's permitting office. [KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/) · [Knox County Codes Administration](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/)

For a house, start with structural drawings and a site plan. Get a certified address from Knoxville-Knox County Planning. For a house on septic, the county asks for the Health Department's drainfield layout. For an addition or accessory building on septic, it asks for septic system certification. Commercial plans require a Tennessee-licensed architect's or engineer's stamp. Check the county's instructions for your project. [Permit requirements](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/permitinfo.php) · [Request a certified address](https://knoxplanning.org/addressing/request-address)

Apply through the county's online permit page or bring the plans to Codes Administration, City County Building, 400 Main Street, Suite 547, Knoxville. Bring a PDF for in-person plans larger than 11 by 14 inches. Save the permit number. On the Codes Administration page, choose **Permit Inspection Lookup** to see scheduled inspections and results. For help adding plans or revisions, call 865-215-2325. [Online permits](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/online_permits.php) · [County inspection lookup and help](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/)
