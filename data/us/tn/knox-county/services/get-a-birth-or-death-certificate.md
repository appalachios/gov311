Check the record type, year and who can request it on the county’s Vital Records page. The office can issue eligible records for events elsewhere in Tennessee. Bring the required identification and documents showing your right to the copy if you are requesting someone else’s record. [Vital Records eligibility and forms](https://www.knoxcounty.org/health/vitalrecords/)

For **birth or death certificates**, use the main health office at **140 Dameron Avenue** in Knoxville. For **birth certificates only**, the West Clinic is at **9000 Executive Park Drive, Suite A301B**. The county page also has mail instructions and online order forms. Questions about an order go to **865-215-5098**. [Locations and ordering options](https://www.knoxcounty.org/health/vitalrecords/)
