Open the clerk’s *Motor Vehicle* page and choose your situation. Buying a used car, moving from another state, replacing a lost title and renewing an existing plate each have a checklist. For a move, use the state checklist for proof of identity, residence, registration and what to do if a lender holds the title. [Knox County motor vehicles](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/motorvehicle/index.php) · [Tennessee new-resident guide](../../_state/services/register-vehicle-after-moving.md)

For a title, take the required original documents to a clerk location. Avoid corrections or whiteout on a Tennessee title. For an annual tag renewal, use the online service linked by the clerk or visit a branch. If claiming a wheel tax exemption, use the affidavit for the renewal year listed on the county page. [County filing instructions and current forms](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/motorvehicle/index.php) · [Branch locations](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/location.php)
