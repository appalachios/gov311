List each service you need and give the provider the street address. Knoxville Utilities Board supplies several services in its territory. Other districts serve parts of Knox County. Knoxville’s directory lists contacts by service, including water systems serving different parts of the county. A Knoxville mailing address alone does not establish that KUB provides water or sewer there. [City and county utility provider directory](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/1156/Utilities)

Before signing up or paying a connection fee, ask the district to confirm service at the address. This matters for vacant lots, where there may be no existing bill to identify the utility. If West Knox Utility District is the provider, use its customer service site. If the home already has service but you need help paying a bill, use the county energy assistance guide.
