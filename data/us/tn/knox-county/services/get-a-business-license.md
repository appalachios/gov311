Start with the business activity, location and taxable receipts. Tennessee Revenue explains how to determine whether an exemption, a minimal activity license or a standard license applies. For a business in Knoxville city limits, check the city registration requirement as well. [Tennessee licensing rules](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/business-tax/registration-and-licensing.html.html) · [Knox County business license instructions](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/businesstax.php)

The county accepts the initial application online, by mail or at a clerk location. For a **minimal activity license**, use the county renewal form. If receipts changed enough to require a standard license, ask the county’s Business Tax Division about reclassification at **865-215-2392**. For a **standard license**, follow Revenue’s state filing instructions. The county clerk receives notice from Revenue when the annual tax has been paid. [County applications and renewals](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/businesstax.php) · [State registration guide](../../_state/services/register-for-business-tax.md)
