1. Use **zoned school** on the district’s enrollment page to find the school for your home address. Choose the packet for the school year and grade you need. A request to attend a different school has a separate transfer process. [Enrollment](https://www.knoxschools.org/studentsfamilies/enrollment) · [Transfer requests](https://www.knoxschools.org/studentsfamilies/transfers)
2. The parent or guardian completes and prints the packet and brings it to the zoned school. Check the instructions for proof of birth, residence, immunization, physical exam and custody documents if applicable. Ask the school when it accepts packets. For enrollment help call **865-594-1502**. [District document checklist](https://www.knoxschools.org/studentsfamilies/enrollment)
3. If your family lacks a fixed, regular and adequate place to stay, tell the school or the district’s homeless education liaison. Students covered by the McKinney-Vento Act have the right to enroll immediately even when the usual documents are missing. The district explains how it obtains records and handles school selection and disputes. [Knox County Schools homeless education services](https://www.knoxschools.org/studentsfamilies/student-supports/homeless)
