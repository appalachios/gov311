Open **Board Agendas / Minutes** on the district’s Board of Education page. Choose the meeting date and check whether it was a work session or a voting meeting. Open the agenda item and its attachments to see the proposed wording. If all you have is a subject, look it up in the policy manual and use the policy number to follow it between meetings. [Board meeting records](https://www.knoxschools.org/about/board-of-education) · [Board policy manual](https://www.knoxschools.org/about/board-of-education/board-policies)

After the meeting, read the motion and recorded action in the minutes. If the board amended a proposal, compare the adopted language and its effective date with the manual before relying on it. A county commission agenda dealing with school funding is a separate record from a Board of Education policy vote.
