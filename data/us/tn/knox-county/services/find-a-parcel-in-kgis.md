For land in Knox County, including Knoxville and the Knox County part of Farragut, open **KGIS Maps**. Under **Search By**, choose **Address**, **Parcel** or **Owner**. If you have a street address, enter it, select the matching result and write down the parcel ID. You can also reach the map through **County Maps** on the Knox County Property Assessor’s website. [Open KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/map.htm) · [Knox County Property Assessor](https://property.knoxcounty.org/)

What you do next depends on the record you need. For the assessed value, open the assessor’s **Property Lookup** and match the parcel ID. For a recorded deed, plat or easement, take the parcel information to the Register of Deeds. [Property Lookup](https://property.knoxcounty.org/) · [Knox County deed guide](get-a-deed.md)

If the parcel is on the **Loudon County** side of Farragut, open the Tennessee Property Viewer and choose **Loudon**. For records or payment, use the Loudon County office that handles the transaction. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/) · [Loudon County land records](../../loudon-county/services/find-property-records.md)
