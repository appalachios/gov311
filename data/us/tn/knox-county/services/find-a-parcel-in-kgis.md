For land in Knox County, including Knoxville and the Knox County part of Farragut, open **KGIS Maps**. Under **Search By**, choose **Address**, **Parcel** or **Owner**. If you have a street address, enter it, select the matching result and write down the parcel ID. You can also reach the map through **County Maps** on the Knox County Property Assessor’s website. [Open KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/map.htm) · [Knox County Property Assessor](https://property.knoxcounty.org/)

- **Tax and ownership records.** For the assessed value, open the assessor’s **Property Lookup** and match the parcel ID. Use the county trustee’s tax search for the amount due. Knoxville also bills city property tax. For a recorded deed, plat or easement, take the parcel information to the Register of Deeds. [Property Lookup](https://property.knoxcounty.org/) · [County tax bills](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/services/pay-taxes/) · [City and county tax guide](pay-or-question-property-tax.md) · [Recorded deeds](get-a-deed.md)
- **Who represents the address.** Select the parcel and open **Property Map and Details Report**. Its **Political Districts** section lists the county commission district and the commissioners who represent the address, including the two at-large members. For current contact details, use the commission directory. To see a boundary on the map, open **Political District Maps**. [KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/map.htm) · [County library’s KGIS guide](https://www.knoxcountylibrary.org/resources/kgis-geographic-information-system) · [County commissioner directory](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/people/)
- **School zones.** Parcel details show school zones. For a student’s zoned schools, use **KGIS School Zone Search** from Knox County Schools. Enter the home address, select the matching suggestion and read the elementary, middle and high school names. [School district’s address lookup and instructions](https://help.knoxschools.org/en_US/school-zone-search)

If the parcel is on the **Loudon County** side of Farragut, open the Tennessee Property Viewer and choose **Loudon**. For records or payment, use the Loudon County office that handles the transaction. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/) · [Loudon County land records](../../loudon-county/services/find-property-records.md)
