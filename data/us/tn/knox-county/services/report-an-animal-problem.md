For an animal running loose, a welfare concern or a nuisance in Knox County, call Young-Williams Animal Services at **865-407-2229**, or use its online report form. For a missing or found pet, follow the Lost and Found link. Give the location of the animal and a description. [Animal Services dispatch, report form and lost pets](https://www.young-williams.org/animal-services/)

For immediate danger, call **911**. The Knox County Sheriff’s animal crimes page explains the division of responsibilities between law enforcement and Young-Williams, including where to direct routine reports. [Sheriff’s animal crimes unit](https://knoxsheriff.org/animal-control/)
