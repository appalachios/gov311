Open **Sign Up** from the Knox County, Tennessee emergency notification page. It takes you to the county’s Everbridge enrollment. Choose how you want to receive warnings. The county offers text, email or voice calls. If you do not have internet access, call **311** or **865-215-4311** for telephone enrollment. [County alert signup](https://www.knoxcounty.org/alert/)

The Knoxville-Knox County Emergency Management Agency serves both the city and county. Signing up registers you for warnings. If you need a response to an emergency in progress, call **911**. [Joint emergency management agency](https://www.knoxvilletn.gov/536/Knoxville-Knox-Co-Emergency-Management-A)
