1. Search the Planning case file by address or case number. Confirm the jurisdiction says **County**. Open the staff report, map and meeting date. The case page has links to submit comments or request to speak. If you are applying, Planning publishes the forms, fees and filing deadlines. [Planning case search](https://knoxplanning.org/cases/) · [Applications and deadlines](https://knoxplanning.org/applications)
2. Look up the Planning Commission's action. A recommendation to approve a county rezoning goes to the Knox County Commission for a decision. A denial by Planning stands unless appealed. The staff report tells you where to file an appeal and the applicable deadline. Knoxville cases go to City Council. Farragut handles zoning through its own town government. [Planning's rezoning process](https://knoxplanning.org/commission/rezoning-process) · [County hearing notice and jurisdiction](../codes/county-zoning.md)
3. Find the county **zoning session** on the Commission calendar, then check the clerk's minutes. Match the parcel, file number, motion and vote. The motion can add a condition absent from the staff recommendation. [County meeting packets](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691) · [County Clerk's minutes](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/commission_minutes.php)

For example, Planning recommended office and medical zoning for **339 Fox Road**, case **9-H-26-RZ**, on September 10, 2026. On October 19 the County Commission adopted RZ-26-10-101 by an 11–0 vote. The motion limited the size and lighting of signs, including a six-foot limit in height or width for monument signs. Someone checking what can be built on that parcel needs the recorded condition as well as the zoning district. [Planning case and staff report](https://knoxplanning.org/cases/9-H-26-RZ) · [County zoning minutes, page 2](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/CommMinutes/2026/10-19-2026_zs.pdf#page=2)
