Take the application and documents verifying your name and address to any Knox County Public Library branch or the downtown Lawson McGhee Library. The library completes registration in person. If your identification does not show your current address, use the library’s list to choose an acceptable second document. [Library card instructions, forms and branches](https://ask.knoxlib.org/faq/337456)

Cards are free for Knox County residents and people who own property in the county. A Tennessee resident who lives elsewhere can check the out-of-county card terms. For a child or teenager, use the youth application and check who must sign. [Eligibility and youth card options](https://ask.knoxlib.org/faq/337456)
