Knox County residents can take their own household waste and recycling to a county convenience center free of charge. Choose a site from the map and check its hours, load limits and rules for the material. The centers direct commercial loads, contractors’ job waste and people hauling waste from rental properties to other facilities. [Center rules](https://www.knoxcounty.org/solid_waste/centers.php) · [County drop-off locations](https://www.knoxcounty.org/solid_waste/map_of_centers.php)

If you have chemicals, paint, tires, electronics or a refrigerant appliance, look up that material before loading it. The City of Knoxville operates the household hazardous waste facility used by the county. It has different hours from the county centers. For county disposal questions, call **865-215-5865**. [Problem and household hazardous wastes](https://www.knoxcounty.org/solid_waste/problem_hhw.php)
