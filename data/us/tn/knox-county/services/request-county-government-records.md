Name the existing record you want and the office that holds it. For a Commission action, start with the clerk's posted minutes. To get the exact wording that passed, ask for the signed ordinance or resolution by number, meeting date and any attachments. For email, specify the office, date range and subject. The county policy lists a public records request coordinator for each office. [County public records](https://www.knoxcounty.org/recordsmgmt/aboutpublicrecords.php) · [Policy and coordinators](https://www.knoxcounty.org/recordsmgmt/pdfs/Public_Records_Policy.pdf) · [Commission minutes](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/commission_minutes.php)

**To inspect records**, Tennessee citizens can ask the appropriate coordinator orally, by email or in writing. **To get copies**, use the county's online request route, or send Form A to the appropriate coordinator by email, mail or in person. The county requires proof of Tennessee citizenship, such as a valid Tennessee driver's license or another acceptable ID. Use Form A to request a cost estimate before the office makes copies. Keep your request number and any written response. [Online request](https://records-knoxcountytn.govqa.us/WEBAPP/_rs/SupportHome.aspx) · [Form A](https://www.knoxcounty.org/recordsmgmt/pdfs/Public_Records_Request_Form_A.pdf)
