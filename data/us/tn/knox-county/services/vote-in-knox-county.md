1. Use the county Election Commission’s **Where do I vote?** lookup for your current residence. Check the notice for the election you plan to vote in. For example, in 2026 the county published a separate relocation map for precinct 66SE in the Farragut area. A previous polling place or a Knoxville mailing address alone may send you to the wrong location. [County voter lookup and precinct notices](https://www.knoxcounty.org/election/)
2. To register or change your voter address, use GoVoteTN. For early voting, the sample ballot or an absentee application, choose the specific election on the county page. Early-voting locations can differ from your assigned Election Day precinct. Questions about your record go to **865-215-2480**. [Tennessee online voter registration](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/) · [Knox County Election Commission](https://www.knoxcounty.org/election/)
3. To verify an outcome, open the [county results archive](https://www.knoxcounty.org/election/electionresults.php), choose the date and find the race or ballot question. Use the certified result when you need the final vote.
