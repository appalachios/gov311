1. In the Commission's meeting calendar, choose the month and the County Commission meeting. Open the dated agenda and select the ordinance or resolution number to read the item and its attachments. Check whether it is on first or second reading. The separate zoning agenda handles parcel rezonings. [County agenda and packet search](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691) · [Meeting notices](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/notices/)
2. At the meeting, the commission can amend the text, change the agenda, postpone an item or vote it down. Use the clerk's minutes for that meeting to find the motion as voted and the roll call. If minutes have yet to appear, the recording can tell you what happened. Ask the clerk for the numbered measure and any veto or override afterward. [County Clerk's minutes](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/commission_minutes.php) · [Meeting recordings](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/past-meetings/) · [Charter and effective dates](../codes/county-charter-and-ordinances.md)

**What changed during a reading?** The October 26, 2026 caption for O-26-10-104 covered pay increases for both county commissioners and school board members. Commissioners voted 11–0 on first reading to remove the school board language. The November 16 second-reading agenda names only county commissioners. On the meeting recording at 1:44:31, voting closes and the clerk announces that the motion passed 10–1. Ask the County Clerk for the filed ordinance to check the signed text and its effective date. [October minutes, O-26-10-104](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/CommMinutes/2026/10-26-2026.pdf) · [November 16 agenda, item 49](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691&dsp=ag&seq=5169) · [November 16 recorded vote, 1:44:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZ8FUBGsrQ8&t=6271s)

**What if an item says “override”?** The September 28, 2026 minutes list a proposed override of the mayor's veto of R-26-8-905. Read the disposition beneath that heading. The commission withdrew the item from the agenda. It held no override vote on it that day. [September 28 minutes, RLA-18866](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/CommMinutes/2026/09-28-2026.pdf)
