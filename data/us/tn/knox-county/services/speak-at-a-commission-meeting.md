Find the meeting and agenda item first. The legislative and zoning sessions open public forum for agenda topics. The forum at the end also accepts other county topics. If you register about an agenda item, the chair offers the choice of speaking during forum or when commissioners consider that item. [Public forum instructions](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/public-forums/) · [Agenda search](https://public.destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=56691)

Use **Request to Speak**, call **865-215-2534** or email the Commission Office by its published cutoff. If you are already at the meeting, speak with the vice chair or the office manager before it starts. Have the meeting date, item or topic and contact information ready. People who live outside Knox County should check the rule for recognition by the chair. [Registration form](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/public-forum-request-to-speak/) · [Registration cutoff and in-person option](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/public-forums/)

For testimony on a zoning case, get the Planning Commission file number and ask which session will hear it. Rule VII sets time limits for presentation and rebuttal and provides for witnesses to be sworn. Tell the office if you want to testify on the case so it can explain where you fit on that agenda. [Commission rules, Rule VII](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/documents/commission-rules/) · [Follow a rezoning](follow-a-rezoning.md)
