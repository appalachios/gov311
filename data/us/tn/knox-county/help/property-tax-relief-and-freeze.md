Open the trustee’s page for the current tax year and read the eligibility rules for the program you want. Property tax relief has different forms for older or disabled homeowners, qualifying disabled veterans and qualifying surviving spouses. The senior freeze has its own income and ownership requirements. Use the limits on that year’s form. [Tax relief and senior freeze applications](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/services/tax-relief)

If you are applying for both, submit **both applications**. The trustee accepts one set of supporting documents for the two applications and requires annual renewal. Bring your documents to a trustee location if you need help completing the forms or making copies. [Application instructions](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/services/tax-relief) · [Trustee locations](https://trustee.knoxcounty.org/about/locations)
