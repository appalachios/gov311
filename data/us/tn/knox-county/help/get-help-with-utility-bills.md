Open the Community Action Committee’s **Utility Assistance** page. For the Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP), use the state application portal linked there. Gather the current utility bill, household income documentation and the other records on CAC’s checklist. Renters whose landlord collects the utility payment should check the additional form. [CAC application, forms and checklist](https://www.knoxcac.org/utility-assistance/)

If you need help using the online application, CAC lists assistance hours and locations, including the Ross Building at **865-637-6700** and the East Neighborhood Center at **865-546-5125**. If service has been disconnected or you have a shutoff notice, tell staff when contacting them. CAC also administers **Project Help** for qualifying households in the Knoxville Utilities Board service territory. Its requirements and intake route are on the same page. [Application assistance and Project Help](https://www.knoxcac.org/utility-assistance/)
