Call **865-215-5030** for the Knox County Women, Infants and Children program. Tell the office whom the appointment is for. Ask which clinic to visit and which identity, residency and income documents apply. Follow the links on the county page for Tennessee WIC eligibility and benefits. [Knox County WIC](https://www.knoxcounty.org/health/wic/) · [Tennessee WIC appointment guide](../../_state/services/make-a-wic-appointment.md)

If you are also applying for SNAP, that application goes through Tennessee Human Services. [Apply for SNAP](../../_state/services/apply-for-snap.md)
