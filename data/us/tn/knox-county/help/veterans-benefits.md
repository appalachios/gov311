Call **865-215-5645** to schedule an appointment. The office is in the Knox Central Building, **1000 North Central Street**, Knoxville. Tell staff whether you are starting a claim, responding to a VA decision or asking about survivor benefits. Ask which service records or decision letters to bring. The office also posts opportunities for one-on-one assistance at senior centers. [Knox County Veterans Services](https://www.knoxcounty.org/veterans/)
