Call **865-524-0319** with the pickup address, destination, appointment time and return plan. Knox County CAC Transit serves county residents outside Knoxville Area Transit’s fixed-route coverage and city residents whose trip the fixed routes do not serve. Staff can check the trip and vehicle availability. The operator lists medical visits, groceries, work and other eligible trips. [CAC Transit service area and ride guide](https://www.knoxcac.org/transportation/)

Use the booking cutoff under **How to Schedule Transportation**. Speak with a representative to reserve the trip, especially before a weekend or holiday. If you need someone to stay with you during an appointment or errand, ask about **Volunteer Assisted Transportation**. It has a separate rider application and depends on a volunteer and vehicle being available. [Reservations and assisted transportation](https://www.knoxcac.org/transportation/)
