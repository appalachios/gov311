If you have a court date, start with the notice. The Knox County General Sessions Civil clerk is at **865-215-2518** for scheduling questions. Keep the scheduled hearing unless the court changes it. Applying for mediation or legal assistance does not change the date. [County eviction instructions](https://knoxhousingassistance.org/eviction-diversion) · [Civil Sessions Court](https://www.knoxcounty.org/civil/)

For legal help anywhere in Knox County, call Legal Aid of East Tennessee’s **Eviction Prevention Program at 865-371-8730**. If your court date is within 14 days, call the Knoxville intake office directly at **865-637-0484**. For help working out an agreement with your landlord, the county lists the **Community Mediation Center at 865-594-1879**. These contacts also serve people in Knoxville and Farragut. [Legal Aid intake](https://laet.org/contact-us/) · [County legal and mediation contacts](https://knoxhousingassistance.org/eviction-diversion)

The **Knox County Eviction Diversion Program** has a narrower service area. To apply, you must rent in Knox County **outside both Knoxville and Farragut**, be at risk of losing your housing within 21 days and meet one of the federal HOME-ARP program’s qualifying categories. The county links to a map to check your address, the federal eligibility definitions and the dates when applications are open. [Check your address, eligibility and application window](https://knoxhousingassistance.org/eviction-diversion)
