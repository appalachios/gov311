Start with the court name and case number on the citation, notice or filing. The County Clerk who handles vehicle tags is a different office.

- **Circuit Court.** Call the Circuit Court Clerk’s Office at **865-215-2400** for its dockets and filing instructions. [Circuit Court Clerk](https://www.knoxcounty.org/circuit/)
- **General Sessions Civil.** For an eviction hearing or other Civil Sessions case, call **865-215-2518**. [Civil Sessions](https://www.knoxcounty.org/civil/) · [County eviction hearing instructions](https://knoxhousingassistance.org/eviction-diversion)
- **Criminal Court, General Sessions Criminal or Fourth Circuit.** The Criminal Court Clerk maintains the records for all three. Call **865-215-2375** or use the court-date and payment tools on the clerk’s site. For a citation, first check the court printed on it. [Criminal Court Clerk](https://criminalcourt.knoxcounty.org/)
- **Chancery or probate.** Call **865-215-2555** for Chancery or **865-215-2389** for its Probate Division. The probate office is in Suite 352, 400 Main Street. [Chancery departments](https://www.knoxcounty.org/chancery/departments.php)
- **Juvenile Court.** Use the Juvenile Court office at **865-215-6400** for its procedures and directions. [Juvenile Court](https://www.knoxcounty.org/juvenilecourt/)
