Use the district map to find your Knox County commissioner. The two at-large commissioners represent the whole county. The directory lists how to reach each member. [District map](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/district-map/) · [Commissioner directory](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/people/)

For an agenda, a proposed ordinance or help signing up to speak, call the Commission Office at **865-215-2534** or email **Commission@knoxcounty.org**. Give the meeting date and the item number or subject if you have it. Check the notice for the meeting time and location. [Commission contacts](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/contact/) · [Meeting notices](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/notices/)

For the vote or adopted text, use the [county measure guide](../services/follow-a-county-commission-measure.md). For a Knoxville City Council or Farragut Board of Mayor and Aldermen item, use the city or town's meeting records.
