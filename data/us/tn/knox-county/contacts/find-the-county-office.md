Start with the street address or parcel ID in **KGIS Maps**. Check whether the land is in Knoxville, Farragut or unincorporated Knox County before choosing a permit office. You can also check the city limits on the state’s municipal boundary map. [KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/map.htm) · [How to look up a Knox parcel](../services/find-a-parcel-in-kgis.md) · [Municipal boundary map](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/pa/gisredistricting/municipal-boundaries.html)

Countywide offices still serve addresses inside the municipalities. The county clerk handles vehicle tags, the election commission handles county voter records, and the register keeps deeds for Knox County land. Near Farragut’s western boundary, first confirm the parcel’s county. The state lists part of town in Loudon County. [Knox County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/knox) · [State certified municipal figures](https://tnecd.com/?force_download=129209) · [Loudon County land records](../../loudon-county/services/find-property-records.md)

If you need a department and cannot identify it from the directory, the Knox County Mayor’s Office is at **865-215-2005**.
