The county clerk’s downtown office and five branches handle registration renewals, marriage applications, business license applications and notary applications. For a driver license renewal or a passport, choose a branch that lists that service. The East office drive-through takes only renewals and disabled placards. Check current branch locations and counter hours before leaving. [Clerk branch directory](https://www.knoxcounty.org/clerk/location.php)

For records, identify the transaction. The county clerk can provide a copy of a marriage license issued in Knox County. A case file or traffic citation belongs with the [appropriate court clerk](court-clerks.md). A birth certificate belongs with the [health department](../services/get-a-birth-or-death-certificate.md).
