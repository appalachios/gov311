Search the street address or parcel ID in KGIS. Check whether it falls in Knoxville, Farragut or unincorporated Knox County. A Knoxville mailing address can reach outside the city limits. Knoxville and the county have separate zoning codes. Farragut administers zoning within its town limits. [KGIS Maps](https://www.kgis.org/kgismaps/) · [Planning's links to all three codes](https://knoxplanning.org/zoning/regulations)

For an unincorporated parcel, use its zoning district to look up allowed uses, setbacks and other requirements in **Appendix A** of the Knox County Code. Check the code's list of recently adopted ordinances and any conditions in the parcel's rezoning record. The Comprehensive Plan gives the county a basis for future land-use decisions. A proposed zoning map is a different document again. For example, the Advance Knox project page says work on Draft Map 2 was paused. Use the enacted code and the parcel's current zoning to check what is allowed today. [County zoning ordinance](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICO_APXAZO) · [Planning process](https://knoxplanning.org/planning-process) · [Advance Knox draft map status](https://advanceknox.org/udo/)

**How much newspaper notice does a county rezoning get?** Planning's process page lists 12 days for its hearing and 15 for the County Commission. On September 21, 2026, the County Commission adopted O-26-8-101. Exhibit A, §§ 6.30 and 6.40, requires at least **21 days** of newspaper notice before each body's hearing on a county zoning map or text amendment. The ordinance has separate rules for other kinds of notice. [Signed ordinance and Exhibit A](https://mcclibraryfunctions.azurewebsites.us/api/ordinanceDownload/12815/1448284/pdf) · [September zoning minutes](https://knoxcounty.org/clerk/CommMinutes/2026/09-21-2026_zs.pdf) · [Planning's process page](https://knoxplanning.org/commission/rezoning-process)

For a possible violation in the unincorporated county, give Codes Administration the property address and enough detail to identify the site. Call 865-215-2325. To track a proposed change, use the [county rezoning guide](../services/follow-a-rezoning.md). [County zoning complaints](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/zoning.php)
