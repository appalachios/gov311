Knox County has its own charter. In the county code, open **Part I, Charter** for the government's powers and **Part II, Code** for ordinances organized by subject. Compare the date of the published supplement with the list of adopted ordinances awaiting inclusion. The County Clerk keeps the numbered ordinance and resolution books and can provide a copy. [County code and new ordinances](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances) · [Charter § 2.09](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTICH_ARTIILEBR_S2.09OREMORRE)

A *reading* means the chair or clerk reads the caption aloud and makes the text available at the meeting. The charter requires one subject per measure and requires an amending caption to identify the measure it changes. For an ordinary ordinance, expect two readings and approval by a majority of the full commission. It ordinarily takes effect 15 days after final passage. A resolution generally takes one reading. Follow the actual motion and any amendments to know which version passed. [Charter §§ 2.09 and 9.03](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTICH_ARTIILEBR_S2.09OREMORRE) · [Follow a county measure](../services/follow-a-county-commission-measure.md)

**Emergency ordinances.** The Commission's summary says a majority can enact one and describes an eight-day wait. Section 2.09(C) calls for two-thirds of the commission's membership unless applicable state law conflicts. A properly scheduled emergency ordinance can pass on one reading. Unanimous consent of members present and voting can add one at a meeting for a reading, with adoption at a second reading at the next meeting. The mayor's signature can put it into effect earlier than day eight. [Charter § 2.09(C)](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTICH_ARTIILEBR_S2.09OREMORRE) · [Commission summary](https://commission.knoxcountytn.gov/ordinances/)

**Vetoes and exceptions.** For most measures, check the mayor's action after the vote. A veto override generally requires a majority of the commission's membership plus one. If an ordinance originally needed two-thirds, the override also needs two-thirds. Measures affecting zoning regulations and the borrowing resolutions specified in § 2.10(A) take effect on commission adoption. [Charter § 2.10](https://library.municode.com/tn/knox_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTICH_ARTIILEBR_S2.10SIVEMAKNCO)
