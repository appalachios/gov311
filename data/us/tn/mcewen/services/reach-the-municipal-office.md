The municipal directory lists **9586 Highway 70 East, McEwen, TN 37101** as the contact address for McEwen. Call **(931) 582-6211** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mcewen)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mcewen)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Humphreys County records](../../humphreys-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
