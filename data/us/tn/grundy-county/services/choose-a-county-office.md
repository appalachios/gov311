For county records or services, start with the office responsible for the record. The Grundy County directory lists county officials and office contacts. [County offices](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/grundy)

- **County Clerk.** For vehicle titles, registration and marriage licenses, call **(931) 692-3622**.

- **Register of Deeds.** For recorded deeds, plats and easements, call **(931) 692-3621**.

- **Assessor of Property.** For property descriptions and assessments, call **(931) 692-3596**.

- **Trustee.** For county property tax bills and payment records, call **(931) 692-3369**.

- **Administrator of Elections.** For voter registration, precincts and election questions, call **(931) 692-3551**.

For a local permit or ordinance, first check which government has authority at the address. The municipal governments covering land in Grundy County include Altamont, Beersheba Springs, Coalmont, Gruetli-Laager, Monteagle, Palmer, Tracy City.
