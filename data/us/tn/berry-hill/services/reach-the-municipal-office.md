The municipal directory lists **698 Thompson Lane, Nashville, TN 37204** as the contact address for Berry Hill. Call **(615) 292-5531** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/berry-hill) · [Municipal website](https://www.berryhilltn.gov/)
