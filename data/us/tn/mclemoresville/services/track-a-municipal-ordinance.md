McLemoresville’s charter gives the board authority to pass ordinances. It sets no number of readings. Tennessee’s default allows the board to adopt an ordinance on one reading unless an applicable law or an adopted local rule requires more. The town’s signed ethics ordinance records passage by roll-call vote, but it does not list how many times the board considered it. [Charter §§ 10 to 12](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/McLemoresville_cht.pdf#page=5) · [Tennessee ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/procedures-adopting-ordinances) · [Town ethics ordinance, last page](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/ethicscommission/documents/local-gov-municipalities/McLemoresville.pdf)

The board meets in regular session on the second Tuesday each month. The mayor may call a special session, or three board members may request one in writing. Call the recorder at (731) 986-9440 for the standing rule on readings, the agenda, the proposed text and the minutes for the meeting where the board acted. [Charter § 10](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/McLemoresville_cht.pdf#page=5) · [Town office and meeting schedule](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mclemoresville)
