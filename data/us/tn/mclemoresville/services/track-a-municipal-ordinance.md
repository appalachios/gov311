To follow a proposal in McLemoresville, get the dated meeting notice, agenda, proposed text and attachments. The municipality reports its regular meeting schedule as “2nd Tuesday at 7:30 PM at Town Hall.” Confirm the date and location before attending. [Meeting schedule and municipal officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mclemoresville)

Look up the charter for the governing body’s powers, readings and vote requirements. Compare a proposed ordinance with the current code section it would change. [Municipal Code](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/mclemoresville) · [Municipal Charter](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/mclemoresville)

After the meeting, use the minutes to identify the motion that actually passed. Ask the recorder or clerk for the adopted ordinance, any amendments and the effective date. A proposal can change during consideration, and a recent ordinance may still be waiting to appear in the code. [Tennessee agenda and minutes requirements](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-open-meetings-act-faqs.html)

Charter § 10 calls for regular session on the second Tuesday of the month. The mayor can call a special session, and three board members can request one in writing. Check the special-session minutes as well as the regular minutes for the vote on a pending ordinance. [Charter classification](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/charter/mclemoresville) · [Governing text](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/McLemoresville_cht.pdf#page=5)
