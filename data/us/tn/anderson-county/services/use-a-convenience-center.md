Open the county’s solid waste page and find the center that takes your material. Check its current hours. The county lists different destinations for ordinary recycling, appliances, electronics and used oil. Glass bottles are excluded from convenience-center recycling. For a chemical or other hazardous material, look for the collection event and its accepted items or call **865-463-6845** before taking it anywhere. [Center locations, accepted materials and collection events](https://andersoncountytn.gov/solid-waste-recycling/)
