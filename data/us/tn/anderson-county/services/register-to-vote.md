Use the county election office’s online registration link or its paper registration form. Check the election deadline before mailing. If you moved, give the office your new residence address and ask it to verify the precinct and districts. The Anderson County Election Commission lists **865-457-6238** for registration questions. For a home on the other side of a county line in Oak Ridge, Rocky Top or Oliver Springs, use the county of residence. [Election office](https://andersoncountytn.gov/election-commission/) · [Registration instructions and deadlines](https://www.acelection.com/voter-registration/)
