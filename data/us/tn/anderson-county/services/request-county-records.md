Identify the office, dates and record you want. Say whether you want to inspect it or receive copies. The county supplies a fillable form, its policy and an email contact. For mail, address the Public Records Request Coordinator, 100 N Main Street, Room 102, Clinton, TN 37716. The county asks for proof of Tennessee residency. If you are looking for a city council vote, request the municipal record from that city. [County public records policy, form and delivery instructions](https://andersoncountytn.gov/humanresources/public-records-request/)
