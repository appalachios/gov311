Find the parcel in the online mapping system linked by Planning and Development and check its zoning and boundary. For a project outside municipal limits call **865-457-6244** or visit Room 127, 100 North Main Street in Clinton. Give the parcel ID and describe what you plan to build or change. Ask which permits or approvals you need before starting. The office posts separate agendas for the Planning Commission and Board of Zoning Appeals. For a parcel inside an incorporated city or town, start with its permit office.
