Open the date on the Commission’s agenda page. Check the regular agenda and the consent agenda. Open any budget or purchasing attachment named in the item. To speak, check the Appearance of Citizens instructions for the time limit and how to address the commission. Check the calendar for special meetings.

For the outcome, search the motion database by subject, resolution number or meeting date. On May 18, 2026, for example, the database lists a motion on floodplain zoning resolution 25-11-1242. A motion referring an item to a committee and a vote approving a resolution are different actions. The county labels this database an addendum. Ask the County Clerk for the official minutes, the wording adopted, amendments and the effective date if you need the rule that applies to a property.
