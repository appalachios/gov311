East Tennessee Human Resource Agency (ETHRA) accepts Anderson County applications for the Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program. Apply through the state portal linked on its page, or call the local office at **865-457-3352, extension 5007**. If you have a cutoff notice, say so when you call. ETHRA accepts qualifying crisis applications throughout the month and lists a narrower window for regular assistance. Ask the utility about the disconnection date while the application is pending. An approved subsidy goes directly to the provider. [Eligibility, application portal and Anderson County contact](https://www.ethra.org/programs/26/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap/)
