The municipal directory lists **1270 Old Hwy 51 S., P.O. Box 277, Brighton, TN 38011** as the contact address for Brighton. Call **(901) 476-8661** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brighton) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofbrighton.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/brighton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Tipton County records](../../tipton-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Pay Water Bill**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Pay Water Bill](https://www.townofbrighton.com/departments/water_department/pay_water_bill.php)
- **Building/Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building/Codes](https://www.townofbrighton.com/departments/building_codes.php)
- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.townofbrighton.com/residents/report_a_concern.php)
