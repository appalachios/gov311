The municipal directory lists **120 Melton Drive, P.O. Box 188, Sunbright, TN 37872** as the contact address for Sunbright. Call **(423) 628-5260** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sunbright) · [Municipal website](https://www.cityofsunbright.net/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sunbright)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Morgan County records](../../morgan-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
