The municipal directory lists **700 Milton Counce Road, South Fulton, TN 38257** as the contact address for South Fulton. Call **(731) 479-2151** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-fulton) · [Municipal website](https://www.southfultontn.com/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-fulton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Obion County records](../../obion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
