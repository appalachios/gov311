Contact Town Hall at **931-639-9448** for the next Board of Mayor and Aldermen agenda and the proposal you want to read. The UT municipal directory lists Town Hall at 105 Division Street. Ask for the ordinance title and both reading dates. [Town contacts and meeting details](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/normandy)

The charter allows first-reading minutes to identify an ordinance by title or subject without printing the body. Passage requires votes on two different days. Except for the budget ordinance, a substantial amendment on final passage must go through the procedure for amending an existing ordinance. Ask the recorder for the signed, numbered version. It takes effect on final passage unless the ordinance sets a later date. [Town charter, Ordinance procedure](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/sites/default/files/private/charters/Normandy_cht.pdf)
