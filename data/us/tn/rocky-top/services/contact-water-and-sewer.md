Check the utility name on the bill. For a Rocky Top municipal account, start at **Water and Sewer** for questions about the service and at **Pay Water Bill** to make a payment. Have the account number and service address handy. For a leak or line break, use the reporting link on the city site so the request goes to the utility crew.
