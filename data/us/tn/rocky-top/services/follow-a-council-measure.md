Check the city’s announcements before going to City Hall. The code schedules regular council meetings, but the September 2026 announcement moved the meeting from September 17 to September 22. Ask City Hall at **865-426-2838** for the dated council agenda, proposed ordinance and attachments.

In the minutes collection, choose **City Council Minutes** or **Special Called Minutes** for the vote. The city also lists public hearing and beer board records there. Read the final motion and request the signed ordinance and effective date from the recorder. Use the charter for council authority and the municipal code for the published local rules.
