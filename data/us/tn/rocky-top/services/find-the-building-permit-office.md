Give Codes Administration the street address, parcel number and proposed work. Its responsibilities include building permits and inspections, manufactured homes and signs, property maintenance and floodplain administration. For an existing concern, identify the site and describe the condition. For a county land record, first check whether the property is in Anderson or Campbell County. Rocky Top has land in both.
