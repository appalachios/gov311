The municipal directory lists **116 West Harper, P.O. Box 246, Troy, TN 38260** as the contact address for Troy. Call **(731) 536-4745** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/troy) · [Municipal website](https://www.troytn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/troy)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Obion County records](../../obion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
