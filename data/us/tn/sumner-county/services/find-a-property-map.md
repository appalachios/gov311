Open the **Tennessee Property Viewer** and choose **Sumner**. Search by address, owner name or parcel ID. Select a result to see the parcel outline and open **Property Assessment Data** for the assessor’s record. [Tennessee Property Viewer](https://tnmap.tn.gov/assessment/)

The Sumner County Property Assessor also publishes a local GIS site. Open the assessor’s GIS page for the current public map. [Sumner County GIS](https://sumnercountytn.gov/departments/assessor-of-property/sumner-county-gis/)

**Tax bills.** For the amount due or a payment record, give the parcel ID and tax year to the Sumner County Trustee at **(615) 452-1260**. [County office directory](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/sumner)

**Who represents this address?** Enter your residential address in the Comptroller’s voter district lookup. Use the local district number and the county directory to find your elected officials. [Voter district lookup](https://tn-cot.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/lookup/index.html?appid=2bd3c9b349634d2ba7153410c0930d27) · [Sumner County officials](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/county/sumner)

**Schools.** One district contact in the state school directory is **Sumner County Schools**. Ask which district serves your address, especially near a city or county line. Give the district the home address, grade and school year to confirm the assigned school. [Tennessee School Directory](https://tnschooldirectory.tnedu.gov/)
