The municipal directory lists **8 Main Street, P.O. Box 97, Stanton, TN 38069** as the contact address for Stanton. Call **(731) 548-2565** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/stanton) · [Municipal website](https://stantontn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/stanton)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Haywood County records](../../haywood-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Office of the Town Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [Office of the Town Recorder](https://stantontn.gov/government/administration/)
- **Building & Codes**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building & Codes](https://stantontn.gov/government/building-codes/)
- **Trash Service**. Check the collection or disposal instructions for the material you have. [Trash Service](https://stantontn.gov/government/public-works/trash-service/)
