The municipal directory lists **1735 Main Street, P.O. Box 67, Surgoinsville, TN 37873-0067** as the contact address for Surgoinsville. Call **(423) 345-2213** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/surgoinsville) · [Municipal website](https://www.surgoinsvilletn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/surgoinsville)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Hawkins County records](../../hawkins-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
