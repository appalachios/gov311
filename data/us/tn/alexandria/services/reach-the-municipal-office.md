The municipal directory lists **102 High Street, P.O. Box 277, Alexandria, TN 37012** as the contact address for Alexandria. Call **(615) 529-2171** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/alexandria) · [Municipal website](https://www.townofalexandria.us/index.html)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [DeKalb County records](../../dekalb-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
