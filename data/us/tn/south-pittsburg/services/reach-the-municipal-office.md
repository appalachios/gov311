The municipal directory lists **204 W. 3rd Street, South Pittsburg, TN 37380** as the contact address for South Pittsburg. Call **(423) 837-5012** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-pittsburg) · [Municipal website](https://www.southpittsburg-tn.org/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Garbage, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/south-pittsburg)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Marion County records](../../marion-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Building Inspection**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Building Inspection](https://www.southpittsburg-tn.org/departments/building_inspection.php)
