The municipal directory lists **PO Box 82, 101 Park Road, Luttrell, TN 37779** as the contact address for Luttrell. Call **(865) 992-0870** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/luttrell)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/luttrell)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Union County records](../../union-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
