The municipal directory lists **27960 Broad St., P.O. Box 247, Hollow Rock, TN 38342** as the contact address for Hollow Rock. Call **(731) 586-7773** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hollow-rock)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/hollow-rock)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Carroll County records](../../carroll-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)
