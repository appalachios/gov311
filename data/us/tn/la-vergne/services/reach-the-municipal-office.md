The municipal directory lists **5093 Murfreesboro Road, La Vergne, TN 37086** as the contact address for La Vergne. Call **(615) 793-6295** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/la-vergne) · [Municipal website](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/la-vergne)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. [Rutherford County records](../../rutherford-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

## More municipal services

- **Report a Concern**. Submit a description of the issue and its location through the city’s reporting page. [Report a Concern](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/requesttracker.aspx)
- **Public Records**. Find the instructions for requesting an existing municipal record. [Public Records](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/374/Public-Records)
- **City Recorder**. Use the municipal office contact information to ask where a city record is kept. [City Recorder](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/173/City-Recorder)
- **Water Billing**. Use the billing page to find payment instructions for your account. [Water Billing](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/385/Water-Billing)
- **Post-Construction Inspections**. Check which permits or inspections apply to your project before starting work. [Post-Construction Inspections](https://www.lavergnetn.gov/669/Post-Construction-Inspections)
