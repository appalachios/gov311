The municipal directory lists **203 Monroe Street, PO Box 267, Sweetwater, TN 37874** as the contact address for Sweetwater. Call **(423) 337-6979** to confirm where to file or visit. [Municipal office and officials](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sweetwater) · [Municipal website](https://www.sweetwatertn.gov/)

The municipality reports these services in its utility listing: **Electric, Gas, Sewer, Water**. Give the service address and ask whether the municipal system serves that location, how to start service and where to report a problem. [Municipal utilities listing](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/directories/cities/sweetwater)

For a county assessment or recorded deed, use the county where the land sits. The municipality extends into Monroe, McMinn. [Monroe County records](../../monroe-county/services/find-a-property-map.md) · [McMinn County records](../../mcminn-county/services/find-a-property-map.md)

The Census place-by-county table lists Sweetwater in Monroe, McMinn. The 2026 Census TIGER/Line place and county polygons and the Tennessee Comptroller’s current city and county map also show land in each of these counties. Use the parcel’s county for county services. [Boundary source](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/reference/codes2020/national_place_by_county2020.txt)
