Open **How to Start a Business** on the department’s [small business guides page](https://www.tn.gov/ecd/small-business/bero/pdfs-you-can-use-now.html) for a flowchart of registration, permits, and sources of help. The same page links to the online **TN SmartStart** guide for financing and mentorship.

If your next step is filing, see [form a Tennessee LLC](../services/form-tennessee-llc.md) or [register for business tax](../services/register-for-business-tax.md). For questions about starting a business, the department directs readers to the Comptroller’s Small Business Advocate at smallbusiness.advocate@cot.tn.gov.
