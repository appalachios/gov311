If you need somewhere to stay, open the Tennessee Emergency Management Authority's shelter map. Select a location and read its notes before traveling. The map distinguishes overnight shelters from storm shelters and warming centers, and hours vary by site. The shelter page also links to local emergency management agencies for help. [Tennessee shelters](https://www.tn.gov/tema/ready-tn/tennessee-shelters.html)

For food, temporary housing, or help clearing debris, choose the need that fits your situation on TEMA's recovery page. You can also call **211** in most parts of Tennessee for local referrals. Some programs open only for a particular disaster. Check the responsible agency's update for your area before applying. [Recovery resources by need](https://www.tn.gov/tema/assistance/assistance-for-individuals/recovery-resources--find-help-by-need.html)
