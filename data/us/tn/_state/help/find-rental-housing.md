**To find an apartment**, search [TNHousingSearch.org](https://www.tnhousingsearch.org/) by city, county, or ZIP code, or call the rental search line at 1-877-428-8844. The Tennessee Housing Development Agency (THDA) sponsors the service. Contact the property manager to ask about vacancies and how to apply. [Rental search service](https://tnhousingsearch.org/About.html) · [HUD’s Tennessee housing guide](https://www.hud.gov/states/tennessee)

**To apply for a Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)**, find the public housing agency that serves the area where you want to live. If THDA runs the program there, follow **Wait List Openings and Closings** on [THDA’s voucher notice](https://www.tnhousingsearch.org/press/articles/HCVProgram.html) to check whether it is taking applications. For another housing agency, use the **Public Housing Authority contacts by State** link on [HUD’s Tennessee page](https://www.hud.gov/states/tennessee).
