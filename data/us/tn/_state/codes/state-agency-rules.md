If an agency refers you to a regulation, get its name or rule number. Open the [State Library and Archives’ guide to Secretary of State publications](https://digitaltennessee.tnsos.gov/sos/) and choose **Rules and Regulations by Month** for the official rules in effect. The [effective rules page](https://sos.tn.gov/publications/services/effective-rules-and-regulations-by-month) turned away the automated reader when this guide was prepared. You can locate a rule by agency in [Cornell’s unofficial compilation](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/tennessee) and confirm the current text in the state’s publication.

If you have heard that an agency *plans* to change a rule, choose **Administrative Register** on the [same publications page](https://digitaltennessee.tnsos.gov/sos/). It includes proposed rules, hearing notices, and emergency rules. Check the kind of filing you are reading and then look up the effective rule before relying on it. For a proposal in the House or Senate, [search the General Assembly’s bills](../services/track-a-bill.md).

To comment on a proposed rule, open the agency’s hearing notice and match the rule number to the subject you care about. Look for the meeting details, where to send written comments, and when the agency needs them. For example, the State Board of Education’s April 2026 hearing notice identified several rules and gave a separate deadline for written comments before the meeting. Use the notice for the hearing you plan to attend.
