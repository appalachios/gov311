If an agency refers you to a regulation, get its name or rule number. Open the [State Library and Archives’ guide to Secretary of State publications](https://digitaltennessee.tnsos.gov/sos/) and choose **Rules and Regulations by Month** for the official rules in effect. The [effective rules page](https://sos.tn.gov/publications/services/effective-rules-and-regulations-by-month) turned away the automated reader when this guide was prepared. Use the link in a browser to read the text.

If you have heard that an agency *plans* to change a rule, choose **Administrative Register** on the [same publications page](https://digitaltennessee.tnsos.gov/sos/). It includes proposed rules, hearing notices, and emergency rules. Check the kind of filing you are reading and then look up the effective rule before relying on it. For a proposal in the House or Senate, [search the General Assembly’s bills](../services/track-a-bill.md).
