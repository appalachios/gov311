1. Find the facility on the Tennessee Department of Correction’s [visitation page](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/correction/state-prisons/visitation.html). Check its notices for cancellations and look up its contact information.
2. Complete the [visitation application](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/correction/documents/forms/CR2152.pdf) and send it to the prison where the person is housed. The department’s visitation page has separate instructions for adults and minors. The prison tells the person in custody whether the application was approved, and that person lets the visitor know.
3. After approval, contact the facility to make an appointment. Check its current visiting schedule and entry instructions before you travel. [Facility schedules and contacts](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/correction/state-prisons/visitation.html)
