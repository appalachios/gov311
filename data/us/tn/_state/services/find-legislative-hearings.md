1. [Look up the bill](track-a-bill.md). Read the latest House and Senate actions and note the committee, chamber, and meeting date if one is listed. Use the legislature’s [abbreviation list](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/About/Abbreviations) to decode a committee name or action.
2. Open [Schedules and Calendars](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Schedule/). Select the week, find the meeting, and open its **Calendar/Agenda**. Look for the bill number or its companion. The listing gives the time and location and has a **View Video** column for meetings with video.
3. After the meeting, return to the bill history. An agenda tells you what members planned to consider. The history tells you what they did. Use **Votes** for a recorded committee or floor vote. The [legislature’s FAQ](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/Help/Index) explains how to read a voice vote when individual votes are not listed.

To contact a legislator before the next vote, [look up your senator or representative](../contacts/find-your-legislator.md).

To ask to speak at a **House** committee meeting, use the House witness card. Enter the committee, meeting date, and bill or resolution number. Choose your position and say whether you want to speak. Check the submission deadline on the form. The House can limit testimony even when a request arrives on time. [House Witness Card Submission Form](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/Apps/HouseWitness/)

For a **Senate** hearing, open the committee named on the agenda and use its contact information to ask how to provide input on that bill. [Senate committee directory](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/CommitteeInfo/AllSenate)
