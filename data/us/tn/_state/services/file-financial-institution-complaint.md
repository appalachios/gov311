1. Raise the issue with the bank, credit union, or lender first. If it remains unresolved, check which regulator handles that institution. Consumer Resources lists federal contacts and takes questions at 800-778-4215. [Regulator directory](https://www.tn.gov/tdfi/consumer-resources.html) · [Department contacts](https://www.tn.gov/tdfi/contact-us.html)
2. For an institution the state regulates, submit a written complaint and attach your supporting documents in the online form. Use the form for confidential records. You can also call 800-778-4215 for a paper form. [Consumer complaint form](https://stateoftennessee.formstack.com/forms/consumer_complaint) · [Filing and mailing instructions](https://www.tn.gov/tdfi/tdfi-how-do-i/file-a-complaint.html)

An examiner reviews your complaint and may ask for more information. In most cases, the department sends the complaint to the institution for a written response before it follows up with you. [How the department reviews complaints](https://www.tn.gov/tdfi/tdfi-how-do-i/file-a-complaint.html)
