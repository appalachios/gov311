1. Gather the proposed company name, principal office address, member count, and the name and address of the registered agent who will receive legal documents for the company. The Secretary of State explains each field and the fee in the form instructions. [Articles of organization, form SS-4270 (PDF)](https://sos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com/s3fs-public/document/SS-4270%20LLC_0.pdf?VersionId=SHQcvmBKLxqwiWe3Ficu3Iv3gOHscm6X)
2. To file online, create an account in Tennessee's Charity and Business Filing System (TNCaB), verify your email, and sign in. Complete the articles and pay the filing fee. Once the state accepts the filing, you can retrieve your documents under **My Completed Filings**. [TNCaB filing portal](https://tncab.tnsos.gov/portal)
3. If you prefer paper, use the mailing or walk-in instructions on SS-4270. Call the Secretary of State at (615) 741-2286 to request a blank form. [Filing instructions (PDF)](https://sos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com/s3fs-public/document/SS-4270%20LLC_0.pdf?VersionId=SHQcvmBKLxqwiWe3Ficu3Iv3gOHscm6X)

The Department of Revenue handles business tax registration separately. [Register for Tennessee business tax](register-for-business-tax.md)
