1. Gather the dates you worked for each employer, what you earned, and why each job ended. The state shows the questions you will see on the claim. [Application questions](https://www.tn.gov/workforce/unemployment/apply-for-benefits-redirect-2/online-application/application-questions.html)
2. Open Jobs4TN and choose **Unemployment Claimant e-Services** to file your claim with the Tennessee Department of Labor and Workforce Development. [Jobs4TN claimant services](https://www.tn.gov/jobs4tn.html)
3. Check your claimant account for messages while the department reviews your claim. Start weekly certification while you are waiting for a decision. This is how you request payment for each week you are still unemployed. The department explains when to start and what happens after you file. [After you file](https://lwdsupport.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/26099211261203-What-are-the-next-steps-after-I-file-my-unemployment-claim)

For help with an unemployment claim, call (844) 224-5818 and choose option 1. The help desk answers Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Central time. [Unemployment help desk](https://www.tn.gov/workforce/contact/unemployment.html)

If the department denies benefits and you disagree, open the determination in your Unemployment Claimant e-Services account and choose **File Appeal**. Check the department’s filing deadline as soon as you receive the decision. You can check the hearing schedule and outcome in the same account. [Appeal an agency decision](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/workforce/unemployment/appeal-an-agency-decision.html)
