Start with the Attorney General’s Civil Rights Enforcement Division if you want Tennessee to review a discrimination complaint. The state transferred this work from the former Tennessee Human Rights Commission. The division explains which issues it handles, including retaliation for reporting discrimination. [About the division](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/cred/about-cred.html)

1. Read the filing deadline on the [complaint instructions](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/cred/cred-s-complaint-process.html). If you are also considering a lawsuit, check the separate court deadline on that page before you wait for a response.
2. Use **file a complaint** on the instructions page to open the division’s [complaint form](https://stateoftennessee.formstack.com/forms/cred). Keep the confirmation and case number you receive.
3. The division reviews whether your complaint falls within its authority. It may contact you for an intake interview. The [complaint process](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/cred/cred-s-complaint-process.html) explains what can happen after intake.
