For a problem with care at a facility, open the Health Facilities Commission’s [complaint instructions](https://www.tn.gov/hfc/division-of-licensure-and-regulation/filing-a-complaint.html) and select **Public Records and Complaints**. You can also call the Complaint Intake Unit at **1-877-287-0010** or download a form and mail it. The page has a separate form for complaints about unlicensed facilities.

The intake unit reviews the complaint and sends it to the regional office for investigation. If your concern is about a licensed doctor, nurse, or other individual professional, use **File a Complaint Against an Individual Health Care Professional** on the same page to reach the Department of Health. For help with a resident’s rights in long-term care, the page lists the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman.
