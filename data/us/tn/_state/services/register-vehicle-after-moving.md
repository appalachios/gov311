1. Open Revenue's initial registration instructions and read the **New Residents** section. Have your current out-of-state registration ready. If you make payments on the car, have the lender's name and address. The same page explains when to bring your out-of-state title and which documents establish identity and residency. [Initial registration](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/title-and-registration/vehicle-registration/initial-registration.html)
2. Find the clerk for the Tennessee county where you live. Contact that office for local fees and take your paperwork there to apply for the title and registration. [County clerk directory](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/title-and-registration/county-clerks-locations.html) · [State registration fees](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360060097432--VR-4-Registration-Fees)

Driver Services handles your personal driver license separately. [Transfer an out-of-state driver license](transfer-driver-license.md)
