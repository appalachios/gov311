1. Find a grant for your project on the [Tennessee Arts Commission grants page](https://tnartscommission.org/art-grants/). Check the program guidelines for who can apply and what it supports. Operating support for an organization and a fellowship for an individual artist have different application schedules. [Application dates](https://tnartscommission.org/art-grants/apply-for-a-grant/)
2. If this is your first application, register in the commission’s grants system as an individual or an organization. Wait for the email with your login before starting the application. [Registration instructions](https://tnartscommission.org/how-to-register/)
3. Sign in through **Apply Now**, complete the application for that program, and submit it by its deadline. If you need feedback on a draft, the commission offers help before the deadline. [Apply for a grant](https://tnartscommission.org/art-grants/apply-for-a-grant/)
