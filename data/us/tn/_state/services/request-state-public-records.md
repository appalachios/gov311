1. Identify the agency that created or keeps the record. Open **State Contacts for Public Record Requests** in the Comptroller’s [coordinator directory](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/state-contacts-for-record-requests.html) and find that agency.
2. Check the agency’s public records policy for the request form, identification instructions, and charges for copies. Tell the coordinator which records you want and whether you want to inspect them or receive copies. A date range and document name can help identify the record. [Public records FAQ](https://www.comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel/open-meetings/frequently-asked-questions/tennessee-public-records-act-faqs.html)
3. If you have a question about access or a dispute over an existing request, use **Contact Open Records Counsel** on the [Comptroller’s site](https://comptroller.tn.gov/office-functions/open-records-counsel.html). Send the records request itself to the agency that holds the record.
