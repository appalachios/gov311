1. Check the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency's fee schedule for your residency and the kind of fishing you plan to do. The schedule also lists special permits for particular waters. [Fishing licenses, fees, and special permits](https://www.tn.gov/twra/license-sales.html)
2. Open Go Outdoors Tennessee. If you have ever held a Tennessee hunting or fishing license or registered a boat in the state, choose **Login** to find your existing account. New customers can enroll on the same page. [Go Outdoors Tennessee account lookup](https://license.gooutdoorstennessee.com/licensing/customerlookup.aspx)
3. Buy your license in the portal or visit a TWRA regional office or license agent. If you only need another copy of an active license, you can sign in and reprint it for free. [License purchases and reprints](https://www.gooutdoorstennessee.com/) · [In-person fishing license options](https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/)
