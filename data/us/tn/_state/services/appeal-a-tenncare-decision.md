If you received a letter, check what TennCare decided and the date for responding.

- **TennCare denied your application or is ending your coverage.** Call TennCare Connect at 855-259-0701 to file an eligibility appeal, or sign in to your account. Explain what you think TennCare got wrong, such as the income or household size it used. The eligibility page also has a paper form and lists legal aid offices that can help. [Eligibility appeal instructions](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-to-file-an-eligibility-appeal.html)
- **You have TennCare but cannot get care or medicine.** Call TennCare Member Medical Appeals at 1-800-878-3192, file through your account, or use the medical appeal form. This route also covers a service your plan cut back or stopped. If your health could suffer while you wait, ask the medical appeals team about an expedited appeal. [Medical appeal instructions](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-to-file-a-medical-appeal.html) · [Medical appeal form](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/tenncare/documents/medappeal.pdf)

If you mail or fax an appeal, follow the directions on the form for your type of appeal. The two teams use different mailing addresses and fax numbers. Keep a copy and a record of when you sent it. [Eligibility form](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/tenncare/documents/RequestWilsonHearingForm.pdf) · [Medical form](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/tenncare/documents/medappeal.pdf)
