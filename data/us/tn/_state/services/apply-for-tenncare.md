1. Gather information about who lives with you, who needs coverage, income, and other health insurance. TennCare publishes the income and resource limits for each coverage group. [Application checklist](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-do-i-apply-for-tenncare.html) · [Eligibility reference guide](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/eligibility-reference-guide.html)
2. Apply online through TennCare Connect or call 855-259-0701 to apply by phone. If you prefer paper, TennCare provides English and Spanish forms with mailing and fax instructions. [TennCare Connect](https://tenncareconnect.tn.gov/) · [Paper applications and instructions](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-do-i-apply-for-tenncare.html)
3. Check for letters from TennCare and send any information the letter requests. You can read letters and check your application status in your TennCare Connect account. [TennCare application instructions](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-do-i-apply-for-tenncare.html)

If TennCare denies your application, ends your coverage, or takes longer than its published decision period, you can ask for a review. Check the notice and TennCare's instructions for the deadline and how to file. [TennCare eligibility appeals](https://www.tn.gov/tenncare/members-applicants/how-to-file-an-eligibility-appeal.html) · [Appeal a TennCare decision](appeal-a-tenncare-decision.md)
