## Who needs this

State law decides which charitable organizations must register before they solicit contributions in Tennessee, and which are exempt. Gov311 does not restate those rules. The Secretary of State explains them on these pages.

- [Charities frequently asked questions](https://sos.tn.gov/charities/faqs) explains who must register.
- [Which charitable organizations are exempt from registration](https://sos.tn.gov/charities/faqs/what-charitable-organizations-are-exempt-from-registration) lists the exemptions.
- [Exemption request guide](https://sos.tn.gov/charities/guides/charitable-organization-exemption-request-guide) explains the yearly filing that some exempt organizations must make.

## Steps to register

1. Go to the [TNCaB portal](https://tncab.tnsos.gov/portal), the Secretary of State's online filing system for charities.
2. Complete the initial registration. Two officers must sign it, and one of them must be the Chief Fiscal Officer.
3. Attach the financial documents the [initial registration guide](https://sos.tn.gov/charities/guides/initial-registration) requires. Larger organizations must include an audited financial statement, and the guide states the revenue level where that applies.
4. Pay the registration fee. The guide states the current amount.

## Renewing each year

Registration must be renewed every year. The [charities FAQ](https://sos.tn.gov/charities/faqs) states:

- The renewal due date, which depends on when the organization's accounting year ends.
- The renewal fee, which depends on gross revenue.
- The late fee.
- How to request an extension.

Read the due date there and put it on your calendar. Gov311 does not restate fees or deadlines, because they change.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming an exempt organization has nothing to file. Check the exemption request guide.
- Missing the renewal date. The state charges a late fee.
