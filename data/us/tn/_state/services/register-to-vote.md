1. Check what your county already has on file. Enter your county, name, birth month, and birth year in the [Tennessee voter registration lookup](https://tnmap.tn.gov/voterlookup/).
2. To apply online, use **New Voter Registration** if this is your first Tennessee registration or you moved to a different county. Choose **Update Voter Registration** for a move within the same county or a name change in the county where you are registered. [Online voter registration](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/) · [Registration choices](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/Registration/RegistrationDetails)
3. For a paper application, open the Tennessee form under **Register or update registration by mail**. Follow the delivery instructions on the form. Vote.gov also lists the deadlines for each method and where you can register in person. [Tennessee registration options and deadlines](https://vote.gov/register/tennessee)

If you cannot find your registration, use the county election commission link on the [state lookup page](https://tnmap.tn.gov/voterlookup/).
