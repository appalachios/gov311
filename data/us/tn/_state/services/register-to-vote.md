1. Check what your county already has on file. Enter your county, name, birth month, and birth year in the [Tennessee voter registration lookup](https://tnmap.tn.gov/voterlookup/).
2. To apply online, use **New Voter Registration** if this is your first Tennessee registration or you moved to a different county. Choose **Update Voter Registration** for a move within the same county or a name change in the county where you are registered. [Online voter registration](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/) · [Registration choices](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/Registration/RegistrationDetails)
3. To finish online, the state needs to match your electronic signature with the Department of Safety and Homeland Security. If it cannot match your signature or you prefer paper, open the Tennessee form under **Register or update registration by mail** on Vote.gov. Follow the delivery instructions on the form. Vote.gov also lists the deadlines for each method and where you can register in person. [Online signature instructions](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/Registration/RegistrationDetails) · [Paper registration and deadlines](https://vote.gov/register/tennessee)

After you submit, the county election office processes your application. Check your registration in the [state voter lookup](https://tnmap.tn.gov/voterlookup/). If it is missing, use the county election commission link there to follow up. [What happens after submission](https://ovr.govote.tn.gov/Registration/RegistrationDetails)
