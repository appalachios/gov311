Check the provider name on your bill. The next step depends on who runs the utility and what you want it to fix.

- If the Public Utility Commission regulates your provider, use its [utility complaint form](https://www.tn.gov/tpuc/utility-complaint-resources/csd-online-utility-complaint-form.html). If you are unsure, call the commission at 615-741-2904. [Complaint contacts](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/working-for-tennessee/protecting-utility-customers/concerns/file-a-complaint.html)
- If your local government runs the water, sewer, or gas system, use **Utility Inquiry** to ask the Tennessee Board of Utility Regulation about your concern. For a formal ruling, first present your complaint at a regular meeting of your utility’s governing board. If you disagree with its decision, check the filing deadline as soon as you receive it and contact Utilities@cot.tn.gov about filing with the state board. The Comptroller directs water quality or sanitation concerns to the Department of Environment and Conservation. [Comptroller’s filing instructions and deadline](https://comptroller.tn.gov/boards/utilities/utility-customers-information/customer-information.html)
- For an electric account with a local power company served by the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA), contact the power company first. If the account issue remains unresolved, use [TVA’s complaint process](https://crp.tva.gov/). Report a power outage directly to the local power company.
