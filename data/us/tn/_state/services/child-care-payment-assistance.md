Check the department’s Smart Steps waitlist update before applying. Some families get child care assistance through a different route, such as a referral from the Department of Children’s Services. The program descriptions explain which route to use and give the response deadline for a Smart Steps invitation. [Child care payment assistance](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/child-care-services/child-care-payment-assistance.html.html)

If you are on the Smart Steps waitlist, watch your email for an invitation from the department and follow its instructions to apply. For an application through One DHS, choose **Child Care Payment Assistance**, upload the documents requested, and check the portal for your case status. For paper applications, the state lists form HS-3408 and the county offices that accept it. Call 1-833-772-8347 if you need help with your case. [One DHS Customer Portal](https://onedhs.tn.gov/) · [Department application instructions](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/child-care-services/child-care-payment-assistance.html.html)
