Choose a museum program for your students’ grade and the history topic you are teaching. The [museum’s field trip guide](https://tnmuseum.org/field-trips) also lists a separate State Capitol tour and says when requests open for each semester.

Send the [on-site field trip request](https://tnmuseum.org/field-trips-and-reservations). Wait for the scheduling office’s confirmation letter before treating the date and time as reserved. [Field trip scheduling instructions](https://tnmuseum.org/field-trips)

If your class will participate from school, choose a program on the [virtual field trips page](https://tnmuseum.org/virtual-field-trips) and use its **Request Here** link.
