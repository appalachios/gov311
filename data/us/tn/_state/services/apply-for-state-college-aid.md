1. Find your program on the Higher Education Commission's financial aid site. For Tennessee Promise, check the application dates for your graduating class. The program has a state application and also requires the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). [State financial aid](https://www.tn.gov/thec/students-families/financial-aid.html) · [Tennessee Promise application requirements](https://www.collegefortn.org/tennessee-promise-scholarship/)
2. Open the **THEC Student Portal**. The sign-in page also uses the name **TSAC Student Portal**, for the Tennessee Student Assistance Corporation. If you are new, create your account, then sign in. Select **Apply for Scholarships**, choose Tennessee Promise or your other program, and submit the application. Creating an account alone leaves the application unfinished. [Student portal instructions](https://www.collegefortn.org/thecstudentportal/) · [Student sign-in](https://clipslink.tsac.tn.gov/studentsignon/Default.aspx)
3. File the FAFSA with Federal Student Aid for the school year you will attend. For programs that use the FAFSA, including the HOPE Scholarship, you can then check your eligibility in the state portal. For help signing in to the state portal, call **800-342-1663**. [Federal FAFSA application](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) · [State portal and login help](https://www.collegefortn.org/thecstudentportal/)
