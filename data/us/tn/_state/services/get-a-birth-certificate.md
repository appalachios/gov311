If Tennessee Vital Records has registered the birth, any Tennessee county health department can issue a certified copy. Bring identification and check the state’s instructions for documents showing your right to request the record. The instructions also list the Nashville Vital Records counter and its hours. [In-person ordering instructions](https://vitalrecords.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/36323891148435-How-do-I-get-my-certificate-In-Person-Local-County-Health-Department-Mail-or-Online)

To order by mail, complete and sign the birth certificate application. Include the identification the state accepts and a check or money order payable to Tennessee Vital Records. Send them to the address under **By Mail**. [Birth certificate application](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/health/documents/vital-records/PH-1654-Application-For-Certificate-of-Birth.pdf) · [Mailing instructions](https://vitalrecords.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/36323891148435-How-do-I-get-my-certificate-In-Person-Local-County-Health-Department-Mail-or-Online) · [State fees](https://vitalrecords.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/36329848421651-Fees)

To order online, follow the state’s link to VitalChek, its authorized vendor. The vendor collects an additional fee. [Online ordering instructions](https://vitalrecords.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/36323891148435-How-do-I-get-my-certificate-In-Person-Local-County-Health-Department-Mail-or-Online)
