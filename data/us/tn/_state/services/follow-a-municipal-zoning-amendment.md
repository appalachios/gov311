Start with the parcel and the government whose zoning map covers it. For a municipal zoning ordinance or an amendment under Tennessee Code § 13-7-203, find the public hearing before the city’s legislative body. The city must publish the time and place at least **21 calendar days** beforehand in its official municipal journal or a newspaper of general circulation in the municipality. Ask the city recorder for the notice, proposed text, map and hearing date. A planning commission meeting may be a different event. [State law, § 13-7-203(a)](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/TNCodeBills/2025/TNCodeBill_Volume1_2025.pdf#page=298) · [Municipal zoning procedures](https://www.mtas.tennessee.edu/reference/zoning)

**Check which version went to a vote.** If the city proposes a change to the text or map certified by the planning commission, § 13-7-203(b) requires the city to send the change to that commission first. If the commission disapproves, passage requires a majority of the entire membership of the city’s legislative body. Compare the certified version with the motion, roll call and enacted ordinance. For a comprehensive zoning ordinance, subsection (c) permits the city to publish its caption and a complete summary when its charter otherwise requires newspaper publication. Ask for the full adopted text and maps to see what applies to your parcel. [State law, § 13-7-203(b) and (c)](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Archives/Joint/publications/TNCodeBills/2025/TNCodeBill_Volume1_2025.pdf#page=298)

**For county zoning, check the county process.** Tennessee Code § 13-7-105 generally requires at least 15 days’ newspaper notice before the county legislative hearing on an amendment. The county’s own rules can require more. For example, Knox County ordinance O-26-8-101 requires at least 21 days’ newspaper notice before both the Planning Commission hearing and the County Commission hearing on a county zoning amendment. For a variance, permit or other individual application, ask which board hears it and which notice provisions govern that proceeding. [County notice law, § 13-7-105(b)](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/109/Bill/SB1809.pdf) · [Enactment record, Public Chapter 577](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=SB1809&GA=109) · [Knox County’s enacted ordinance](https://mcclibraryfunctions.azurewebsites.us/api/ordinanceDownload/12815/1448284/pdf)
