1. Search for your name or business in Tennessee's unclaimed property records. If you find a match, start a claim from that record. [State unclaimed property search](https://unclaimedproperty.tn.gov/app/claim-search)
2. Check what Treasury needs to connect you to the property. For example, an old utility bill can help establish an address reported by the business that turned over the money. Treasury lists separate instructions for estates, heirs, and businesses. [Proof of ownership](https://treasury.tn.gov/Unclaimed-Property/Claim-Unclaimed-Property/Prove-Your-Ownership)
3. Once you have filed, use the claims portal to upload requested documents or check the status of your claim. For help with an existing claim, call the Unclaimed Property Division at (866) 370-9429. [Claims portal](https://unclaimedproperty.tn.gov/app/claim-status-search) · [Treasury claims contacts](https://treasury.tn.gov/Unclaimed-Property/Claim-Unclaimed-Property/Find-Your-Missing-Money)
