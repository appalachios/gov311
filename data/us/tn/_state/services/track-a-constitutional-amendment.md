When legislators propose changing the Tennessee Constitution, look for a House or Senate **joint resolution** (HJR or SJR). A majority of the elected members of each chamber must approve it in the first General Assembly. In the next elected Assembly it takes two-thirds of the elected members of each chamber to put it on the ballot. Voters decide at the next general election for governor. The governor’s signature is not part of this route. [General Assembly guide to resolutions](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/About/BillToLaw) · [Resolution referring a proposal to voters](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/114/Amend/SA0009.pdf)

1. Select the General Assembly and include **Resolutions** in [Bill Search](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillSearch/BillSearchAdvanced). Search for the subject or the article and section of the Constitution. The same proposal can have a different number in the next Assembly.
2. Match the two records. The proposal about crime victims’ rights in Article I, Section 35 was **HJR 94** in the 113th General Assembly and **SJR 9** in the 114th. The later resolution’s amended opening paragraphs identify the earlier one. Check the action history and votes for each. [HJR 94](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=HJR0094&ga=113) · [SJR 9](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default?BillNumber=SJR0009&ga=114) · [Amended text](https://capitol.tn.gov/Bills/114/Amend/SA0009.pdf)
3. Match the resolution to the ballot by subject and constitutional section. On Hamilton County’s November 2026 sample ballot, the same proposal appeared as **Constitutional Amendment #3**. The ballot gives the proposal and the text voters were asked to approve. [Official sample ballot](https://elect.hamiltontn.gov/Portals/12/Archives/2026/2611StateGeneral/GenSample.html)
4. To find out whether voters approved the change, use **Election Results** on the Secretary of State’s voter site. Check the statewide result and whether it has been certified. To pass, the amendment must receive more yes votes than no votes statewide. The number of yes votes must also exceed half of all votes cast for governor. [GoVoteTN](https://web.go-vote-tn.elections.tn.gov/) · [How Tennessee counts amendment votes](https://www.sequatchiecountytn.gov/news-detail.php?slug=proposed-amendments-on-the-november-2026-ballot)
