1. Tell your supervisor about the work injury promptly. Your employer reports it to the insurance adjuster. The bureau’s guide lists the reporting deadline and links to separate instructions for Tennessee state government employees. [Report a workplace injury](https://www.tn.gov/workforce/injuries-at-work/injured-workers/injured-workers/filing-a-claim.html)
2. For treatment under the claim, ask your employer or adjuster for the list of doctors you can choose from. The bureau calls this list a **panel of physicians**. [Medical care under a claim](https://www.tn.gov/workforce/injuries-at-work/injured-workers/injured-workers/filing-a-claim.html)
3. If your supervisor refuses to take the report or you cannot get the list of doctors, and you do not have an attorney, call the bureau at **800-332-2667**. Ask for an ombudsman who can explain your options and help with the claim. [Help when an employer refuses a claim](https://lwdsupport.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/201353900-My-supervisor-won-t-accept-my-work-injury-claim)
