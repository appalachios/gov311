If you will serve alcohol at a business with a liquor-by-the-drink license, check the commission’s [server permit page](https://www.tn.gov/abc/permitting/server.html) for the requirements and timing that apply to you.

Complete training through a program the commission has approved and file your own permit application through **RLPS**, the state’s licensing system. Finishing the class is one step. The commission still has to issue your permit. The server permit page links to the licensing system and **View Approved Programs**. [Server permit and application](https://www.tn.gov/abc/permitting/server.html)

For help with the online form, open **Server Permits** on the [RLPS tutorials page](https://www.tn.gov/abc/rlps/rlps-tutorials.html) and select the server permit application tutorial. The same page includes instructions for printing an issued permit.
