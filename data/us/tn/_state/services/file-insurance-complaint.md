1. Contact your insurer or agent about the problem. Save the correspondence and note whom you spoke with, when you called, and what they said. [Complaint instructions](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/commerce/insurance/consumer-resources/file-a-complaint/complaint-instructions.html)
2. Check which office handles your coverage. For example, the state directs TennCare complaints to TennCare Oversight and questions about self-insured employer health plans to the U.S. Department of Labor. If the dispute concerns someone else's policy, call Consumer Insurance Services at 615-741-2218 to ask about your options. [Coverage and complaint contacts](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/insurance/consumer-resources/file-a-complaint.html) · [Coverage handled by other offices](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/commerce/insurance/consumer-resources/file-a-complaint/complaint-instructions.html)
3. If your dispute is still open and the state handles your policy, select **Online Insurance Complaint Form** on the instructions page to send the complaint to Consumer Insurance Services. [File a complaint](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/commerce/insurance/consumer-resources/file-a-complaint/complaint-instructions.html)
