1. Contact the business and ask a manager or the owner to resolve the problem. If that fails, read **Who can file a complaint** to check whether the division can handle your dispute. [Consumer Affairs complaint instructions](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/working-for-tennessee/consumer/file-a-complaint.html)
2. Select **Online Complaint** and describe the transaction. Include the business's contact information. If you send supporting documents, use copies and cover sensitive details such as account numbers. The division shares the complaint with the business. [File a consumer complaint](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/working-for-tennessee/consumer/file-a-complaint.html)
3. Keep the emailed copy of your complaint. If the business responds, the division sends you its reply and gives you a chance to respond. Both sides decide whether to accept a resolution. For questions about your filing, call 615-741-4737. [How mediation works](https://www.tn.gov/attorneygeneral/working-for-tennessee/consumer/file-a-complaint.html)

For a dispute about your insurance policy or bank account, start with the regulator for that service. [Insurance complaints](file-insurance-complaint.md) · [Financial institution complaints](file-financial-institution-complaint.md)
