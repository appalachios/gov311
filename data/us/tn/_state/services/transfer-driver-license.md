1. If you moved to Tennessee with a regular driver license from another state, check the transfer deadline and document list. Look up whether you need a driving record from your former state and which tests apply if your license has expired. [New resident instructions](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/safety/driver-services/classd/dlnew.html)
2. You can start the application in e-Services and upload your documents for review. Bring the originals or certified copies to your appointment, even if Driver Services approved them online. [Driver Services application](https://dl.safety.tn.gov/?Link=DLApplication)
3. Choose a full-service Driver Services Center. The location finder lists services, hours, and appointment options. [Driver Services locations](https://www.tn.gov/safety/driver-services/locations.html)

For a Tennessee plate and title for your car, start with the county clerk. [Register a vehicle after moving](register-vehicle-after-moving.md)
