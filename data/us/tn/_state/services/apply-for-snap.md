1. Submit a SNAP application through the One DHS Customer Portal. For a paper application, visit a county family assistance office. [One DHS Customer Portal](https://onedhs.tn.gov/) · [County office directory](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/office-locator-family-assistance.html)
2. Complete the eligibility interview. If you applied online, you can schedule it under **My Benefits** on your dashboard. If you leave scheduling to the department, watch for a call. Most interviews take place by phone. [Interview instructions](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html)
3. Send the documents your caseworker requests. For example, a pay stub can show income and a lease can show rent. Upload photos in the portal or take copies to a county office. The state lists the documents it accepts and when it needs them. [SNAP document checklist](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html)
