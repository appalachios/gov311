1. Apply through the [One DHS Customer Portal](https://onedhs.tn.gov/), or get a paper application from a [county family assistance office](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/office-locator-family-assistance.html). To apply by mail, use the **Forms and Applications** link under **Submit a Paper Application by Mail** and send the completed form to the Cookeville address shown there. [SNAP submission instructions](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html.html)
2. Complete the eligibility interview. If you applied online, you can schedule it under **My Benefits** on your dashboard. Otherwise, watch for a call. If you miss the interview, call 1-833-772-8347 to complete it or arrange another time. [Interview instructions](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/what-to-expect-after-applying-for-snap.html)
3. Send the documents your caseworker requests. For example, a pay stub can show income and a lease can show rent. Upload photos in the portal, take copies to a county office, or mail copies to the address in the [SNAP document checklist](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/applying-for-services.html.html).

If your household has little food or money, the department may process your application more quickly based on your need and resources. Its [guide to expedited processing](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/what-to-expect-after-applying-for-snap.html) explains when to expect an interview and decision.
