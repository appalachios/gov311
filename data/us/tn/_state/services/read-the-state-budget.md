For a department’s proposed funding, open **The Budget** on the Department of Finance and Administration’s [current budget page](https://www.tn.gov/finance/fa/fa-budget-information/current-budget.html). Search the document for the department name. The companion volume **Expenditures by Object and Funding by Source** breaks the figures down by program.

The [interactive budget](https://www.tn.gov/transparenttn/finances/interactive-budget0.html) lets you compare years. Its newest year shows the governor’s recommendation. Earlier years show budgets passed by the General Assembly. When you cite a figure, include the fiscal year and say which stage it represents.

To check what lawmakers approved for the newest year, look for the *appropriations bill* and its public chapter in the [General Assembly’s legislation search](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Legislation). The [legislative glossary](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/About/Glossary) explains how that bill puts the budget before lawmakers.
