1. Apply through the One DHS Customer Portal or use the paper Family Assistance Application (HS-0169). The same form lets you request SNAP if you also need food assistance. [One DHS Customer Portal](https://onedhs.tn.gov/) · [Printable applications](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/families-first-tanf/applying-for-services.html)
2. If you use paper, sign the application. You can upload it in One DHS, take it to a county office, or mail it to the Cookeville address in the program's instructions. Send copies of supporting documents and keep your originals. [Submission instructions](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/families-first-tanf/applying-for-services.html) · [County offices](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/humanservices/for-families/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/office-locator-family-assistance.html)
3. Complete the interview and send any records your caseworker requests. If you meet the eligibility criteria, the caseworker will help you prepare a Personal Responsibility Plan. It spells out the work or training activities that apply to your family. Sign and return the plan by the department's deadline so it can finish your application. [Interview, plan and decision](https://www.tn.gov/humanservices/for-families/families-first-tanf/applying-for-services.html)
