1. Start on the Department of General Services’ [supplier information page](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/generalservices/procurement/central-procurement-office--cpo-/supplier-information.html). Use **Finding Bid Opportunities** for directions to public solicitations. You can review opportunities before registering.
2. Open the event for the goods or services you provide. Check its documents for the closing time, submission instructions, and event administrator. If you are still registering, look in the event documents for instructions on submitting a paper bid. [Supplier FAQ](https://www.tn.gov/generalservices/procurement/central-procurement-office--cpo-/supplier-information/faq.html)
3. To receive an award, complete supplier registration through **Supplier Portal Home Page** on the [supplier page](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/generalservices/procurement/central-procurement-office--cpo-/supplier-information.html). Select the category codes that match what you sell to get notices about future opportunities. Save your registration ID so Supplier Maintenance can find your application if you need help.
