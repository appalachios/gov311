1. Open the [Registry’s campaign finance search](https://apps.tn.gov/tncamp/public/cpsearch.htm). Choose **Candidates** or **PACs** (political action committees) and enter the candidate’s last name or the committee’s name. Use the office, district, or election year fields to narrow your search.
2. Choose **Search Reports** for a filing, or **Search Contributions & Expenditures** to look for individual donations and payments. The Registry’s [records page](https://www.tn.gov/tref/tref-public-records-request.html) also links to campaign audits and explains how to request records that are unavailable online.

For county and municipal candidates, contact the local election commission. Their reports are outside the state database. For example, Hamilton County’s filing table shows which offices file locally and which file with the state.
