Look up the person or company you plan to hire in the state's public license search. Match the name on the record to the name on your estimate, then check the license status and expiration date. The state also displays public disciplinary actions. [Verify Public Search](https://search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov/)

If you need help identifying the right license for the work, call the Board for Licensing Contractors at (615) 741-8307. The board handles contractors, home improvement contractors, and limited licenses for electrical and plumbing work. Its help page also links to the complaint form for suspected unlicensed work. [Contractor board help and support](https://www.tn.gov/commerce/regboards/contractors/help-support.html)
