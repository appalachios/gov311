1. Read Revenue's instructions for your business location and gross receipts. They distinguish a local minimal activity license from a standard license and have separate instructions for out-of-state businesses and contractors. [Business tax registration and licensing](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/business-tax/registration-and-licensing.html.html)
2. If you need to register with Revenue, open the Tennessee Taxpayer Access Point (TNTAP). First-time businesses can select **Register a New Business**. If your business already has a Tennessee account, sign in to add an account or location. [TNTAP](https://tntap.tn.gov/eservices/) · [Revenue's registration instructions](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360059954591-TNTAP-Registration-7-Registering-a-New-Business-in-TNTAP)
3. If you need a local license, contact the county or city clerk identified by Revenue's instructions to arrange payment and obtain the license. [Registration and licensing](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/business-tax/registration-and-licensing.html.html)

If you are forming a Tennessee limited liability company, the Secretary of State handles that filing. [File articles of organization](form-tennessee-llc.md)
