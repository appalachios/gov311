For an interstate or state route, report the problem to the Tennessee Department of Transportation (TDOT). For a city street or county road, contact the local government that maintains it. Call **911** for a life-threatening emergency. [TDOT maintenance requests](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/maintenance-request.html) · [State and local road contacts](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/debris-removal.html)

1. Note the route and the nearest mile marker, cross street, or interchange. Give the location as precisely as you can.
2. Call **833-836-8349** or submit the maintenance request form. On the form, choose the type of repair. If the list does not describe the problem, choose **Other** and explain what crews will find. [Report a road problem](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/maintenance-request.html)

If a pothole damaged your vehicle, requesting a repair and filing a damage claim are separate steps. The Tennessee Department of Treasury reviews claims. TDOT's pothole page explains what evidence to gather and links to Treasury. [Pothole damage claims](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/engineering-operations-division/potholes.html)
