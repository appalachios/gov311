1. Open **Tennessee Historical Commission Viewer** on the commission’s [National Register page](https://www.tn.gov/historicalcommission/federal-programs/national-register.html). Search the address, choose **Identify**, and select a purple triangle for the listing. You can also search the state’s listing spreadsheet on its [listing process page](https://www.tn.gov/historicalcommission/federal-programs/national-register/listing-process.html).
2. If you do not see your building, check for a surrounding historic district. Buildings within a district can share a listing, so an individual building may have no map symbol. Ask National Register staff at National.Register@tn.gov if you need them to confirm whether your property is included. [How to read the map](https://www.tn.gov/historicalcommission/federal-programs/national-register.html)
3. To propose a new listing, start with the commission’s **Information Packet** and check for instructions specific to your kind of property. State staff review eligibility before a formal nomination proceeds. The National Park Service makes the final listing decision. [Steps for proposing a property](https://www.tn.gov/historicalcommission/federal-programs/national-register/listing-process.html)
