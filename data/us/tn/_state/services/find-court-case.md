If your case is on appeal, open the Administrative Office of the Courts’ [Appellate Case Search](https://pch.tncourts.gov/). Select the kind of search that matches what you have. You can enter a case number, the case name as it appears on the papers, a party’s name, or a business name.

For a case still in a trial court, use the court name and county on your paperwork. Look up that county in the [court clerk directory](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/clerks-of-court) and ask the clerk for the docket or record you need. One county can have several court clerks, so give the court name when you call. The University of Tennessee explains [which records clerks keep](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/eli/duties-clerks-court).
