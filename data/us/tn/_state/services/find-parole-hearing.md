Open **Parole Hearing Schedules** on the [Board of Parole website](https://www.tn.gov/bop.html). Look for the schedule for the state prison or county jail where the person is held. The board updates its schedules regularly. A hearing officer may recommend a decision, and board members review the recommendation. [How parole hearings work](https://www.tn.gov/bop/parole-hearings-division.html)

If you want to take part, read **Interested Party Participation in Parole Hearings** on the board’s website. It also lists a video participation signup for county jail hearings. Victims seeking notice of a hearing or wishing to provide a statement can use **Victim Notification Request** or **Victim Impact Statement** under **Victim Services**. [Board schedules and forms](https://www.tn.gov/bop.html)

If your family member is in a Tennessee Department of Correction prison, direct questions about their hearing, decision, or release to the **Institutional Probation Parole Officer** at that prison. That officer connects the facility with the board. [Board Operations](https://www.tn.gov/bop/about-us/divisions/board-operations.html)
