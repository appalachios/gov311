Open the Tennessee Department of Health’s location map and select your county, or use the text directory to find the county by name. All 95 counties have a health department. Some have several clinic locations. Before traveling, call the listed office and name the service you need, such as immunizations or WIC. Ask which location provides it, whether you need an appointment and what to bring. [County clinic map](https://www.tn.gov/health/local/map.html) · [County contacts and services](https://www.tn.gov/health/local.html)

**Davidson, Hamilton, Knox, Madison, Shelby and Sullivan** operate locally governed health departments. Choose the county link under **Metro Health Departments** for local clinics and scheduling. The state operates the other 89 county departments through seven regional offices. In the state directory, one asterisk beside a county listing indicates primary care and two indicate dental services. Call the clinic to confirm availability for the care you are seeking. [Metro departments, regional offices and clinic listings](https://www.tn.gov/health/local.html)
