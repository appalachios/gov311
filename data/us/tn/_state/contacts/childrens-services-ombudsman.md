If you are concerned about how the Department of Children’s Services or a provider handled a child’s case, open the [Ombudsman Program page](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/tccy/programs0/ombuds.html). Use the contact form under **How Can You Contact the Ombudsman?** Children, families, foster parents, and others concerned about a child can make a referral.

The ombudsman works for the Tennessee Commission on Children and Youth, an agency independent of the Department of Children’s Services. The ombudsman can review case records, speak with the people involved, and recommend changes. The office has no power to enforce a recommendation. [What the ombudsman can review](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/tccy/programs0/ombuds.html)

To report suspected child abuse or neglect, use the [Tennessee Child Abuse Hotline](child-abuse-hotline.md).
