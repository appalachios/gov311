Call the Tennessee Military and Family Readiness Operations office at (615) 840-4878 and tell staff what you need. For example, if a family member needs a military ID or help finding the right TRICARE contact, a readiness specialist can direct you to the service. [Military family programs](https://www.tn.gov/military/programs-benefits/military-family-programs.html)

To contact a specialist near you, use the locations on the [Soldier and Family Readiness page](https://www.tn.gov/military/programs-benefits/military-family-programs/soldier-and-family-readiness.html). For help filing a veterans benefits claim, [find a veterans service office](veterans-service-offices.md).
