If you know the agency’s name, find it in Tennessee’s [Department Directory](https://www.tn.gov/directory/department-directory.html). The directory includes boards, commissions, and programs as well as departments. If you know the office but need its number, open **State Phone Directory** in the [government directory](https://www.tn.gov/directory.html).

If the issue belongs to a city or county, use **County & City Web Sites** in the same directory.

If you know the profession but not the agency, [find its licensing board](../services/find-a-licensing-board.md). Health care professionals and occupations such as real estate and cosmetology have separate license lookups.

The next step depends on which branch handles your question.

- For a state service, start with the department that runs it. For example, Driver Services handles an [out-of-state driver license](../services/transfer-driver-license.md). For a [vehicle title and plates](../services/register-vehicle-after-moving.md), the Department of Revenue publishes the instructions and the county clerk takes the application.
- For a proposed change to state law, the [General Assembly](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/About/Index) is Tennessee’s legislature. Its two chambers are the House and Senate. You can [find your legislators](find-your-legislator.md) or [look up a bill](../services/track-a-bill.md).
- For an existing court case, the court handling it is the place to start. [Look up an appeal or find the trial court clerk](../services/find-court-case.md).

If you have already tried the state agency and still need help, the [Governor’s Office](governor-constituent-services.md) takes requests about unresolved agency issues.

## If you have a complaint or an appeal

The office depends on what happened. For example, the Attorney General’s [consumer complaint service](../services/file-consumer-complaint.md) handles disputes with businesses. A [discrimination complaint](../services/file-discrimination-complaint.md) goes to the same department’s Civil Rights Enforcement Division. If a health plan denied care, use the instructions for [TennCare member appeals](../services/appeal-a-tenncare-decision.md) or [insurance complaints](../services/file-insurance-complaint.md), depending on your coverage.
