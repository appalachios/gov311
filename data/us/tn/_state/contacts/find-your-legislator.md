Enter your house number, street name, and city in [Find My Legislator](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/Apps/fml/lookup). Look for your Tennessee Senate and House districts.

The General Assembly’s [guide to contacting a legislator](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/Information/Help/Contacting) links to the House and Senate member directories. Use the member’s listing for their office phone number, mailing address, or email. If you are writing about a bill, include its number and explain how it would affect you.
