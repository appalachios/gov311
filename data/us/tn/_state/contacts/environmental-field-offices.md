Call **1-888-891-8332** to reach a Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation (TDEC) field office. The line routes your call based on where you are calling from. If the issue is somewhere else, find that location on the field office map and use the office's direct number. Field staff can also help you find the permit office for a project. [Field office directory](https://www.tn.gov/environment/contacts/field-offices.html) · [Field office map and phone numbers](https://www.tn.gov/environment/contacts/field-offices/efo-map.html)

For a general question, you can use **Ask TDEC**. If you need to file a formal complaint or appeal, call the field office for filing instructions. The question form cannot accept legal filings. [Ask TDEC](https://www.tn.gov/content/tn/environment/contacts/ask-tdec-questions.html)
