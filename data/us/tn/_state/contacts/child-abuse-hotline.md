**Call 911 if a child is in a life-threatening emergency.** If you are unsure whether a report needs an emergency response, call the Tennessee Child Abuse Hotline at 1-877-237-0004. [Department emergency instructions](https://carat.app.tn.gov/carat/referral/emergency.html)

For suspected abuse or neglect that took place in Tennessee and can wait for review, call the hotline or select **Report Abuse or Neglect** in the state’s Child Abuse Referral and Tracking system. If you report online, save the **Referral ID** from the confirmation page. You can use it to check the status later. [Report or track a referral](https://carat.app.tn.gov/carat/) · [Reporting questions](https://carat.app.tn.gov/carat/help.html)
