## What it helps with

211 is a phone number for information about social services and other assistance. When you call, a person listens to what you need and refers you to local programs that can help.

United Way and its partners run 211. It is not a government office. The Federal Communications Commission set aside the number 211 for this purpose in 2000.

## How to ask for help

Dial 211 from your phone. You can also search for your local 211 at [211.org](https://www.211.org).

Calls to 211 are confidential, and you can stay anonymous.

## When to call a different number

- In an emergency, call 911.
- For a city service such as a pothole or a missed trash pickup, call your city's 311 line if it has one.
