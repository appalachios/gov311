## What it helps with

The benefit finder is the federal government's tool for finding benefits and financial help. You choose the categories that fit your situation, and it shows the benefits you may qualify for and how to apply for each one.

The older site, benefits.gov, now sends visitors to this tool.

## How to use it

1. Go to the [USA.gov benefit finder](https://www.usa.gov/benefit-finder).
2. Choose the categories that describe your situation.
3. Read the list of benefits and follow the application instructions for each one. A state or local office handles many of these applications, so the instructions may send you to your state's website.

## If you would rather talk to a person

Call USA.gov at 1-844-USA-GOV1 (1-844-872-4681). Agents answer Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, except federal holidays.

For local help with food, rent, or utilities, you can also [call 211](211.md).
