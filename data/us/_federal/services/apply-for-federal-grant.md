## Who needs this

Any organization that plans to apply for a federal grant through Grants.gov, the federal government's website for finding and applying for grants. A nonprofit applying for the first time must finish these steps before it can submit anything.

## Before you start

Start well before your first deadline. A SAM.gov registration takes time to become active, even when your organization already has a federal tax ID, and it can take much longer if SAM.gov cannot verify your information. Grants.gov publishes its current estimate on its [organization registration page](https://www.grants.gov/applicants/applicant-registration/organization-registration).

Every step is free.

## Steps

1. **Register your organization in SAM.gov.** SAM.gov is the System for Award Management, the federal government's registry of organizations it does business with. Start at the [SAM.gov entity registration page](https://sam.gov/entity-registration). You sign in with a Login.gov account. During registration, your organization names an Electronic Business Point of Contact (EBiz POC), the person who manages the organization's access.
2. **Receive your Unique Entity ID (UEI).** SAM.gov assigns this ID during registration. Federal agencies use it to identify your organization.
3. **Create a Grants.gov account.** The EBiz POC [registers at Grants.gov](https://www.grants.gov/register) with the same email address used in SAM.gov, then adds an organization profile with the UEI.
4. **Assign roles.** The EBiz POC gives team members the roles they need in Grants.gov, such as the Authorized Organization Representative (AOR), the person allowed to submit an application for the organization.
5. **Apply in Workspace.** Workspace is the Grants.gov tool where your team fills out and submits the application forms. See the [Workspace overview](https://www.grants.gov/applicants/workspace-overview) and [how to apply for grants](https://www.grants.gov/applicants/grant-applications/how-to-apply-for-grants).

## Common mistakes

- Starting registration the week of the deadline. An organization cannot apply until its SAM.gov registration is active.
- Letting the SAM.gov registration expire. It must be renewed every year.
- Using a different email address in Grants.gov than the one the EBiz POC used in SAM.gov.

## Where to get help

- Grants.gov support: 1-800-518-4726 or support@grants.gov. Grants.gov says support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except federal holidays.
- SAM.gov questions: the [Federal Service Desk](https://www.fsd.gov/).
